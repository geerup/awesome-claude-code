# Global Communications Manager, Social & Content
**Canonical (Ubuntu) · Remote, EMEA time zones**

---

## About the role

Canonical is the company behind Ubuntu, the operating system that runs most of the public cloud and a large share of the world's developer machines. Our audience is developers, sysadmins, and technical decision-makers. They have high standards and no patience for content that sells at them.

Our social and content function does not yet exist in structured form. Posting is ad hoc, untracked, and disconnected from pipeline. You will build it: the platform strategy, the content system, the measurement layer, the creator network, and the commercial link to Salesforce. You start as a one-person function with a mandate to grow it into a team.

This is a builder role. If you need an established playbook, a media budget, or a paid-follower line item, this is the wrong listing. Everything here is earned.

## What you will do

- Own platform strategy across LinkedIn, YouTube, X, Facebook, and Instagram, with a defined role, tone, and content type for each.
- Build the content production system: editorial calendar, format library, brief templates, QA standards, and a publishing cadence that scales past 2,500 posts a year without losing quality.
- Connect social to revenue. Work with marketing ops so every social-sourced MQL carries a Salesforce source, and report social's pipeline contribution to leadership every quarter.
- Direct the social side of global product launches with partners such as Dell, Intel, NVIDIA, and Framework, across a calendar spanning 5 portfolios and 20+ products.
- Build and run an always-on creator network across hardware, homelab, cloud, and open source communities, structured in tiers and reported like a channel.
- Lead social and earned media coverage for flagship moments: Ubuntu releases, anniversary campaigns, and Ubuntu Summit.
- Conceive and run organic-only demand programmes, including virtual events with registration targets in the thousands and email performance held above benchmark.
- Launch and grow owned audience assets: a LinkedIn newsletter, a YouTube programme with SEO-led publishing standards, and repeatable community formats.
- Establish a testing discipline (ICE or equivalent), competitive share-of-voice monitoring against HashiCorp, SUSE, VMware, and Red Hat, and a monthly reporting rhythm.
- Author the social and communications guidelines the company adopts as standard across regions.

## What success looks like

**Year one.** The function exists. Platform strategy is documented and live, Salesforce attribution works, and the first full year shows a step change: MQL growth near 9x on the prior year and 600K+ website sessions driven by organic social.

**Year two.** The system compounds. Total audience passes 2.4M, LinkedIn grows 90%+ year on year, and social MQLs move from double digits to high hundreds within a single year, all Salesforce-tracked.

**Year three.** The channel is an institution. Audience holds above 2.7M with a sustained 5%+ organic engagement rate, the newsletter passes 100K organic subscribers, flagship-event earned media multiplies by an order of magnitude, and the company earns its first analyst mention with marketing contribution.

## What we are looking for

- 10+ years in social and content marketing, including at least one function built from zero at a global company.
- Demonstrated pipeline attribution: you can show a Salesforce or CRM report proving what your content generated, and you insist on that standard.
- Fluency with deeply technical products and developer audiences. You can read a product brief about MicroCloud or NoSQL databases and turn it into content engineers respect.
- A working data practice: Sprout Social or equivalent, GA4, CRM reporting, and Meltwater or equivalent for share of voice.
- Writing quality high enough to set the bar for a team, and the editorial judgment to kill content that misses it.
- Comfort presenting results and strategy to VP and C-level audiences on a quarterly cadence.

## Nice to have

- An existing creator or influencer network in developer, hardware, or open source communities.
- Event marketing experience at global scale, physical and virtual.
- Prior work in open source, enterprise infrastructure, or another trust-driven technical category.

## Reporting and shape

Reports to the VP of Marketing. Remote-first, distributed team, quarterly in-person sprints. Success in this role is measured in Salesforce, Sprout, and Meltwater, in that order.
