# Portfolio update (A13, case by A20)

Target: asanhoury.com, case study 10. Arc format. Audience: CMOs. Pillar: governed agentic marketing systems.

## Case study: The Maharat Marketing Engine

**Challenge.** A marketing function that scales output without scaling headcount risk, in two languages, without ever letting an unreviewed asset reach the public. Most agentic marketing stacks fail on the last clause.

**Strategy.** Treat governance as the product. Design the system so no auto-send path exists in the codebase, then let volume come from agent specialization: multi-agent content and campaign pipelines with human-approval gates.

**Execution.** 21 agents across 4 swarm patterns, a 73-skill layer where every skill carries formal evals, and a review tier that surfaces defects for writers to fix rather than fixing them silently. Tooling was selected with Arabic capability as the decisive filter. A parallel build, the Skill Paths pipeline, ships under 6 versioned contracts with four-tier review.

**Results.** Built to approval-ready and dev-handoff-ready state. Scale numbers above prove scope; no live performance figures exist yet, and none are claimed. Campaigns run to the gate, where a human says yes or no.

**Takeaway.** The gate is the differentiator. Any team can generate content at volume now; the scarce skill is building the system that decides what deserves to ship.
