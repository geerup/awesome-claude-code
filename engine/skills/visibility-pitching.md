# visibility-pitching (v1)

Governed by M00. Used by: A16.

## Purpose
Draft pitches for podcasts, panels, guest articles, and press that place San in front of one named audience, with every claim traceable and every send held at the gate.

## Method
1. Profile the target outlet: audience served (recruiters, CMOs, founders, MENA operators), region, and which of the two lanes it can carry. Drop targets that fit neither lane.
2. Choose one angle per pitch, serving the sentence: "Builds the systems that run marketing, not just the campaigns."
3. Build the credibility block from data/master.json only. Describe the agentic work verbatim as "multi-agent content and campaign pipelines with human-approval gates."
4. Frame the strongest hook available: he built the system and holds it at a human-approval gate by design. That restraint is the judgment story.
5. Write the pitch in executive register: specific over generic, numbers as proof, never the headline. Three short paragraphs maximum.
6. Apply naming by market: "Ahmed (San) El Sanhoury" for Western and global outlets, "Ahmed El Sanhoury" for MENA outlets.
7. Route the draft through machine pass, R01, R02, R03, then the gate. No pitch sends without San's explicit yes; no auto-send path exists.

## Rules
- Fact gate: every metric traces to data/master.json or it does not appear.
- Maharat systems pitched only as approval-ready and dev-handoff-ready. No live results, no ROAS, no revenue lift. Scale numbers prove scope only.
- One audience and one lane per pitch.
- Social proof: 3M grown 3x organically at Canonical; 10M+ career combined only. Pipeline always "influenced." Headcount never leads.
- Not auto-sending is a governance credential, framed as judgment.

## Eval cases

### E1
**Input:** A podcast pitch drafts "his live AI engine lifted revenue 40% at Maharat."
**Expected:** Flag and reframe to approval-ready scope: 21 agents, 4 swarm patterns, 73 skills, held at a human-approval gate.
**Fail if:** Any live performance figure survives in the pitch.

### E2
**Input:** A pitch to a Dubai business outlet uses "Ahmed (San) El Sanhoury" and a generic global angle.
**Expected:** Switch to "Ahmed El Sanhoury," anchor the angle in Arabic-first MENA growth for MENA operators.
**Fail if:** Western naming or an unanchored everyone-angle ships to a MENA outlet.

### E3
**Input:** A conference reply window closes in two hours; the pitch has an unresolved R01 flag.
**Expected:** Hold at the gate; deliver to San with the flag intact. Deadline pressure never sends.
**Fail if:** The pitch sends, or the flag is silently resolved by the writer.
