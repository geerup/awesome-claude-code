# longform-writing (v1)

Governed by M00. Used by: A19.

## Purpose
Write articles and newsletter issues that argue one idea for one audience, sourced first from the seven case study files and holding every fact to master.json.

## Method
1. Source first from the seven case study files named in master.json.content_source_material: canonical-organic-from-zero, mindvalley-organic-machine, youtube-organic-growth, linkedin-organic-content-system, organic-content-as-pipeline, goodwall-community-led-growth, observance-content-strategy. They are import_required; until import, facts come only from master.json.
2. Fix one thesis serving "Builds the systems that run marketing, not just the campaigns." One pillar, one audience.
3. For portfolio-fed pieces, structure on the case study arc: Challenge, Strategy, Execution, Results, Takeaway. Where Results have no approved figure, write scope-and-outcome language.
4. Draft in executive register: terse, one formulation per idea, numbers as proof inside the argument and never the headline.
5. State Canonical scope precisely where it appears: led marketing communications, content marketing, and social media campaigns; did not own GTM.
6. Describe the agentic work verbatim as "multi-agent content and campaign pipelines with human-approval gates," with the gate framed as judgment.
7. Close with an honest fit or risk note for the reviewers, then submit to machine pass, R01, R02, R03, and the gate.

## Rules
- Fact gate: every metric traces to data/master.json or it does not appear. No estimates, no reconstructions, no rounding.
- Maharat systems: approval-ready and dev-handoff-ready, NOT live. No ROAS or revenue figures exist. Scale numbers prove scope only.
- Social proof: 3M grown 3x organically at Canonical; 10M+ career combined only. Pipeline "influenced." Headcount never leads.
- Banned tokens and constructions per brand.json; no em dashes anywhere.
- Import gap is stated, never papered over: missing case study detail becomes a named gap, never invented color.

## Eval cases

### E1
**Input:** An article's Results section states "the Maharat engine cut cost per lead 30% in production."
**Expected:** Flag; reframe Results to approval-ready and dev-handoff-ready scope with scale numbers, and the gate as the judgment credential.
**Fail if:** Any live performance figure survives in the piece.

### E2
**Input:** The case study files are not yet imported; a draft quotes vivid campaign details from memory of "the YouTube case."
**Expected:** Strip unverifiable detail; write only from master.json facts and name the import gap in the risk note.
**Fail if:** Reconstructed detail from unimported files remains in the draft.

### E3
**Input:** A portfolio-fed newsletter issue rambles through wins with no structure.
**Expected:** Rebuild on Challenge, Strategy, Execution, Results, Takeaway with one thesis for one audience.
**Fail if:** The arc is missing or the piece serves two audiences.
