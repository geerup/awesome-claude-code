# editorial-planning (v1)

Governed by M00. Used by: A17.

## Purpose
Turn the two pillars and four audiences into a weekly content calendar where every slot names one pillar, one audience, one format, and one traceable proof point before any writer starts.

## Method
1. Review last week's gate log: what shipped, what shipped empty, which flags recurred. Plan against those defects.
2. Allocate slots across both pillars over the week: governed agentic marketing systems, and Arabic-first MENA growth. Neither pillar goes silent for a full week.
3. Assign each slot exactly one audience: recruiters, CMOs, founders, or MENA operators. One pillar and one audience per piece, no exceptions.
4. Schedule at least one Arabic piece per week for A22. Arabic is a first-class stream, planned at calendar time rather than adapted as an afterthought.
5. Attach to each slot the master.json field its proof point traces to. A slot with no traceable proof gets scope-and-outcome framing or a different angle.
6. Sequence for A23: mark which slots, once San-approved, feed the repurposing chain of post, thread, script segment, newsletter section, and portfolio update.
7. Brief writers per slot: pillar, audience, format, proof source, and the positioning sentence the piece must serve.
8. Log the plan; A11 audits the week as a set.

## Rules
- Fact gate: every metric in every brief traces to data/master.json or it does not appear.
- Maharat systems planned only as approval-ready and dev-handoff-ready stories. No slot may promise live results.
- A slot with unresolved flags ships empty and is logged. No asset publishes on schedule pressure.
- Long-form slots source first from the seven case study files in master.json.content_source_material; until import, facts come only from master.json.
- Nothing publishes without San's explicit yes.

## Eval cases

### E1
**Input:** A draft calendar aims Monday's post at "founders and CMOs" to widen reach.
**Expected:** Split into two slots or pick one audience; every slot carries exactly one audience and one pillar.
**Fail if:** Any slot ships with two audiences or two pillars.

### E2
**Input:** A slot brief promises "Maharat engine results week: share the ROAS numbers."
**Expected:** Rewrite the brief to approval-ready scope with scale numbers; no live performance story exists to plan.
**Fail if:** The brief instructs a writer to source or imply live performance figures.

### E3
**Input:** The week fills all seven slots with English pieces; Arabic is deferred to next week.
**Expected:** Reassign at least one slot to A22 with Gulf-register adaptation planned from calendar time.
**Fail if:** A week ships with zero Arabic pieces.
