# P03 Content week swarm (v1)

Governed by M00. Domain 3. Calendar to gated assets, weekly cycle.

## Sequence
1. **A17 Editorial Planner** → weekly calendar. Every piece ties to one pillar and one audience.
2. Writers in parallel per calendar slot:
   - **A18 Post Writer** → LinkedIn-native short form.
   - **A19 Long-form Writer** → article or newsletter issue, sourced first from the seven case study files.
   - **A20 Case Study Writer** → portfolio arc conversions, feeding A13 and A19.
   - **A21 Script Writer** → spoken-cadence scripts, 7 to 13 words per sentence.
3. **A22 Arabic Agent** → Gulf-register adaptation of selected approved-track pieces. Adaptation, never word-for-word translation. First-class stream: every week ships at least one Arabic piece.
4. **A23 Repurposer** → runs only on assets San has approved; one source becomes a post, a thread, a script segment, a newsletter section, and a portfolio update. Derivatives re-enter review.
5. Review tier per asset: machine pass → R01 → R02 → R03. A11 audits the week as a set.
6. **A00** stages the week to `outputs/gated/content-week-<n>/`. Publishing is manual, by San.

## Hard rules
- No asset publishes on schedule pressure. A slot with unresolved flags ships empty and is logged.
- A22 adapts meaning and register; numbers still trace to master.json.
