## 2. Core angle

```
core_message:  Twenty years at the top of regional marketing, distilled into how people
               actually decide, and how you make them decide for you. Marketing is decision
               architecture, and this class hands you the way of thinking, not a list of tactics.
rationale:     Every one of Elda's three personas shares one root pain: effort that does not
               convert into a decision in their favour (a clean funnel that does not sell, work
               that ships into silence, tools that do not produce growth). The angle does not
               promise them growth, which would be off-limits and not credible. It promises the
               missing layer that sits above tactics: clearer judgement about how choice works,
               from someone who has operated at the most senior level in the region. That is a
               gain the offer can actually deliver (frameworks and clearer thinking), and it is
               the one thing free content rarely gives in a structured, senior voice.
serves:        All three segments, on the shared gain "clearer marketing judgement." Per-segment
               the named pain it relieves differs (see section 4 and the per-segment tuning).
through_line:  Decision architecture over tactics. The hero is the learner who gains clearer
               judgement; Elda is the master guide. The villain is the safe, forgettable
               strategy, never the learner.
```

Per-segment tuning (the core idea stays one; the entry pain differs):
- Data-driven marketers: "better questions beat more data." Tune toward the gap between a clean
  funnel and an actual decision.
- Self-taught builders: "make people care." Tune toward turning a shipped product into a story
  people choose.
- Skilled-but-stuck executors: "strategy over features." Tune toward the layer above tool
  mastery that produces movement.

Voice direction for stream 4 (not approved copy, illustrative of Elda's two-beat, contrarian,
aphoristic register per voice.md):
- myth-flip: "السوق لا يكافئ أفضل منتج، بل المنتج الذي يعرف كيف يتكلم."
- pain-question: "كل شيء جاهز: البيانات والقمع التسويقي والمؤشرات. فلماذا لا أحد يشتري؟"
- credential drop: "خبرة أكثر من 20 سنة مع أكبر العلامات في المنطقة، ملخصة في صف واحد."
- direct promise: "في هذا الصف ستتعلم كيف يتخذ الناس قراراتهم، وكيف تجعل قرارهم أنت."

Empowering check: the angle speaks to judgement the reader can build, not to a deficit to fix.
No shame framing. No revenue or growth promise. No accreditation claim. No held-back claim. No
