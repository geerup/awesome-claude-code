## 1. Announcement Plan (visibility angle)

### 1.1 Announcement objective

This is not a launch announcement. The masterclass launched in February 2026 and the launch plan
was executed (trailer, poster, Omnicom and Arman posts, LinkedIn employee advocacy). The PR
objective for this campaign is sustained post-launch visibility: earned amplification of the
class and its thought-leadership angle in regional marketing, business, and professional media
so that the masterclass remains visible to new audiences and reinforces Maharat's positioning
as the platform for senior, credible regional expertise.

The angle from the strategy-artifact: marketing is decision architecture. Twenty years of
operating at the most senior level of regional marketing, distilled into how people actually
decide and how you build the thinking to influence that. The hero is the learner who builds
clearer marketing judgement. Elda is the expert guide.

### 1.2 News hook

- news_hook: A published masterclass from the CEO of Omnicom Media Group MENA, available now
  on Maharat, brings 20 years of senior regional marketing practice into a structured framework
  for how people decide, and why most marketing fails to influence that decision.
- hook_strength: moderate. This is a post-launch visibility push, not a breaking launch. The
  genuine hook is the sustained relevance of the thought-leadership angle ("marketing is
  decision architecture") tied to a real, named, senior regional practitioner on a published
  platform. It is not a fresh event hook. Flagged as moderate so the team can consider whether
  a timing peg (a relevant industry event, a marketing conversation trending in the region)
  could sharpen it. Do not invent a peg; surface one only if it is real and already public.

Note on hook strength: a moderate hook still justifies outreach and earned coverage. The angle
is genuinely differentiated (a senior regional operator, a decision-first framework, an
Arabic-first platform) and the Omnicom and LinkedIn reach surface is a real PR lever unique to
Elda. The recommendation is to lead with the thought-leadership angle and the instructor's
voice rather than a product-announcement frame.

## 2. Press Release Brief

This is a brief for the copywriters, not final copy. Route Arabic to copywriter-ar and English
to copywriter-en. The release runs arabic-copy-qa then brand-qa (Arabic) and english-copy-qa
then brand-qa (English), then compliance-privacy-check, then brand-qa-reviewer. The hub does
not write final copy.

### 2.1 Structure

Arabic-first. The English version follows the same facts and the same boundary. It is not a
loose translation; it carries the same empowering framing and the same sayable-set discipline.

```
1. Headline (AR then EN): one sayable line, plain and empowering. The class name, the
   instructor's senior credential, and the thought-leadership angle in one sentence.
2. Dateline: city and date. Riyadh is appropriate as the primary market.
3. Lead paragraph (AR and EN): the single sayable news stated plainly. The class is
   available on Maharat. The instructor is CEO of Omnicom Media Group MENA with 20 plus
   years. The frame is "marketing is decision architecture." Empowering, not product-push.
4. Body (2 to 3 paragraphs): expand on the thought-leadership angle, the three audience
   pains the class addresses (data-driven marketers, self-taught builders, skilled-but-stuck
   executors), and the access point (Chapter 1 free). Each claim traces to the sayable set.
   No lesson list. No price. No accreditation claim.
5. Quote: attributed to the approved spokesperson (OPEN ITEM). Placeholder slot in the brief.
6. Boilerplate about Maharat: stable facts from 01-company-brief.md. Arabic-first online
   platform for self-development, launched 2023, Arabic-speaking adults in the Arab world,
   200,000 plus social followers, 1.5 million plus unique visitors, 100 plus countries. No
   roadmap (do not mention Skill Paths). No fundraising.
7. Media contact: name and contact channel. Personal data handled need-to-know only, per
   data-handling rules in section 4.
```

### 2.2 Key messages (for copywriters to build from)

All copy is thematic, not a lesson list. No revenue or growth promise. The hero is the learner.

- Primary: The class brings 20 years of senior regional marketing practice into a framework
  for how people decide, available in Arabic on Maharat.
- Credential anchor: Elda Choucair is CEO of Omnicom Media Group MENA with 20 plus years of
  experience across the region's major markets.
- Thought-leadership angle: "Marketing is decision architecture." The class teaches how people
  actually decide and how to build thinking that influences that decision, not a list of
  tactics.
- Audience resonance: built for the marketer whose clean funnel is not converting, the builder
  whose shipped product hears silence, and the skilled executor whose growth has stalled.
- Access: Chapter 1 is free. The class is on Maharat now.
- Platform context: Maharat is an Arabic-first platform for the Arab world. The class is
  available in Arabic and English.

### 2.3 Boilerplate guidance (for copywriters)

Draw from 01-company-brief.md only. Include: Arabic-first self-development platform, launched
2023, audience of Arabic-speaking adults in the Arab world, GCC core and Saudi Arabia primary,
200,000 plus social followers, 1.5 million plus unique site visitors, users in 100 plus
countries, masterclasses from regional experts, completion certificates (do not describe as
accredited). Do not include: Skill Paths (do not announce), fundraising or investment,
roadmap, any unconfirmed instructor names, any plan or price.

### 2.4 Voice direction for copywriters

Tone benchmark: Thmanyah. Clear, modern, intelligent, confident, never stiff. Elda's register
is sharp, contrarian, aphoristic. The villain is the safe, forgettable strategy, never the
learner. The reader is a smart, busy professional. Short sentences, active voice, concrete
nouns. No em dashes, no tatweel, Western numerals (0 to 9) only. RTL correct for Arabic.
No accreditation claim, no price, no promotion, no lesson list, no held-back claims.

## 3. Media List and Outreach Plan

### 3.1 Target profile (no invented outlets or contacts)

built from the target profile below. Actual outlet contacts and the confirmed contact channel
for each are sourced at build time from the PR team's verified, need-to-know contacts. Do not
fabricate a contact; flag any unconfirmed source as an open item.

marketing-professional and business-media angle.

Tier 1: Regional marketing and business media (Arabic and English)
- Profile: outlets covering regional marketing, advertising, media, and business, with an
  audience of marketing professionals, CMOs, founders, and agency leaders in the GCC.
- Angle fit: the thought-leadership angle ("marketing is decision architecture," a senior
  operator's framework) resonates with this beat. The Omnicom connection is a legitimate news
  hook for marketing trade press; Elda's CEO credential is genuinely newsworthy in this space.
- Outlet types: regional marketing trade publications, business and management media with a
  marketing section, media-industry outlets covering MENA advertising.
- Example outlet profiles (do not name without confirming real current contact): the regional
  editions of international marketing trade titles, Arabic-language business media with a
  marketing vertical, Entrepreneur Middle East (which has covered Maharat before, per catalog
  context), Campaign Middle East profile.
- Contact role to target: editor or beat reporter covering marketing, advertising, or media
  technology; editor at a business publication with a marketing section.
- Pitch angle: the class and its thought-leadership framing. Not a product ad. The story is
  "CEO of Omnicom Media Group MENA teaches marketing as decision architecture, on an
  Arabic-first platform." A feature or interview angle, not a product announcement.

Tier 2: LinkedIn and thought-leadership media (digital and newsletter)
- Profile: digital-first media, newsletters, and LinkedIn publications read by marketers and
  founders in the Arab world. This is the natural surface for the "marketing is decision
  architecture" angle given that LinkedIn was Elda's primary launch channel and the Omnicom
  advocacy program ran there.

  thought-leadership amplification. Pitch to editors or newsletter authors who cover marketing
  strategy, decision-making, or professional growth in Arabic.
- Outlet types: Arabic-language professional newsletters, marketing-focused digital publications,
  business podcasts with a marketing or leadership angle in MENA.
- Contact role to target: newsletter editors, podcast producers, digital editors covering
  marketing or professional development in the Arab world.
- Pitch angle: the thought-leadership frame. "How does the region's most senior marketing
  operator define the work?" Not "buy this class." An interview, feature, or contributed
  piece by Elda or Maharat.

Tier 3: Saudi and GCC mainstream business and technology media
- Profile: mainstream business and technology outlets in Saudi Arabia and the UAE with a
  professional and entrepreneurial readership. This tier broadens reach beyond the marketing
  trade.
- Angle fit: the story is the Arabic-first platform offering structured senior expertise to the
  next generation of Gulf professionals. The instructor's credential (CEO, Omnicom MENA) and
  the platform's regional scale (200,000 plus social followers, 1.5 million plus site visitors,
  100 plus countries) are the news anchors.

- Contact role to target: technology or business reporter, education and professional
  development beat.
- Pitch angle: Maharat as the platform story, with the Elda masterclass as the lead example
  of senior regional expertise made accessible in Arabic.
