## 1. Content Plan

### Angle restated (from strategy-artifact, not modified here)

Core message: Twenty years at the top of regional marketing, distilled into how people
actually decide, and how you make them decide for you. Marketing is decision architecture,
and this class hands you the way of thinking, not a list of tactics.

Through-line: Decision architecture over tactics. The hero is the learner who gains clearer
judgement. Elda is the master guide. The villain is the safe, forgettable strategy, never
the learner.

Per-segment tuning (the core idea stays one; the entry pain differs):
- persona-1 data-driven marketers: better questions beat more data
- persona-2 self-taught builders: make people care
- persona-3 skilled-but-stuck executors: strategy over features

### Themes and formats

The 14-day window uses 4 confirmed copy units (POST-1 to POST-4, AR and EN) distributed
across 3 channels and 4 formats. The content plan extends reach by mapping each caption to
the format and channel where it lands with the most force, then repurposing one visual
asset (AB5 carousel concept, C4 "One Line") into multiple posts.

| Theme | Format | Channel | Segment served | Angle tie |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Decision-architecture myth-flip | Feed post, single image or carousel cover card | Instagram, LinkedIn | All 3 personas | The first beat sets up the belief; the flip lands on decision architecture. POST-1 (AR), POST-1 (EN). |
| Pain-question, data layer | Feed post, single image | Instagram | persona-1 data-driven marketers | Better questions beat more data. The friction is the clean funnel that does not convert. POST-2 (AR), POST-2 (EN). |
| Provocation, make-people-care | Feed post, single image or carousel cover card | Instagram, LinkedIn | persona-2 self-taught builders | The market is not ignoring the product; it cannot feel it yet. POST-3 (AR), POST-3 (EN). |
| Credential drop, senior voice | Feed post, single image | Instagram, LinkedIn, X | All 3 personas | 20 years in one class; Elda as the guide, not the subject. POST-4 (AR), POST-4 (EN). |
| Two-beat flip carousel | Carousel (AB5, C4 "One Line") | Instagram, LinkedIn | All 3 personas, staggered | One aphorism per card, the flip across swipes. Repurposed from the myth-flip angle. |
| Story pulse (awareness and CTA) | Story (AB6, C4 "One Line" vertical) | Instagram | Broad reach, all personas | Short-form, single beat, routes to the link-in-bio or class page swipe-up. |

Promise discipline across all posts: frameworks and clearer thinking only. Never revenue or
growth outcomes. No price, no plan name, no promotion, no lesson list. Chapter 1 free is the
confirmed low-friction entry point; the cheatsheet is the confirmed lead magnet.

## 6. Community Engagement Guidance

### Tone (grounded in the angle)

The angle: marketing is decision architecture. Elda is the master guide. The learner is the
hero who gains clearer judgement. The villain is the safe, forgettable strategy, never the
reader.

Every reply carries Elda's two-beat register lightly: acknowledge the tension the commenter
names, then resolve toward a next step. Plain, confident, empowering. Never defensive, never
corporate, never hype. Thmanyah benchmark. Arabic-first; English replies to EN posts follow
the same spirit.

Voice notes for replies:
- Short lines. Concrete. One idea per reply. No lecture.
- The reply invites action; it does not describe the class or invent details.
- The hero is always the commenter. The reply does not sell at them; it opens a door.
- No tatweel, no Eastern Arabic numerals, no em dashes. Western numerals if a number is
  ever needed. RTL for Arabic.

### Reply patterns

| Message type | On-brand reply shape | Must not claim |
|---|---|---|
| "Where do I start?" or "How do I sign up?" | Point directly to the class page link in bio (Instagram) or the URL in the post (LinkedIn, X). Say the first chapter is free. One clear step. In Arabic: something close to "من هنا، الفصل الأول مجاني [رابط في الملف الشخصي]". Do not over-explain. | No invented price, plan name, or promotion. No lesson list. No timeline. |
| Compliment or excitement about the content | Warm and brief. Acknowledge the specific thing they responded to if possible (the flip, the question, the aphorism). Invite them to watch the first chapter. In Arabic: something close to "شكرا، الفصل الأول ينتظرك". | No accreditation claim. No roadmap or unannounced plan. No invented next class or Skill Path title. |
| Question about Elda's background or credentials | Answer only what is confirmed: CEO of Omnicom Media Group MENA, more than 20 years in regional marketing. Point to the class page for more. Do not add claims beyond what is in the brief. | No "100 plus brands", no dollar-spend figures, no Cannes reference. These are held-back and must not appear in any reply. |
| Question about what the class covers | Give the thematic angle only: how people actually decide, the thinking above tactics. Point to the first chapter (free) to experience it directly. Do not describe modules, duration, lesson count, or a topic not confirmed in the brief. | No invented lesson list, module names, or duration. No accreditation. |
| Pricing or plan question | The honest, empowering answer: the first chapter is free and no commitment is needed to start. For the full class details, point to the class page. Do not state a price; it is not confirmed. | No invented price, plan structure, or promotion. Do not invent a trial offer. |
| Complaint or criticism | Acknowledge plainly. Do not be defensive. Take the conversation to DM or the support email. Reply shape: "شكرا لملاحظتك، يسعدنا نكمل الحديث على الخاص" (Arabic) or the EN equivalent. Escalate internally; do not resolve in public. | No public promise to change anything. No invented resolution. |
| "I already watched it, what next?" | Empowering: the class continues. Point to the class page for the full access path. Do not describe next chapters by name or content. | No invented module or chapter title. No roadmap or future class announcement. |
| Question about a certificate or accreditation | The class issues a completion certificate. It is not accredited. Do not imply accreditation. Route to the class page for certificate details if relevant. Escalate to compliance-privacy-reviewer before any public reply on this topic. | Never imply accreditation. This is a hard guardrail. |

### What to amplify

Comments and replies that name the exact tension the angle is built on are worth amplifying
(liking, pinning if the platform allows, or responding warmly to draw them into the feed
for others to see):
- "I have all the data but I still can't figure out why no one converts" aligns with persona-1.
- "I launched and heard nothing, I thought my product was the problem" aligns with persona-2.
- "I know all the tools but I feel like I'm missing something strategic" aligns with persona-3.
- "Started Chapter 1 and it reframed how I think about the whole thing" is social proof and
  worth a warm, specific reply.

These surface the shared pain that the angle resolves and encourage others to click through.

### Escalation rules

| Trigger | Route to | Before any public reply |
|---|---|---|
| Any data-collection question (email, WhatsApp, what you do with data) | compliance-privacy-reviewer | Yes. Do not reply publicly until reviewed. |
| Any accreditation or certificate question | compliance-privacy-reviewer | Yes. |
| Any pricing question beyond "first chapter is free, see the page" | Human (Ahmed or owner) | Yes. Do not invent a price or promotion. |
| Any mention of instructor names other than Elda Choucair | compliance-privacy-reviewer | Yes. Do not name or confirm any other instructor. |
| Any fundraising, investment, or roadmap question | compliance-privacy-reviewer | Yes. The reply is simply: not something discussed publicly. |
| Sensitive or distressed message (mental health, personal hardship, angry) | A human, not an auto-reply | Yes. No templated response. |
| Legal question or threat | Ahmed directly | Yes. No public engagement until cleared. |

### Never say in any reply

- Any price, plan name, or promotion not confirmed in the brief.
- "Worked with 100 plus brands", any dollar-spend figure, or any reference to Cannes.
  These are held-back claims and must not appear in any reply, ever.
- Any implication that the completion certificate is accredited.
- The name of any future or unannounced class, instructor, or Skill Path.
- Any launch date, roadmap, or future product plan.
- Any personal or sensitive data echoed back into a public thread.
- A promise to fix a complaint in a specific way or on a specific timeline.

### Public reply is a gated action

The community guidance above is approval-ready direction. No reply goes public without
explicit sign-off per the human gate. Silence is not approval. For fast-moving comment
threads, the team queues replies for gate review and posts the approved ones only.
