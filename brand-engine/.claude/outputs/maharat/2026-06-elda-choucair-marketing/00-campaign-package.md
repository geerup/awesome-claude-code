## Strategy and angle

- product: Masterclass "Elda Choucair, Teaches Marketing" (EN) / "إلدا شقير، تعلّم التسويق" (AR),
  the hook into a B2C subscription. Title and URLs verified public (masterclass-pages.md, claim
  row 7). Elda is named because the published Maharat class page confirms her publicly.
- angle: 20 years at the top of regional marketing, distilled into how people decide and how you
  make them decide for you. Marketing is decision architecture, the way of thinking, not a list
  of tactics. The hero is the learner who gains clearer judgement; Elda is the master guide; the
  villain is the safe, forgettable strategy, never the learner.
- promise discipline: frameworks and clearer thinking only, never revenue or growth outcomes.
  "20 years in one class" and "marketing is decision architecture" are approved. Held-back claims
  (100 plus brands, spend figures, Cannes) appear nowhere.
- segments (Elda's own three, the spine): data-driven marketers (better questions beat more
  data), self-taught builders (make people care), skilled-but-stuck executors (strategy over
  features). Plus an owned-recency cut that sequences the lifecycle flow, and a B2B-adjacent
  professional surface (Omnicom, LinkedIn) that skews paid and PR.
- offer in copy: drive to Chapter 1 free and the PDF cheatsheet, then the gate and a Maharat
  subscription. Plans referenced generically. No price stated until confirmed.
- success_metric: structure only. Primary, campaign-attributable subscription conversions
  (lifecycle-first). Secondary, email CTR and reactivation, Chapter 1 plays, gate completions.
  The TARGET NUMBER and DATE are not supplied and are the first open item. Not invented.
