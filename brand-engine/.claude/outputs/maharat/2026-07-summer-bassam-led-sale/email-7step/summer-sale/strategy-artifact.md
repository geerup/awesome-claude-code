### angle

Core message: this summer, build a real skill with the people who set the standard. Start with
one of the region's most recognized makeup artists, then keep going across everything Maharat
teaches.

Serves: leads with the beauty-interested-engaged gain (the confidence and skill to create looks
themselves from a leading artist) and relieves its pain (no trusted, structured way to learn). It
widens, across the arc, into the breadth that serves the general-engaged segment, then the sale
gives every segment a time-boxed reason to act. See per-segment tuning below.

Rationale:
- Empowering, never deficit-framed. It leads with what the learner can make and become this
  summer, in their own hands, not with anything they lack. This matches the brand voice and the
  brief's offer_framing_notes. The sale tail keeps the frame: a time-boxed reason to start, not a
  "you are behind" push.
- It is true to the offer and earns attention first. Opening on the best-performing class from a
  publicly confirmed, recognized artist (Bassam Fattouh) is the strongest hook for a non-payer who
  has never paid. It earns the open and the play before the arc asks for anything.
- The arc is the structure. Hook (E1 to E2) proves the quality, breadth (E3 to E4) shows the
  reason to subscribe rather than buy one class, sale (E5 to E7) gives the time-boxed reason to
  act. One through-line, three movements.
- Seasonal timing, dated and real. Summer is a genuine high-intent self-development window in the
  GCC (longer free hours, downtime). This is an external seasonal context, dated, not a
  Maharat-asserted fact and not an invented occasion. It informs the framing and the timing only;
  it does not license any invented offer, title, or instructor.
- It does not overclaim. No lesson count, no duration, no accreditation, no promise the content
  lineup has not confirmed. The draw is the artist, the craft, and the breadth, all confirmed or
  cleared. The sale percentage, code, and dates stay placeholders until confirmed.

Per-segment tuning (the core idea stays one):
- beauty-interested-engaged: lead directly with creating looks themselves from a recognized
  artist (the sharpest hook framing), then the breadth and the sale as the reason to start now.
- beauty-interested-lapsed: lead with the draw of the best-performing class and the artist to earn
  the re-open first, then the breadth and the time-boxed sale.
- general-engaged-nonpayers: use the hook as proof of platform quality, then carry the weight on
  the breadth widening (E3 to E4) and the sale (E5 to E7) that fit their wider interest.
- dormant-nonpayers: light posture, the strongest touches only (the hook and the sale announce),
  no full cadence.

Illustrative customer-facing lines (direction only, not final copy):
- "هذا الصيف، ابدأ مهارتك التالية مع نخبة العرب. ابدأ مع أحد أشهر خبراء المكياج في المنطقة."
- EN direction: "This summer, build a real skill with the people who set the standard."
