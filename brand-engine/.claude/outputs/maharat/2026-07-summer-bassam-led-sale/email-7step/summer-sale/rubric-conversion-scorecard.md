- scored_by: independent senior email-conversion reviewer (not the author)
- scored_against: rubric-conversion.md (8 metrics, each /10), brand-voice.md
- date: 2026-06-21
- scoring stance: exacting. A merely fine subject is a 7. 9 to 10 is reserved for genuinely strong work.
- PASS rule: all 8 metrics >= 8 in BOTH languages. Otherwise FAIL with blocking metrics named.

Metric key: M1 subject stopping power, M2 subject set strength, M3 preheader pull, M4 headline hook,
M5 body persuasion, M6 voice and premium tone, M7 CTA strength and fit, M8 claims/mechanics/parity.

Global mechanics scan (all 14 cards): no em dash found in any customer-facing field, no tatweel
found, Western numerals only (20, 30, 2026-07-06), no "platform" or "منصة" leaked into any
customer-facing field (the words appear only in the author's internal Movement/Objective notes,
which do not ship). All clean on raw mechanics. Where M8 drops below 8 it is for parity or a
softer claims-discipline concern, not a mechanics break.
