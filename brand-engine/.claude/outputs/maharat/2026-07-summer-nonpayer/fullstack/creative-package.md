## Concept framing note

The single locked angle from strategy is: "This summer, build a real skill, taught by the
people who set the standard." The breadth of the roster is the hook. Every concept here
traces to that angle. The creative purpose is to make the reader feel the choice and the
possibility: many fields, one platform, one summer, and in each field a teacher who set the
standard in it.

This campaign is not a single-instructor campaign. The dominant motif is breadth across
seven fields, organized under one platform promise. Individual field concepts (per instructor
domain) are variants that run on paid acquisition interest-targeting, not the primary
platform-level hero.

All concepts in this package:
- Specify visual constants #141414, #1A1A1A, #009975 on every element.
- Are text-free in any generated or AI-assisted image or video layer. Copy lands in overlay
  slots only, authored downstream by copywriter-ar and copywriter-en.
- Require rights-cleared photography or footage for any instructor likeness. Generated
  likeness is a hard stop. Each likeness-dependent concept carries a named abstract fallback
  that needs no instructor photo.
- Leave all overlay slots empty and labeled by language (ar for copywriter-ar, en for
  copywriter-en).
- Contain no em dashes, no invented titles, no accreditation marks, no invented price or
  promo, no non-nameable instructor names, no verify-before-public-use facts.

## Concepts

### C1. "Many Fields, One Summer" (platform-level breadth hero)

Rationale. The strategy angle is breadth: many fields, one platform, one summer. A hero
concept that makes the variety of the roster visible as a single composed visual is the
highest-leverage asset in this campaign. It answers the owned non-payer's "where do I even
start" by showing that every starting point exists, and it anchors every other piece in the
campaign. Runs at the top of paid prospecting, organic, email, and the landing hero.

Visual intent. An editorial grid composition on a near-black #141414 field. Seven card
surfaces in #1A1A1A, each representing one field of the roster (music, cooking, acting,
makeup, business, styling, marketing), arranged in a clean asymmetric mosaic. Cards vary
slightly in size to create visual rhythm without clutter. Each card is empty in the
generated layer, a clean #1A1A1A surface with generous interior space for an overlay field
label and a brief descriptor. One card, the one that sits at the natural visual focal point,
carries a thin emerald #009975 left-border stripe as a brand accent to draw the eye. No text
baked in. The composition breathes: generous spacing between cards, #141414 visible between
every card edge. No instructor faces, no product imagery. Premium and calm.

Abstract fallback. This concept does not depend on instructor photography. It is its own
primary execution. No fallback needed.

Channel routing. Paid prospecting (Meta, Instagram, primary), organic social (hero feed
post, story), landing page hero (passed to web-design-director), email header (primary).

### C3. "The Standard" (instructor-led premium concept, photography-dependent)

Rationale. Where approved, rights-cleared instructor photography exists, the strongest
credibility signal for the per-field cuts is the instructor's presence as the visual anchor.
This concept pairs that presence with the platform-level promise. It is the highest-authority
format for each field's paid and organic creative when assets are confirmed. It is blocked
until per-instructor photography is cleared; the fallback is C2.

Visual intent. A real, rights-cleared instructor photograph or class still, sourced from the
confirmed Maharat asset library, centered or two-thirds dominant on a #141414 field. The
image is treated as an editorial portrait: quiet confidence, professional authority, generous
space, uncluttered. A thin emerald #009975 bar or hairline at the bottom edge of the
composition anchors the brand without flooding it. No heavy color grading that alters skin
tone or clothing. The field above the instructor carries generous space for an overlay
headline. No text baked in. All copy lands in overlay slots.

This concept is produced per instructor. Each is its own creative execution with its own
asset brief. The visual layer changes per instructor; the overlay slot structure is identical
across all seven.

Channel routing. Paid prospecting and retargeting per interest cut (Meta, Instagram), organic
social feed post per field. Blocked per instructor until approved photography is confirmed.
Fallback for all seven: C2 per-field abstract variant.

### C4. "One Free Lesson" (abstract free-entry re-engagement concept)

Rationale. The strategy's owned non-payer segment (both never-engaged and lapsed-engaged)
needs a warm re-entry with a low-friction ask. The free intro chapter is the strategy's
named pivot: try a free first lesson in the field you choose, then subscribe to keep going.
This concept makes the free entry the visual and conceptual hero, keeping the message simple
and empowering: you are already here, the first step is free, the rest of the summer is
yours. It requires no instructor photography and is the primary visual for the owned
lifecycle flow.

Visual intent. Text-free abstract still or short motion (7 to 10 seconds). A single emerald
#009975 path or mark on a #141414 field, suggesting a first step or a beginning: a thin
clean arc that starts from off-frame left and settles into the center of the composition,
resting with a gentle glow on a #1A1A1A card plane. The metaphor is "a first step that
opens the path." Not a brushstroke; more like a path or a trace, precise and confident. The
#1A1A1A card plane occupies the center third of the frame vertically, giving a grounding
surface for overlay copy. Above and below the card plane, the #141414 field is clear for
overlay text. No face, no product, no field imagery. Premium and calm.

Motion note (for Reels, TikTok, and app push: the arc draws on in 1.0 second, settles with
a faint pulse at frame 60, holds for 2 seconds, fades to #141414 clean. Total 7 to 10
seconds. Overlay copy is not baked in; it is added as a text layer in final composite by the
designer after copy is confirmed.

Channel routing. Owned lifecycle email (header and inline, all messages), app push companion
still, organic social re-engagement posts, paid retargeting (secondary, when C5 retargeting
is blocked by missing assets).

### C5. "This Summer, Choose Your Field" (breadth-field-grid retargeting concept)

Rationale. The retargeting segment has already seen top-of-funnel creative and has not
converted. The strategy names their hesitation as uncertainty about value and which field to
choose. The most direct answer is to surface the breadth again, this time as a field-choice
invitation: all seven fields, one grid, one platform. This reinforces the platform-level
promise and reduces the "which one is right for me" friction. It works with or without
instructor photography and directly addresses the "is this for me" question by showing
every option.

Visual intent. A clean grid of seven small #1A1A1A field tiles on a #141414 field. Each
tile carries a field-evocative abstract icon in #009975 (same system as C2: music, cooking,
acting, makeup, business, styling, marketing, each as a minimal single-line icon shape, no
text baked in). The grid is balanced and uncluttered, with the seven tiles arranged in a
2-3-2 or 3-4 pattern with generous spacing. One tile glows slightly brighter in emerald as
a visual focal point, suggesting the reader's choice. No text in the generated layer. All
field labels and the platform headline land in overlay slots. A thin full-width emerald
#009975 accent bar anchors the bottom edge. No instructor faces.

Channel routing. Paid retargeting (Meta, Instagram, carousel and single image), organic
social retargeting posts. Standalone concept, no photography dependency.

### C6. "Watch the First Lesson Free" (video hero concept, footage-dependent)

Rationale. YouTube and TikTok are in the strategy's channel plan. A short video asset that
surfaces the free intro chapter across multiple fields is the highest-engagement format for
breadth campaigns. It shows, rather than asserts, that the standard is high across every
field. This is the campaign's master video asset. It requires real, rights-cleared class
footage; it is blocked until footage is confirmed per instructor. The fallback is C4 motion.

Visual intent. An edited, footage-led breadth reel. Structure: a fast-cut sequence of five
to seven class-footage moments, each from a different field. Each moment is 2 to 3 seconds,
showing a real teaching instant: a hand movement, a teaching gesture, a direct-to-camera
moment, a craft action. No talking-head monologues, only teaching instants. Between each
moment, a 0.5-second #141414 hard cut. The sequence opens and closes on a #141414 title
card. All on-screen words are overlay slots; no text baked into the footage or the mattes.
Footage is real, rights-cleared Maharat class material only, never generated. If footage for
fewer than three fields is available, the video concept is blocked and the campaign runs on
C4 until more footage is confirmed; flag at human gate.

Total target length. 20 to 30 seconds master. A 15-second cut for paid pre-roll and a
6-second bumper for YouTube TrueView are repurposed from the master by the designer using
the Blotato workflow when the BLOTATO_API_KEY credential is confirmed, or by manual cut
until then.

Channel routing. Organic YouTube (master cut), organic Reels and TikTok (vertical
reformat), paid YouTube pre-roll (15-second cut), paid TikTok (vertical cut). Blocked until
multi-field footage is confirmed. Fallback: C4 motion per single field.

confident. No text baked in.

Channel routing. Email header (campaign-identity version), organic social campaign-launch
post, app push companion still, paid traffic creative (secondary, lowest lift). No
photography or footage dependency.
