## 1. Content plan

### 1.1 Strategic angle (inherited from strategy-artifact sections 4.1 and 4.2)

"This summer, build a real skill, taught by the people who set the standard." The campaign
sells capability and choice, not correction. The learner is not behind. They have a season and
a platform where, in whatever field they pick, the teacher is someone who set the standard in
that field. Credibility comes from recognizable regional experts, named only with clearance and
only on cleared, page-sourced facts. Proof comes from the breadth itself: seven fields, seven
real published masterclasses, each with a free first chapter.

Organic social owns the top of funnel for the new-acquisition-by-interest segment (strategy
section 3.3 and 5). It heroes the breadth, the free intro chapter across fields, and the
per-field credibility. It builds retargeting pools and routes the most ready follower directly
to the signup gate.
