## 1. Channel mix

### 1.1 Meta and Instagram (primary: prospecting and retargeting)

Role: primary cold acquisition engine and retargeting closer across the full two-month flight.

Rationale: the strategy-artifact defines the new-acquisition segment as Arabic-speaking adults
roughly 18 to 35 in the GCC, Saudi Arabia primary. Meta and Instagram carry the highest
concentration of this audience in the region. The campaign's creative spine is roster breadth
across seven distinct fields: music, cooking, acting, makeup, business, styling, and marketing.
Meta's interest-based and behavioral targeting can hold each field as a parallel ad set,
letting the plan test which field pulls hardest without changing the platform-level promise.
The free intro chapter 1 hook works natively in short video, carousel, and Stories formats that
Meta supports, giving the plan a genuine low-friction entry offer for cold prospecting. The
retargeting layer (class-page visitors, intro players, gate non-completers) is also strongest
on Meta given the pixel's coverage of the web conversion path. Meta is the conversion engine.

### 1.2 TikTok (upper funnel: reach and video seeding across selected fields)

Role: upper-funnel awareness and retargeting pool seeding, especially for the younger part of
the 18 to 35 band and for the visually demonstrable fields (makeup, cooking, music, styling).

Rationale: the strategy-artifact identifies the breadth-led angle as the creative spine. Four
of the seven fields, makeup, cooking, music, and styling, have a strong short-form visual
format: a clip of the instructor at work shows the transformation before any copy is needed.
TikTok's in-feed video is built for that format and reaches the 18 to 28 slice of the target
audience at scale in Saudi Arabia. TikTok is not the conversion engine; Meta holds that role.
TikTok's job is awareness reach and building video-play retargeting audiences that feed back
into Meta and YouTube. Budget allocation here is deliberately moderate, weighted toward the
first half of the flight for seeding. The fields with a weaker short-form visual (business,
marketing, acting) are lighter on TikTok and stronger on Meta and Google.

### 1.3 Google Search (precision: high-intent query capture)

Role: capture active-search intent around learning, the cleared instructor names, and
self-development in the GCC. Covers all seven fields at meaningful volume via Arabic and
English keyword groups.

Rationale: the strategy-artifact notes that the target audience is a "smart, busy adult who
wants to grow." A subset of that audience will express that intent in search rather than in
social. Arabic queries around learning music, cooking, acting, makeup artistry, business
skills, styling, and marketing from recognized experts represent a decision-frame moment.
Intercepting them with a direct, field-specific message is a precision complement to Meta's
reach engine. Search volume for Arabic-language skill courses in the GCC is real but narrower
than the social audience, so this channel receives a modest share. Its role is efficiency:
lower volume, higher intent, lower cost per click in the subscription consideration frame.
Brand keywords on the cleared instructor names are a priority subgroup.

### 1.4 YouTube (mid funnel: video reach and reinforcement)

Role: mid-funnel video to warm and semi-warm audiences after Meta and TikTok have seeded
awareness. Reinforces the class as a premium, credible product with a longer trailer or
instructor clip format.

Rationale: YouTube reaches a large, engaged video audience in the GCC that is receptive to
longer-form content, including class trailers and instructor feature cuts. The strategy-artifact
anchors credibility to the cleared, page-sourced instructor facts: Ragheb Alama's 40 years in
music, Salam Dakkak's Michelin award winning restaurant, Bassam Fattouh as a most sought-after
regional makeup artist, and so on. These facts carry more weight with a 15 to 30 second
YouTube pre-roll than a 6 second TikTok cut. YouTube is most effective in the second half of
the flight, when awareness pools are large enough to run high-affinity interest and remarketing
targeting. This channel is conditional on rights-cleared instructor footage or class trailer
assets being confirmed (OPEN ITEM, brief section 10).

### 1.5 Reserve (within-budget reallocation pool)

Role: mid-flight performance reallocation to the best-performing channel, without requiring a
new budget approval from Ahmed.

Rationale: a standing proportional reserve allows the plan to respond to early data, such as
one field pulling dramatically harder on Meta, or TikTok underperforming on its CPV targets,
without a full replan. The reserve is not pre-assigned to any channel. It is released to the
channel delivering the lowest cost per signup-gate completion or subscription event after the
first performance readout, which per the proposed schedule falls at the end of the first two
weeks. This reallocation is a proposal to the human gate, never an autonomous spend action.

### 2.2 Notes on the shape

Meta at 45 percent reflects its role as the primary conversion engine, its audience density in
the GCC, its retargeting capability once the pixel and CAPI are wired, and the fact that seven
field-based interest sets run in parallel as distinct ad sets, making it the platform where the
per-field test lives.

TikTok at 18 percent is awareness and seeding, not conversion. Its share is front-loaded into
phase 1 and drops materially in phase 2. If TikTok's retargeting pool after phase 1 is smaller
than 1,000 users per field, the TikTok share is folded into the reserve and then to Meta.

Google Search at 17 percent is a steady, precision layer. Its share does not change materially
across phases. Brand keyword groups for the seven cleared instructor names are a priority
subgroup within this allocation.

YouTube at 12 percent is conditional on a rights-cleared trailer or instructor footage asset
being confirmed before launch. If no cleared asset exists at launch, the 12 percent rolls into
the reserve, bringing the reserve temporarily to 20 percent of the total, until the asset is
cleared. Generated instructor likeness is never an alternative.

The 8 percent reserve is held flat through phase 1. After the first performance readout, the
reserve is released to the channel or field set with the lowest cost per signup-gate completion,
subject to human gate approval of the proposed reallocation.

## 4. Bid strategy

No target CPA or ROAS has been confirmed in the brief (OPEN ITEM, ASSUMPTION). The bid
strategy below is staged to operate without a hard cost cap in phase 1 and to transition to
a cost-cap or target-CPA structure in phase 2 once enough conversion events accumulate. The
hard CPA target is set by paid-build-engineer in the campaign structure only after Ahmed
confirms the number. No placeholder number is inserted here.

### 4.1 Phase 1 (first 3 weeks: awareness, seeding, and learning)

Objective: build awareness, seed retargeting pools, and collect enough gate-completion and
subscription events to exit the algorithm learning phases. Prioritize learning-phase
completion over CPA efficiency, which is unreliable until event signal accumulates.

- Meta and Instagram: optimize toward gate-completion events (email or WhatsApp capture as
  the measurable conversion step). Do not set a cost cap yet. Allow the algorithm to spend
  freely within the phase 1 daily budget to collect conversion signals and exit the learning
  phase. Meta's learning phase requires roughly 50 optimization events per ad set per week;
  set individual ad set budgets so the seven field interest sets each have a realistic chance
  of hitting that threshold. Consider consolidating the lowest-volume fields into a combined
  "other fields" ad set in phase 1 if budget per field is thin at the confirmed budget level.

- TikTok: maximize video views objective for the 4 visual field sets in phase 1. Switch to
  a reach objective if CPV rises above the directional efficiency threshold. No conversion
  bid until the TikTok pixel has accumulated at least 30 gate or subscription events.

- Google Search: maximize clicks on the brand and field-learning keyword groups. Monitor daily.
  Transition to Target CPA once the campaign has accumulated at least 30 conversions in a
  30-day window.

- YouTube: CPV (cost per view) bidding. Awareness objective. No conversion bid on YouTube
  in phase 1. This channel reinforces awareness, not conversion at this stage.

### 4.2 Phase 2 (weeks 4 to 6: conversion push)

Objective: drive subscription conversions. Retargeting and best-performing prospecting field
sets push toward subscription. Introduce cost caps once event volumes support it.

- Meta and Instagram: introduce a cost cap per gate completion once the gate has fired at
  least 50 events per ad set. Apply the Ahmed-confirmed CPA target as the cap value. If the
  CPA target is not yet confirmed by the start of phase 2, maintain maximize-conversions
  bidding and flag the missing target as an open item in the performance readout. Pause the
  lowest-performing field sets by cost per gate completion and reallocate within the Meta
  budget to the top 3 performing fields.

- TikTok: shift to maximize conversions against the gate or subscription event if pixel volume
  supports it. If volume is insufficient, maintain video-view objective and retargeting reach
  only. If TikTok's retargeting pool is smaller than 1,000 users after phase 1, fold the
  remaining TikTok budget into the reserve.

- Google Search: move to Target CPA if 30 or more conversions are available; otherwise remain
  on maximize clicks. Tighten to the converting keyword groups and remove underperforming terms.

- YouTube: maintain CPV bidding; shift budget weight from YouTube to Meta if the cleared
  trailer asset is not available or if YouTube CPV is high relative to Meta efficiency.

### 4.3 Phase 3 (final 2 weeks: close and retarget)

Objective: maximize subscription conversions from warm and retargeting audiences. Minimize
waste on cold prospecting.

- Meta and Instagram: retargeting primary. Cold prospecting continues only on the 2 to 3
  best-performing field sets. Frequency watch: if frequency exceeds 5 impressions per user per
  week on any retargeting audience, reduce budget on that audience and refresh the creative
  rather than burning the pool.

- TikTok: retargeting only. Pause cold TikTok prospecting unless phase 2 data shows a strong
  TikTok-to-gate conversion rate.

- Google Search: maintain. Add any new negative keywords surfaced in phases 1 and 2. No bid
  increases without a confirmed CPA target.

- YouTube: hold at phase 2 level or wind down. No scaling in phase 3.

### 4.4 Reserve release

After the first performance readout (proposed end of the second week of the flight), release
the 8 percent reserve to the channel and field set with the lowest cost per signup-gate
completion. This reallocation is a proposal to the human gate, not an autonomous spend action.
The analytics-reporter performance readout triggers this proposal.

## 5. Flighting and pacing

The proposed flight window is 2026-07-01 to 2026-08-31 (ASSUMPTION from the brief and
strategy-artifact; confirm with Ahmed). The window is 62 days across three phases. All dates
below are directional; if the confirmed window shifts, the phase proportions apply unchanged.

### 5.1 Phase 1: approximately days 1 to 21 (first 3 weeks)

Objective: build awareness, seed retargeting pools across all 7 fields, collect conversion
events to exit learning phases, validate which field sets pull hardest.

Launch sequence:
- Meta and Instagram: launch all 7 field interest prospecting ad sets. Prospecting-heavy.
  Free chapter 1 hook is the lead creative for each field. CTA leads to the relevant class
  page, not directly to the plans page, so the class page does the sell before the
  subscription ask.
- TikTok: launch the 4 visual-field video creatives (makeup, cooking, music, styling) with
  video-view objective.
- Google Search: launch brand keyword group and all 7 field-learning intent groups. Monitor
  daily for irrelevant traffic.
- YouTube: launch trailer or instructor intro clip if a rights-cleared asset is confirmed.
  If no cleared asset at launch, hold YouTube budget in reserve.
- Retargeting: activate Meta retargeting ad sets from approximately day 3 onward, as
  class-page visitor pools begin to build. Do not force retargeting before audience pools
  have reached minimum viable size.

Pacing: spend evenly across the phase. Do not front-load day 1. Learning-phase algorithms
respond poorly to high early spend before event signal accumulates.

### 5.2 Phase 2: approximately days 22 to 42 (middle 3 weeks)

Objective: shift spend toward best-performing field sets and retargeting; introduce
conversion bids where event volume supports it; activate lookalike audiences.

- Meta and Instagram: pause the lowest-performing field sets by cost per gate completion
  after the phase 1 readout. Scale the top 3 field performers. Activate lookalike of
  subscribers and lookalike of gate completers if seed sizes are sufficient and PDPL
  compliance is confirmed.
- TikTok: evaluate CPV from phase 1. If sufficient retargeting pool, shift to retargeting.
  If not, fold remaining TikTok budget to reserve.
- Google Search: transition to Target CPA if conversion volume supports it.
- YouTube: increase weight if cleared asset is available and CPV is efficient.
- Reserve release: release the 8 percent reserve to the best-performing channel and field
  set, subject to human gate approval.

### 5.3 Phase 3: approximately days 43 to 62 (final 2 weeks)

Objective: maximize subscription conversions from warm audiences before the flight closes.
High-intent close window coinciding with the last weeks of summer.

- Meta and Instagram: conversion-focused retargeting. Class-page and plans-page visitors
  receive the subscription value message. Gate completers who have not subscribed receive the
  reinforcement message. 25 percent video viewers who have not converted receive a mid-funnel
  message. Cold prospecting on 2 to 3 best-performing fields only.
- TikTok: retargeting only if pool is active. Pause cold prospecting.
- Google Search: maintain. Add negatives. No new bid changes.
- YouTube: hold or wind down.

Rationale for seasonal pacing: the brief and strategy-artifact identify summer in the GCC as
a high-intent, time-rich self-development window. The two-month flight captures both the early
summer peak (when intent is highest) and the late-summer window (when the "use this season to
build something" urgency is closing). The phase 3 close window maps to approximately late
August, which is a natural decision point before routine resumes.

## 6. In-flight optimization triggers (all proposals through the human gate)

All optimization moves below are triggered by analytics-reporter performance readouts (stream
8). None are autonomous spend actions. Each is a proposal routed to the human gate.

Trigger 1, Creative fatigue: CTR on the top creative drops by 30 percent or more
week-over-week, or frequency exceeds 5 impressions per user per week on Meta retargeting.
Proposed action: rotate in the next-ranked creative for that field from the copy-package.
If all field creative variants are in rotation and CTR is still falling, flag to
creative-director for a new asset before extending the flight.

Trigger 2, Meta learning phase stuck: an ad set has not exited the learning phase by day 7
(fewer than 50 optimization events in 7 days). Proposed action: consolidate the lowest-volume
field sets to concentrate event volume, or broaden the optimization event temporarily to a
higher-funnel action (class-page visit or video view) until gate events accumulate. Both
options require human gate approval.

Trigger 3, TikTok underperformance: TikTok CPV is more than 2x the Meta CPM-adjusted
equivalent, or the TikTok retargeting pool per field after phase 1 is fewer than 1,000 users.
Proposed action: pause TikTok prospecting and reallocate the TikTok budget share to Meta.
Keep TikTok retargeting running only if the pool is large enough to be meaningful. Proposal
to the human gate.

Trigger 4, Reserve release: end of the phase 1 performance readout from analytics-reporter.
Proposed action: release the 8 percent reserve to the channel with the lowest cost per gate
completion. One-sentence budget proposal with the destination and justification, routed to the
human gate.

Trigger 5, CPA exceeded (activates only after the target is confirmed with Ahmed): actual
cost per subscription is more than 20 percent above the confirmed target CPA for two
consecutive days. Proposed action: reduce spend on the offending channel by 20 to 30 percent
and redirect to the best-performing channel, or tighten the audience by removing the broadest
interest cluster. Specific action depends on the readout at that point. Requires human gate
approval.

Trigger 6, CPA undercut (efficiency opportunity, activates only after the target is confirmed):
actual cost per subscription is more than 20 percent below the confirmed target CPA. Proposed
action: increase spend on the overperforming channel within the confirmed budget ceiling. Do
not exceed the confirmed total budget without a new approval from Ahmed.

Trigger 7, YouTube asset not confirmed by launch: rights-cleared trailer or instructor footage
is not available before the flight start. Proposed action: hold YouTube budget in reserve. Do
not run YouTube with uncleared or generated creative. Surface to the human gate for a decision
on whether to wait for the asset or fold the YouTube share permanently into Meta.
