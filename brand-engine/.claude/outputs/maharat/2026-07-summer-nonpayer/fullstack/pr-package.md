## 2. Announcement Plan

### What is being announced

A seasonal campaign: "Summer of Skills" (صيف المهارات). The announcement celebrates the breadth
of Maharat's live masterclass roster and the regional experts who teach on the platform, framed
as a summer invitation to build a real skill in a field of the learner's choosing.

This is not a product launch announcement. It is an earned-media campaign around an existing,
live product. The newsworthiness is the breadth of the roster (seven confirmed fields, seven
recognizable regional experts, each with a published and live Maharat class), the summer timing
as a high-intent self-development window in the GCC, and the accessible entry point (a free first
chapter on every featured class).

## 3. Press Release Brief

### Brief to copywriter-ar and copywriter-en

**Campaign:** 2026-07-summer-nonpayer

**Language:** Arabic primary (copywriter-ar). English variant (copywriter-en). Arabic is not a
translation of English. It is the primary text and must read naturally in Modern Standard Arabic
with Gulf-familiar wording, Thmanyah benchmark.

**Format:** Press release. Headline, subhead, dateline, two to three body paragraphs, boilerplate,
contact line. No em dashes anywhere in either language. Western numerals only (0 to 9). No tatweel
or kashida in Arabic. RTL-correct for the Arabic version.

**Tone:** Plain, confident, empowering. The learner is gaining capability in a field they choose,
not correcting a gap. Short sentences. Active voice. No hype words, no over-explanation.

**The news angle:**
Maharat launches its "Summer of Skills" (صيف المهارات) campaign, inviting Arabic-speaking
learners across the GCC to build a real skill this summer from a roster of seven live masterclasses
taught by recognizable regional experts across music, cooking, acting, makeup, business, styling,
and marketing. Every featured class opens with a free first chapter. This is the breadth story:
many fields, many experts, one platform, one summer.

**Headline direction (Arabic, primary):**
State the campaign and the roster fact. One sentence, no em dash. Lead with the season and the
invitation, then the breadth.

Example direction (not final copy, for copywriter orientation only):
"مهارات تطلق صيف المهارات: سبع مهارات مع نخبة من يصنعون المعيار والدرس الأول مجاني"

**Headline direction (English):**
Same fact. Campaign name, breadth, free first chapter. One sentence.

Example direction (not final copy): "Maharat Launches Summer of Skills with Seven Masterclasses
from Regional Experts, First Chapter Free on Every Class"

**Subhead direction:**
A single plain sentence that names the fields or the promise. Arabic primary, English follows
the same shape.

Example direction (Arabic, not final copy): "موسيقى، طهي، تمثيل، مكياج، أعمال، تنسيق الأزياء،
وتسويق، كلها على منصة واحدة"

**Body paragraph 1 (news peg):**
State the campaign launch. Name the platform (Maharat). State the number of featured fields (seven).
Name the fields: music, cooking, acting, makeup, business, styling, and marketing. State that
every featured masterclass opens with a free first chapter. Do not name instructors in this
paragraph; the breadth is the lead.
