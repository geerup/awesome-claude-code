## How the run executed (the four shapes)

1. Pipeline: brief intake and strategy (strategy-lead) seeded every downstream stream.
2. Parallel fan-out: 14 reasoning specialists generated in parallel off the strategy-artifact,
   then the dependent builders (designer, web-designer, paid-build, conversion) ran.
3. Verify-then-advance: the gate stack (skill eval, arabic-copy-qa / english-copy-qa,
   brand-voice-qa, design-qa, web-design-qa, accessibility, compliance, brand-qa) wrapped every
   customer-facing asset. The first pass produced three FAILs; every fix returned to the owning
   author and every gate re-cleared. Full record in qa-copy-design-verdicts.md.
4. Human gate: the swarm assembled the approval-ready package and stopped at
   human-gate-package.md.
