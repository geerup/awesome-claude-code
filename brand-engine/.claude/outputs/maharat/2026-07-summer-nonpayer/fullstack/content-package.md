## 1. Editorial Calendar

### Strategic logic

The calendar is organised around the strategy angle from strategy-artifact section 4.1:
"This summer, build a real skill, taught by the people who set the standard." It sequences
content in three movements across the 8-week window:

- Movement 1 (weeks 1 to 2): Breadth and orientation. Help the reader see the full landscape.
  Which field? What is possible? Who teaches it? These pieces answer the "where do I even start"
  question that the strategy identifies as the primary owned non-payer barrier.
- Movement 2 (weeks 3 to 6): Per-field depth. One article per cleared instructor domain gives
  each interest segment a concrete entry point grounded in real class content. Arabic-first.
- Movement 3 (weeks 7 to 8): Conversion and proof. A summer-wrap framing ("what you built
  this summer") reinforces the subscription value and anchors the lifecycle close.

All seven cleared instructor domains are represented. The four non-nameable instructors
(Rahma Riad, Sami Al Jaber, Mona Ataya, Mo Islam) are never named and appear only in the
breadth framing as "and more."

The editorial calendar uses Arabic as the primary language throughout. English variants are
written for the two pieces with the highest new-acquisition (cold SEO) search potential:
the pillar orientation piece (Article 1) and the business piece (Article 8). English variants
are independent pieces adapted for English search intent, not translations.

Arabic is the primary delivery for the Saudi Arabian and GCC owned-audience. Every Arabic
article routes to copywriter-ar. English variants route to copywriter-en.

exact dates are ASSUMPTION pending seo-specialist keyword priority confirmation and Ahmed's
