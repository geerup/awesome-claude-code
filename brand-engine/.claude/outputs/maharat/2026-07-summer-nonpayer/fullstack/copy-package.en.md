## How to read this file

Variants are organized by channel block. Each variant carries: id, segment, headline, body,
one CTA, language en. Subject lines for email messages include a primary flag and a preheader.
The fills section at the end maps every variant to its asset-brief copy slots.

No em dashes anywhere. Western numerals only. One clear CTA per asset. No invented price,
promo, Skill Path title, lesson title, count, or instructor claim beyond cleared, page-sourced
facts. No accreditation.

The locked campaign angle: "Summer of Skills: choose your field and build a real skill with
the people who set the standard." Breadth-led, value-led, empowering.
