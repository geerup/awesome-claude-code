## Objective

Soft launch Skill Paths for the first time. Build an early-access cohort and prime the
audience for the full launch. Two concrete conversions in this flight:

1. Early-access signups at the gate (email or WhatsApp), from new and owned audiences.
2. App installs where the app surfaces Skill Paths.

This is a cohort-and-priming objective, not a revenue objective. No price, no promo, no firm
public launch date. The cohort built now is the warm base the full launch later converts.

## Audience segments

Sized from company-brief planning figures and the brief. Live sizes resolve at send time and
are recorded in the lifecycle package. No size is invented; where data is needed it is flagged.

### Segment 1: Owned non-payers (entry point B)

- definition: Maharat email contacts who have not purchased. Planning estimate about 18,000
  non-paying contacts of about 23,000 total (company-brief). Suppress unsubscribed and
  hard-bounced. Exclude existing Skill Paths early-access members if any (source to confirm,
  open item). Live size resolves at send time.
- recency cut (proposed, needs live data): a "recently engaged" tier (opened or clicked in a
  recent window) gets the fuller early-access flow first; a "dormant" tier gets a lighter
  re-engagement touch. Exact recency thresholds depend on live engagement data, flagged.
- pains: a skill they keep deferring, a busy day with no room for long lectures, prior
  interest in Maharat that never converted to a paid habit.
- gains: a format that fits minutes a day, visible progress, an early seat before the crowd.
- per-segment angle: "you already know us, here is the easiest way yet to start." Warm,
  insider, low-friction early-access invitation. The hero is the learner finally getting a
  method that fits, not a lapsed user being scolded. Illustrative anchor (routes to
  copywriter-ar, not final): ابدأ مهارتك التالية بخطوة واحدة كل يوم.

### Segment 2: New acquisition by self-improvement interest (entry points A and C)

- definition: new Arabic-speaking adults, roughly 18 to 35, GCC with Saudi Arabia primary
  (company-brief and brief), reached by paid and organic on a self-improvement and
  habit-building interest cut. Paid platforms per brief: Meta and Instagram primary, TikTok,
  Google and YouTube secondary. Organic across the owned social accounts (about 180,000
  followers, planning estimate). Exact interest-targeting taxonomy resolves in the media plan
  (performance-marketer), not here.
- pains: intent without a system, time scarcity, past starts that did not stick, skepticism
  toward yet another learning app.
- gains: a credible Arabic-first way to build a real skill in small daily steps, a streak that
  makes consistency feel achievable, the pull of being early.
- per-segment angle: "intent is the seed, here is the path that turns it into a skill."
  Discovery-led, benefit-first, the small-step and streak motif. The hero is the busy adult
  who can become someone with a skill that stays. Illustrative anchor (routes to copywriter):
  خطوة صغيرة كل يوم تبني مهارة تبقى معك.

### Segment 3: Retargeting (entry point A, paid)

- definition: people who already engaged but did not sign up. Sources: site and gate visitors
  who did not complete, social engagers, video viewers, and where lawful and available, app
  users who have not signed up for early access. Audience assembly and eligibility resolve in
  the media plan and tracking plan; no list is fabricated here. Owned email non-payers are
  segment 1 and are reached by lifecycle, not paid retargeting, to avoid double-paying.
- pains: interest that stalled at the threshold, hesitation, "I will do it later," unanswered
  questions about how it works or what it costs.
- gains: a clear reason to finish the signup now, reassurance that a step is small, the nudge
  that the early window is open.
- per-segment angle: "you were almost in, the seat is one step away." Reminder and
  reassurance, the method-not-the-clock reframe, completion of an action already started. The
  hero is the learner who only needs the last nudge. Illustrative anchor (routes to
  copywriter): مقعدك في الدخول المبكر بانتظارك، خطوة واحدة وتبدأ.

Note across all three: no Skill Path title, lesson, count, or lineup is named for any segment.
The angle speaks to format and benefit only, per the binding guardrail.

## Core angle and offer framing

- core angle: "Build a real skill, one small step a day." A hero's-journey arc from intent to
  a skill that stays, with Maharat as the guide and early access as the call to adventure.
  This is the angle the June social draft already established; this run carries it across all
  channels and re-times it to the July flight. The offer can actually deliver this: the format
  is bite-sized and streak-based by design, so the promise of small daily steps is honest.
- why it works: it meets the audience's real pain (time, not desire) and reframes it as a
  method problem the product solves, never as a deficit in the learner. It is empowering: the
  hero is the learner who builds a skill, Maharat is the guide.
- offer framing: early access to a new way to learn on Maharat. The conversion is a free
  signup at the gate (email or WhatsApp) and, where relevant, an app install. No price is
  required for an early-access signup, so no price appears. Framing positions the value as
  getting in early on a habit-forming, fits-your-day format, never as a discount or a deadline
  to an unconfirmed launch date.
- what framing must not do: no firm public launch date, no countdown to an unconfirmed date,
  no invented titles or lineup, no roadmap or unannounced plans, no accreditation claim
  (completion certificates only, never implied accredited), no price or promo until confirmed.
- empowering check: every anchor above speaks to who the learner becomes, not what they lack.
  No deficit framing.
