## Strategy angle served

"Build a real skill, one small step a day." Hero's-journey arc from intent to a skill that
stays. Maharat is the guide. Early access is the call to adventure. Streak and small-step
motifs are the visual and narrative thread across all four concepts and the teaser video.

The June social draft already established this arc (posts 1 through 10, path and streak motifs,
text-free briefs). This package extends and formalizes it across paid and organic formats for
the July flight, adds the paid ad set (static and video), adds a lifecycle email header concept,
and adds the teaser/hype video structure.

## Concepts

### C1. "The spark and the path" (organic social, foundational motif)

- description: a single emerald point of light on near-black. From it, a clean unbroken path
  line traces forward, growing brighter with each step. The path is the protagonist. No
  destination shown yet, only the direction of movement. This is the hero's ordinary world
  opening into potential.
- rationale: mirrors the strategy's "intent is the seed" angle for segment 2 (new
  acquisition). Establishes the visual language the full arc re-uses across all ten organic
  posts. Continuity is the creative equity: by post 10, the audience has seen this path grow
  across the whole hero's journey, so the arc itself becomes the reason to follow.
- visual constants: #141414 background, #009975 spark and path line, #1A1A1A subtle card
  surface if used. Generous negative space. No text, UI, or illustration of a specific skill
  or course.
- channel routing: organic social (entry point C). Posts 1 and 2 of the hero's-journey
  sequence. Re-timed from June draft to July flight.
- copy-slot direction for copywriters: one AR headline overlay, one EN headline overlay per
  asset. Empowering and brief. No mention of specific paths or titles. Soft-launch framing.

### C2. "The streak" (paid static and organic, conversion-driver motif)

- description: a horizontal row of emerald step-markers, left to right, most lit and bright,
  one "today" marker glowing at the leading edge. The streak is abstract: no numbers, no
  labels, no UI chrome. The emphasis is on the unbroken chain and the satisfying act of
  adding one more. This is the product's gamified loop expressed in pure visual form.
- rationale: mirrors the strategy's "small daily habit becomes a skill that stays" angle for
  segments 1 and 2. The streak motif is the product's most differentiated mechanic relative
  to long-form video courses. Showing it as an abstract visual, not as a screenshot, protects
  the guardrail on no UI and no invented path names while still communicating the format
  benefit honestly.
- visual constants: #141414 background, #009975 lit markers (full brightness), #1A1A1A
  unlit upcoming markers, #00684f dim variant for paused or dip state (C4 usage).
- channel routing: paid social (entry point A) for static ad units targeting segment 2 and
  retargeting segment 3. Also used in organic post 6 (Tests and Trials) and organic post 10
  (The Return).
- copy-slot direction for copywriters: AR headline overlay and EN headline overlay. For paid
  retargeting (segment 3), the slot direction is a reassurance hook, e.g., the seat is still
  open, one step. For paid acquisition (segment 2), the slot direction is benefit-first, the
  small habit becomes the skill. Exact wording is the copywriter's call, not set here.

### C3. "The threshold" (paid video, teaser, and story motif)

- description: a single emerald doorway or threshold shape in the near-black field. The path
  from C1 leads up to and through it. The threshold is not a product screenshot or a UI
  element: it is a pure geometric form, a tall rectangle of ambient emerald light. On the
  near side: the familiar dark field. On the far side: a faintly brighter version of the same
  dark, suggesting the next chapter is the same world but transformed. The CTA moment lives
  here: early access is the open door.
- rationale: maps to the strategy's "crossing the threshold" beat and the early-access offer
  framing. For paid, this is the conversion creative: the prospect has seen the path (C1) and
  the streak (C2), now they see the door. For organic, this is post 5 (early access opens).
  The same visual works as the hero frame in the teaser/hype video structure (V1 below).
- visual constants: #141414 field, #009975 threshold glow and linework, #1A1A1A frame or
  architrave detail around the threshold.
- channel routing: paid social video (entry point A), organic Stories and feed post 5 (entry
  point C), teaser/hype video anchor frame.
- copy-slot direction for copywriters: AR and EN CTA chip ("register interest" placeholder
  direction). No firm launch date. No seat count unless confirmed. Soft-launch / early-access
  framing only.

### C4. "The return, transformed" (lifecycle email header, reward motif)

- description: the full emerald progress ring, complete and glowing, with a clean abstract
  seal or completion badge at its center. The path from C1 is now closed in a loop. No clutter,
  no product UI, no external accreditation logo. The ring and seal are abstract achievements:
  they represent earned progress, not a credential. This is the reward beat, the moment the
  learner looks back at the streak they built.
- rationale: maps to the strategy's owned non-payer segment (segment 1) and the lifecycle
  re-engagement angle ("you already know us, here is the easiest way yet to start"). For a
  non-payer, the reward visual communicates what completing a learning path feels like before
  they have started, which converts aspiration into a concrete goal. It is also the correct
  visual for organic post 9 (The Reward).
- visual constants: #141414 background, #009975 ring and seal mark, #1A1A1A card surface
  behind the ring.
- channel routing: lifecycle email header (entry point B, segment 1), organic post 9 (entry
  point C). Sized per channel below.
- copy-slot direction for copywriters: AR and EN headline overlay. Empowering and forward-
  looking. No deficit framing. Guardrail: no language implying accreditation. The seal is a
  completion mark only.

## Text-free image and video prompts

These are direction prompts for a text-free visual pass by the designer (or a designer-approved
generative tool after build-vs-buy-eval). No Arabic, no Latin copy, no app UI, no specific
skill or course title, no instructor likeness, no accreditation mark appears inside any
generated image or video. All copy lives in overlay slots, applied at build time.

### Prompt P1 (for concept C1, static): "Spark and path, single frame"

A single point of deep emerald light against a near-black field (#141414). From the point, a
thin clean line traces forward and slightly upward across the lower two-thirds of the frame,
ending before it reaches any edge, suggesting an open horizon. The line brightens gradually
from origin to tip. A faint #1A1A1A card panel occupies the lower third, set back slightly,
giving a premium layered depth. No text, no characters, no UI elements, no decorative
elements. Generous empty space in the upper half. Mood: calm before a beginning, premium
stillness. Lighting is internal, the line is its own source of light.

### Prompt P2 (for concept C2, static): "Streak row, horizontal"

A horizontal row of small circular or pill-shaped markers occupying the central band of the
frame. The markers to the left and center are full emerald (#009975), lit, evenly spaced.
The leading marker at the right edge glows slightly brighter, as if just activated. Two or
three markers beyond the lit sequence are dim (#1A1A1A), showing the path continues. No
numbers, no labels, no text. The background is near-black (#141414). A subtle #1A1A1A
surface runs behind the marker row like a track, slightly recessed. Mood: momentum, habit,
satisfying order. The composition is centered and balanced.

### Prompt P3 (for concept C3, static): "Threshold, single door of light"

A tall, narrow rectangle of ambient emerald light (#009975) centered in the frame, set against
a near-black field (#141414). The rectangle is outlined with a fine emerald linework architrave
or minimal frame. The interior of the rectangle is a slightly lighter version of the background,
not white, not bright: a #1A1A1A to #222222 gradient, suggesting the other side is the same
world but further along. The path line from P1 approaches from the lower left and terminates
at the base of the rectangle. No text, no UI, no characters. Mood: invitation, a clear choice
point, premium restraint. The visual weight is centered and draws the eye directly to the
threshold.

### Prompt P4 (for concept C4, static): "Completion ring and seal"

A centered circular progress ring at approximately 70 percent of frame height, drawn in
emerald (#009975), complete and closed, with a soft glow extending a few pixels outward.
Inside the ring, at the center, a simple abstract seal or mark: two or three concentric
arcs or a minimal radial motif, emerald on near-black. No text, no numbers, no external
logos, no accreditation marks. The background is near-black (#141414). A card surface
(#1A1A1A) sits behind the ring, slightly larger than the ring, giving a premium framed feel.
Mood: earned stillness, quiet achievement. The ring is the subject; nothing competes with it.

### Prompt P5 (for concept C2, motion variant): "Streak lighting, short loop"

A motion version of P2. The markers are dark at the first frame. Over 6 to 9 seconds, each
marker lights in sequence left to right, each activation accompanied by a brief, soft emerald
pulse. The leading marker at the right, once lit, pulses steadily for 2 seconds, then holds.
The final 1 second of the loop holds the fully lit streak. No text, no characters, no UI.
Background and palette identical to P2. Mood: building momentum, the habit growing one step
at a time. The motion is smooth and unhurried, not bouncy or gamified-feeling. Audio direction
is separate and set by the post producer: this prompt covers visuals only.

### Prompt P6 (for teaser video V1, motion): "Hero's journey in 30 to 45 seconds"

A sequential motion piece assembling the arc across the four concepts. All text-free inside
the video; copy overlays are applied at the build stage by the designer.

Frame sequence direction:

Act 1 (0 to 8 seconds): the spark and dark field from P1. A single emerald point appears in
near-black. Silence or a minimal ambient tone. The point breathes once and holds.

Act 2 (8 to 20 seconds): the path begins. The emerald line traces forward from the point,
slow at first, then gaining pace. The streak markers from P2 appear along the path, lighting
one by one as the line passes through them.

Act 3 (20 to 32 seconds): the threshold from P3 appears at the end of the path. The path
arrives at the threshold base. The emerald door glows and opens slightly inward, suggesting
entry. The visual does not show "inside": only the invitation.

Act 4 (32 to 42 seconds): the completion ring from P4 fades in as a final frame. Full ring,
glowing seal, held for 3 seconds. Then the frame holds on near-black with space for the brand
mark overlay and CTA overlay, which are added at build time.

Throughout: no text, no app UI, no specific path title, no instructor, no accreditation mark.
Palette constants hold for every frame. Transitions are slow dissolves or continuous motion,
not hard cuts. The aesthetic is premium and restrained throughout.
