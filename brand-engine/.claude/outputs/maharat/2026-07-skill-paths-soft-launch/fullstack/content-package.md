### AR-01: خطوة واحدة كل يوم: كيف تبني عادة تعلم حقيقية

- language: Arabic
- routes_to: copywriter-ar
- publish_target: 2026-07-01
- funnel_stage: awareness
- word_count_target: 700 to 900 words (readable in under 5 minutes, fits the small-step
  promise)

**Angle:**
The reader has the intention to learn something new but keeps starting and stopping. This
article reframes that pattern as a method problem, not a motivation problem. One small
daily step, consistent over weeks, builds a real skill. The hero is the reader who finally
has the right method. Empowering, never deficit-framed.

**Target keyword intent (placeholder, update from seo-package):**
Primary: تعلم يومي / بناء عادة تعلم. User intent: informational, Arabic-speaking adults
searching for how to build a consistent learning routine. Secondary: تطوير الذات, مهارات
جديدة.

**Suggested structure and headings (internal working structure, not final Arabic headings):**
1. Opening hook: a scene the reader recognizes. The learner who opens an app, watches
   five minutes, then stops for three weeks. Short, concrete, empathetic.
2. The method gap: why most people fail to build a learning habit is not about
   willpower. It is about the size of the step. Cite a concrete principle (habit
   science is general knowledge, no invented study or stat).
3. What a small daily step actually looks like: concrete, time-based framing (minutes
   a day, not a course to finish). Practical and honest.
4. How consistency compounds: the streak as a visible record of progress, not a
   punishing streak you lose. Motivating, not threatening.
5. Closing invitation: now is a good time to start. Link to the early-access gate.

**Internal-link targets:**
- Link to the signup gate (early access) from the closing CTA section.
- Link to AR-03 (streaks article) from section 4 once it is live. Use a contextual
  anchor on the streak concept.
- Link to EN-01 if cross-language linking is in scope for the blog CMS. Flag as
  conditional on CMS confirmation.

**CTA:**
Closing CTA routes to the early-access signup gate. Framing: "ابدأ مهارتك التالية
بخطوة واحدة كل يوم" (illustrative, routes through copywriter-ar and arabic-copy-qa
before use). No price. No firm launch date. No invented Skill Path title. Gate URL is
an open item pending gate platform confirmation.

Confirm the exact Arabic keyword target with seo-specialist before the copywriter

### AR-02: التعلم في دقائق: لماذا يناسبك التعلم المصغر

- language: Arabic
- routes_to: copywriter-ar
- publish_target: 2026-07-05
- funnel_stage: consideration
- word_count_target: 650 to 850 words

**Angle:**
The reader's real barrier to learning is time, not interest. This article makes the case
that micro-learning, learning in bite-sized pieces, is not a shortcut or a lesser format.
It is the format that works for a busy adult. The hero is the reader who realizes their
day already has enough minutes for a real skill. Empowering. No deficit framing.

**Target keyword intent (placeholder, update from seo-package):**
Primary: تعلم في وقت قصير / التعلم المصغر. User intent: informational, someone who wants
to learn but feels they do not have time. Secondary: مهارات جديدة, تعلم سريع.

**Suggested structure and headings:**
1. Opening: the reader's familiar feeling of wanting to learn but not having time. One
   short, concrete scenario. No lecturing.
2. What micro-learning actually is: a clear, plain explanation of bite-sized learning.
   No academic jargon. Explain the format benefit (fits a commute, a lunch break, a
   quiet 10 minutes).
3. Why small pieces lead to real skills: explain how spaced repetition and short
   sessions work together. General habit-science framing, no invented statistics.
4. A practical time map: show the reader where minutes already exist in their day.
   Concrete and realistic. Not aspirational fantasy.
5. Closing invitation and CTA: link to the early-access gate.

**Internal-link targets:**
- Link to AR-01 (habit-building article) from section 3, on the consistency angle.
- Link to AR-03 (streaks article) from section 3 once it is live.
- Link to the early-access gate from the CTA.

**CTA:**
Closing CTA routes to the early-access signup gate. Framing angle: the reader has the
time, here is the format that fits it. No price. No Skill Path title. Gate URL pending
platform confirmation.
