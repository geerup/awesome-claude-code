## Body

### 1. Channel mix

No prior report-artifact exists for this campaign. Channel roles are justified by the strategy-artifact angle ("Build a real skill, one small step a day"), the two acquisition segments (new self-improvement interest audience and retargeting), and the objective (early-access signups and app installs) across a 14-day flight in GCC, Saudi Arabia primary.

#### Meta and Instagram (primary prospecting and retargeting)

Role: primary paid channel, prospecting and retargeting.

Rationale: Meta and Instagram are the brief's designated primary paid channel (brief sec 5). The platform reaches Arabic-speaking adults 18 to 35 in Saudi Arabia and the GCC at scale, with interest-based and lookalike prospecting at practical reach for a 14-day flight. Instagram Stories and Reels fit the short-form, streak-and-small-step creative angle. Retargeting via custom audiences (site visitors, video viewers, social engagers, app users) is mature and well-supported. The soft-launch and early-access framing suits a lower-funnel, sign-up objective that Meta's conversion and lead-generation campaign types can optimize toward. App install campaigns (Mobile App Installs objective) are available and relevant where the app surfaces Skill Paths.

#### TikTok (secondary prospecting, reach extension among younger segment)

Role: secondary paid channel, prospecting only in this flight. No retargeting on TikTok in week 1 given the small initial pool; retargeting becomes viable in week 2 if video-view pools are large enough.

Rationale: TikTok is designated secondary in the brief (brief sec 5). It extends reach among the 18 to 25 sub-segment where consumption of short-form self-improvement content is high. The streak and small-step creative concept is native to the format. Budget allocation is conservative given this is a first campaign with no platform-specific prior data. TikTok account access is an open item; the channel is planned and staged but live action is blocked until access is confirmed.

#### Google Search (secondary, high-intent acquisition)

Role: secondary paid channel, high-intent keyword targeting for users actively searching for learning apps, Arabic skill development, or habit-building apps in the GCC.

Rationale: Google Search captures demand already in motion, complementing the awareness-and-discovery role of Meta and TikTok. For a soft launch, the intent signal is valuable: a user searching for a learning app is closer to signup than a scrolling social user. Budget is intentionally modest because search volume for Arabic-language learning intent in the GCC, while real, is smaller than the broad social audiences. This channel's role is efficiency, not reach. Google Ads account access is an open item; planned and staged, live action blocked until confirmed.

#### YouTube (secondary, upper-funnel reach and retargeting)

Role: secondary paid channel. In this 14-day flight, YouTube runs in week 2 only, after Meta and TikTok have produced video-view retargeting pools. Role is reach and retargeting, not primary conversion. The short-form (YouTube Shorts) and skippable in-stream formats suit the brand creative.

Rationale: YouTube is designated secondary in the brief. A 14-day flight is short for YouTube as a primary conversion driver, so this plan phases it into week 2 as a retargeting and reinforcement channel rather than a prospecting engine. This also allows budget to concentrate in Meta during the critical week-1 seed phase. Google Ads (which covers YouTube) account access is an open item; same block as Google Search.

#### Channels not included

- Programmatic display: not in the brief channel list; not added.
- LinkedIn: audience skew does not match the 18 to 35 Arabic self-improvement segment for this offer at this budget.
- Snapchat: not in the brief; noted as a potential future channel for younger GCC audiences, but not added without brief authorization.

### 3. Audience strategy

No personal or sensitive data appears in any definition below. All segments are defined by interest, behavior, and engagement signal, not by individual-level data. Suppression and exclusion lists are assembled by paid-build-engineer from the approved sources at build time, not fabricated here.

#### Meta and Instagram audiences

Prospecting (Segment 2: new acquisition by self-improvement interest):

- Core interest prospecting: Arabic-speaking adults, 18 to 35, Saudi Arabia primary, GCC secondary. Interest and behavior targeting across: personal development and self-improvement; productivity and habit formation; online learning and e-learning; books and reading habits; career growth and professional skills. Layered to exclude existing page engagers and existing website visitors (to avoid overlap with retargeting). Language targeting: Arabic.
- Lookalike audience: 1 percent lookalike of the Maharat website custom audience (all visitors) in Saudi Arabia, once the pixel pool is confirmed sufficient (minimum roughly 1,000 seed events). If the pool is below threshold at build time, this lookalike is held and the prospecting budget runs on interest targeting only. Paid-build-engineer confirms the pool size at build. Lookalike expanded to 2 to 3 percent if 1 percent reach is insufficient for the 14-day flight budget.
- Exclusion applied to all prospecting: existing Maharat customers (custom audience from the customer list, once suppression list is confirmed); existing early-access signups (open item, source to confirm); existing Meta page followers (optional, reduces wasted impressions on owned audience who are better reached by lifecycle).

Retargeting (Segment 3: paid retargeting):

- Website visitors (all pages) in the last 30 days who did not complete the early-access signup, once the gate page URL is confirmed.
- Video viewers: people who watched 50 percent or more of any Maharat video on Meta or Instagram in the last 60 days.
- Instagram and Facebook social engagers: people who engaged with the Maharat page or profile in the last 60 days.
- App users (OPEN ITEM): if the app custom audience (SDK or mobile measurement partner events) is available and the pool is large enough, include app users who have not completed early-access signup. App audience size is an open item; this pool holds until confirmed.
- Exclusion applied to all retargeting: same customer and early-access suppression as prospecting.

App install campaign audience (where Meta App Install objective is used):

- Prospecting: same interest targeting as above, narrowed toward the 18 to 30 sub-segment where app-install propensity is higher. Lookalike of existing app installer events if the mobile measurement partner event data is available at build time.
- Retargeting: website visitors and social engagers who have not installed the app, using a device-type filter (mobile only).

#### TikTok audiences

Prospecting (Segment 2):

- Interest targeting: self-improvement, productivity, online education, book and reading communities, life goals. Arabic language targeting. Saudi Arabia primary, GCC secondary. Age 18 to 35.
- Behavioral targeting: users who have interacted with education and self-development content.
- Exclusion: existing followers of the Maharat TikTok account; previous video viewers (moved to retargeting in week 2).

Retargeting (week 2, Segment 3, conditional on pool size):

- Video viewers: users who watched 75 percent or more of any Maharat TikTok video in the last 30 days. This pool is built in week 1. If the pool is below the TikTok minimum audience size threshold at the start of week 2, the retargeting budget (5 percent, 500 in whatever currency) rolls to Meta retargeting and paid-build-engineer flags it as a human-gate proposal.
- Exclusion: same suppression as prospecting.

#### Google Search audiences

Keyword intent targeting (Segment 2, high-intent):

- Primary intent cluster: queries indicating intent to learn a skill in Arabic, find a learning app in Arabic, or build a habit. Examples of intent themes (not exhaustive and not final keyword lists, which resolve in paid-build-engineer's build): Arabic language skill development apps, online learning Arabic, self-improvement app, habit building app Arabic, Maharat app (branded).
- Branded keywords: Maharat branded terms and variations. These capture users who have already heard of Maharat and are looking it up. Branded campaigns run at higher bids and separate from non-branded.
- Geo: Saudi Arabia primary, UAE and Kuwait secondary.
- Language targeting: Arabic and English (users in GCC search in both languages for learning content).
- Audience layering (observation mode): in-market for education apps; self-improvement content consumers as a Google audience signal. These do not restrict reach but inform bid adjustments.
- Exclusion: existing customers excluded by Customer Match list, once the suppression list is confirmed.

Retargeting (Google Display Network, week 2 only, modest):

- Remarketing list for search ads (RLSA): users who visited the Maharat site (all pages) in the last 30 days get a bid uplift on relevant search queries. This does not create a separate ad set; it modifies bids on the existing search campaigns for warm users.

#### YouTube audiences (week 2 only)

Retargeting and reach (Segment 3 and Segment 2 warm):

- YouTube video viewers: users who watched any Maharat YouTube content in the last 30 days. This is the primary retargeting pool.
- Website remarketing (Google Ads remarketing): users who visited the Maharat site in the last 30 days.
- Custom intent audience: users who searched for self-improvement and online learning queries on Google in the last 30 days, targeted with video ads on YouTube. This bridges search intent with video engagement.
- Prospecting (secondary): affinity and in-market audiences for self-development and online education, Arabic-speaking adults, Saudi Arabia primary. Kept at a small budget given the week-2-only flight window.
- Exclusion: same customer and early-access suppression as other channels.

### 4. Bid strategy

The brief's stated target is early-access signups and app installs. The numeric cost-per-signup target is an OPEN ITEM (not set in brief or strategy-artifact). The bid strategy below is structured to optimize toward the conversion objective and positions the campaign to report cost per signup once the target is confirmed.

Currency is an open item. All per-unit bid references below are structural (percentage relationships and logic), not absolute amounts, and resolve once currency is confirmed.

#### Meta and Instagram

- Primary bid type: cost-cap or bid-cap at the conversion objective (Lead or Complete Registration event at the signup gate). Cost-cap is preferred: it instructs Meta to stay at or below a target cost per result while maximizing volume. The specific cost-cap value is set to the confirmed cost-per-signup target once Ahmed provides it. Until the target is confirmed, the plan uses a learning-budget approach in week 1 (no hard cost cap, lowest-cost bidding) to gather conversion data, then switches to cost-cap in week 2.
- App install campaigns: App Event Optimization toward the install event (or a downstream event such as registration within app if the mobile measurement partner passes it). Bid type: lowest cost in week 1, shifting to cost cap in week 2 if enough install events are recorded (Meta requires roughly 50 optimization events per week per ad set to exit the learning phase).
- Retargeting ad sets: lowest-cost bidding in week 1 (small pool, needs volume); if retargeting pools grow enough for cost-cap to be stable, shift in week 2 via the optimization proposals in section 6.

#### TikTok

- Campaign objective: conversions (if the TikTok pixel is live on the signup gate) or traffic in week 1 if the pixel pool is too small for conversion optimization. Bid type: lowest cost (oCPM) in week 1. If conversion volume from TikTok is sufficient by end of week 1 (at least 20 to 30 events), shift to target CPA bidding in week 2.
- App install campaigns: App Event Optimization (AEO) in TikTok Ads Manager if the TikTok SDK is integrated. Falls back to app install objective without AEO if SDK events are not available at build time. Paid-build-engineer confirms SDK status at build.

#### Google Search

- Branded campaigns: target impression share at the top of the page (90 percent or above). Branded terms have minimal competition, so this is a low-cost, high-value capture strategy.
- Non-branded campaigns: maximize conversions in week 1 to gather conversion data (requires conversion tracking to be live at the gate URL). If conversion volume is sufficient by end of week 1 (roughly 30 or more conversions), shift to target CPA bidding in week 2. If volume is insufficient, remain on maximize conversions for the full flight.
- Bid note: a confirmed cost-per-signup target resolves the target CPA for week 2. Without it, the campaign remains on maximize conversions for the full flight. This is flagged as an open item at the human gate.

#### YouTube

- Week 2 only. Campaign type: video action campaign (VAC), which optimizes toward a conversion action (signup or install) rather than views. Bid type: target CPA if the conversion target is confirmed; maximize conversions if not.
- For reach with retargeting pools that are too small for conversion optimization: CPV (cost per view) at a skippable in-stream placement as a secondary option.

### 5. Flighting and pacing

Flight window: 2026-07-01 to 2026-07-14, 14 days, supplied by Ahmed.

#### Week 1: seed phase (2026-07-01 to 2026-07-07)

Goals for week 1:
- Establish Meta and TikTok ad account delivery and exit the learning phase with enough conversion data to inform week-2 bids.
- Build the custom audience pools (video viewers, site visitors, engagers) that fuel week-2 retargeting.
- Capture branded and high-intent Google Search traffic at the lowest cost.
- Identify which Meta ad sets (interest vs. lookalike, which creatives) are driving signups at lower cost, so week-2 reallocation proposals have data to act on.

Channel activity:
- Meta and Instagram: prospecting and early retargeting (if pools are large enough from organic/pre-existing audiences). Conversion objective (Lead or App Install). Broad interest and lookalike sets running in parallel for learning. Daily budget spread evenly across the 7 days.
- TikTok: prospecting only. Traffic or conversion objective depending on pixel readiness. Builds video-view pools for week-2 retargeting.
- Google Search: branded and non-branded campaigns live from day 1 at a steady daily rate.
- YouTube: paused in week 1.

Learning budget note: Meta and TikTok require a minimum number of conversion events per ad set per week to exit the learning phase (Meta roughly 50 per ad set per week, TikTok roughly 20 to 30). At 10,000 total, the per-ad-set weekly budgets are modest. Paid-build-engineer should consolidate ad sets where possible (fewer, broader ad sets rather than many narrow ones) to concentrate conversion signals and reduce time in learning. This is a structural note for the build, flagged here for the human gate.

#### Week 2: optimize phase (2026-07-08 to 2026-07-14)

Goals for week 2:
- Activate retargeting audiences built in week 1 across Meta, YouTube, and (conditionally) TikTok.
- Shift budget toward the ad sets and channels that proved most efficient in week 1, via the in-flight optimization proposals in section 6.
- Maintain Google Search at its steady rate; apply RLSA bid uplifts.
- Drive the final push toward early-access signups before the flight closes on 2026-07-14.

Channel activity:
- Meta and Instagram: prospecting continues at reduced proportional spend; retargeting activates with the custom audiences built in week 1. Cost-cap bidding applied if conversion data is sufficient.
- TikTok: retargeting activates (conditional on pool size). Non-performing prospecting ad sets are paused or reduced per section 6 proposals.
- Google Search: steady rate continues; bid adjustments applied if data shows device or location skew.
- YouTube: activates in week 2 with retargeting and custom intent audiences. Video action campaign toward conversion.

End-of-flight actions (2026-07-14): all campaigns move to paused at end of flight. Final spend is reported. No campaign extends beyond 2026-07-14 without a new brief input and human-gate approval.

### 6. In-flight optimization triggers (human-gate proposals)

These are proposals. No change takes effect without explicit human-gate approval per action. The agent does not act on spend; paid-build-engineer executes approved proposals.

The following triggers are defined in advance so that when the performance-readout arrives from analytics-reporter (stream 8), the performance-marketer can assess it against these criteria and submit a specific, actionable proposal to the human gate.

#### Trigger 1: underperforming channel reallocation

Condition: by end of day 5 (2026-07-05), a channel or ad set has spent at least 20 percent of its weekly budget and is showing zero or near-zero conversions (signups or installs), with a cost per click or cost per result more than 3 times the average of other active channels.

Proposed action: pause the underperforming ad set or reduce its daily budget by 50 percent and reallocate to the best-performing active channel. Route to the human gate with the specific ad set ID (or campaign name), the current spend, the conversion count, and the receiving channel. The human gate approves or rejects the reallocation before any budget moves.

#### Trigger 2: TikTok retargeting pool too small for week 2

Condition: by end of day 7 (2026-07-07), TikTok video-view retargeting pool is below the platform's minimum audience size threshold (typically 1,000 in the region).

Proposed action: cancel TikTok retargeting for week 2 and reallocate the 500 (currency TBC, 5 percent of total) to Meta retargeting. Route to the human gate as a proposal before moving.

#### Trigger 3: Meta learning phase not exiting

Condition: by end of day 7, a Meta ad set has not exited the learning phase and is spending more than 20 percent of its planned weekly budget with a cost per result more than 2 times the current average.

Proposed action: consolidate that ad set into a broader audience group or pause it and consolidate its budget into the ad set that has exited learning. Route to the human gate.

#### Trigger 4: strong early performer, increase budget

Condition: by end of day 5, a channel or ad set has delivered signups at a cost per signup below 50 percent of the average across all channels, with statistical confidence (at least 15 to 20 conversions).

Proposed action: increase that ad set's daily budget by up to 30 percent, funded by a proportional reduction in the weakest-performing ad set(s). Route to the human gate with the current cost per signup, the proposed new daily budget, and the source of the reallocation. Human gate must approve before the budget increase is made. The total across all channels never exceeds 10,000.

#### Trigger 5: early-access seat cap reached

Condition (only if a cap is confirmed): the early-access seat count reaches the cap.

Proposed action: pause all paid acquisition campaigns immediately (all channels, all ad sets). Route to the human gate as an urgent proposal with the conversion count and the cap number. Do not let campaigns continue driving signups to a full gate. This trigger activates only if the open item on seat cap is resolved as "yes, there is a cap."

#### Trigger 6: Google Search non-branded conversion rate is low

Condition: by end of day 7, non-branded Google Search has spent its week-1 budget and produced fewer conversions than branded Search at a cost more than 2 times branded.

Proposed action: reduce non-branded Search daily budget by 30 percent and increase branded. Route to the human gate. No change without approval.
