# Minimal launchable slice: Skill Paths soft launch

The smallest, cleanest piece of the campaign that can go live while the heavy blockers stay
open. Designed to sidestep the gate platform, the retargeting and lookalike consent question,
the cross-border hashed-list upload question, and the rights-cleared asset dependency. This is a
proposal, staged and paused. It does not spend. Spend is the gated action and needs an explicit
per-action approval plus the reduced must-haves below.

- campaign_id: 2026-07-skill-paths-soft-launch
- parent: fullstack/paid-launch-package.md and fullstack/paid-spend-plan.md
- status: gated-pending

## The idea

Run prospecting only, with abstract brand-constant creative, to destinations that already exist.
That removes the four blockers that otherwise hold the whole paid launch:

- No retargeting and no lookalikes, so no custom-audience build and no cross-border hashed-list
  upload to Meta, TikTok, or Google. The PDPL transfer question does not gate this slice.
- Abstract emerald-on-near-black creative (concepts C1 to C3, no app UI, no invented titles), so
  no dependency on approved Skill Paths product imagery or a video asset. YouTube stays out.
- Destinations that exist today, so no dependency on a new landing page or gate platform for the
  cleaner tier.

## Two tiers, by how clean they are

### Tier 1, cleanest: app-install prospecting to the store listings

- Channels and objectives: Meta app-install prospecting, TikTok app-install prospecting, Google
  App Campaign (UAC). Prospecting interest and behavior targeting only (self-improvement, habit,
  learning). No retargeting, no lookalikes.
- Destination: the existing Maharat App Store and Google Play listings. We collect no personal
  data ourselves, the stores handle the install, so the signup-gate platform and the gate
  privacy notice do not block this tier.
- Creative: C2 streak motif and C3, abstract, text-free, Arabic as overlay in build. Copy from
  copy-package.ar.md and copy-package.en.md prospecting variants (already gate-passed).
- Measurement: platform-reported installs work on day 1. An MMP (AppsFlyer, Adjust, Branch)
  improves attribution and is recommended but is not required to start. Flag as a fast-follow.

### Tier 2, unlocks with the gate: web-signup prospecting to the early-access page

- Same prospecting channels, web-signup objective, landing on the early-access page and gate.
- Blocked until: the gate platform (email or WhatsApp) is named, the privacy notice names the
  legal data controller, and a live privacy policy URL is linked at the gate (compliance C-01,
  C-02, C-11). Until then, Tier 1 carries the slice.

## What this slice still needs before it spends (reduced must-haves)

1. Currency confirmed (SAR or USD).
2. Ad-account access for Meta, TikTok, and Google, and the app listed for app-install campaigns
   on each platform.
3. Basic install tracking live: platform SDK or the MMP. Platform-reported installs are enough to
   start, MMP is the fast-follow for clean attribution.
4. Creative rendered: the abstract C2 and C3 specs built by the designer (design-qa passed at
   spec level, a human design check on the render remains).
5. An explicit per-action approval to spend, and reconfirmation of the soft-launch framing.

## What it deliberately leaves out (and why)

- Retargeting and lookalikes: need the consent basis and the cross-border transfer answer (PDPL
  C-08). Excluded so the slice can move now.
- YouTube: needs a video asset. Excluded.
- The email and app-push sends, and the organic comment-to-DM mechanic: need the send and DM
  platforms. Out of this slice.
- PR distribution: needs the launch-announcement reconfirmation and a spokesperson. Out.
- Tier 2 web-signup: held on the gate platform and privacy policy.
