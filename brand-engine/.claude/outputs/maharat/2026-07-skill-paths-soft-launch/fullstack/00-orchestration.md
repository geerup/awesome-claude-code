## Hard guardrails for this campaign (bind every agent)

- Do NOT invent Skill Path titles, lessons, counts, streak specifics, or a content lineup. Speak
  to the format and the benefit only: a small step a day, a streak that builds a real skill,
  learning that fits a busy day.
- Soft launch and early access framing only. No firm public launch date. No countdown to an
  unconfirmed date. No roadmap, fundraising, or unannounced plans. No accreditation.
- Arabic-first, MSA Gulf-familiar, Thmanyah tone, empowering never deficit-framed.
- No em dashes, no tatweel, Western numerals. Brand constants #141414, #1A1A1A, #009975.
- Generated images text-free, Arabic overlaid in build, never baked. No rendered app UI or
  invented titles in any asset.
- No price or promo unless the brief confirms one. No personal or sensitive data in tracking.
