## 2. Announcement plan

### What is being announced

Maharat is making Skill Paths available to an early-access cohort for the first time. This is a
soft launch. It is not a full public launch. The announcement invites a limited early-access group
to experience a new way to learn on Maharat: a gamified, bite-sized format built for consistent
daily progress, where a small step each day builds a skill that stays.

What is said publicly is limited to:
- The format exists and is now open for early access.
- The format is gamified, bite-sized, and designed for daily habit-building.
- Interested people can join the early-access list via a signup at the gate.
- Maharat is an Arabic-first self-development platform.

What is not said publicly (hard stops carried from the guardrail check):
- No Skill Path titles, lesson counts, or content lineup.
- No firm public launch date or countdown.
- No price or promotional offer (brief confirms none yet).
- No roadmap, future feature, or unannounced plan.
- No accreditation claim.
- No spokesperson by name until confirmation arrives.

### Confirmed-public facts the announcement rests on

1. Skill Paths is a gamified, bite-sized, Duolingo-like learning format built by Maharat.
   Source: brief sec 4, brand-voice.md.
2. The product is in first-time soft launch and early access is now open.
   Source: brief sec 2 (authorized by Ahmed 2026-06-05).
3. Maharat is an Arabic-first self-development platform serving Arabic-speaking adults,
   primarily in the GCC and Saudi Arabia.
   Source: brand-voice.md, brief sec 3.
4. The conversion is a free early-access signup (email or WhatsApp gate).
   Source: brief sec 4 and 5.
5. The format is designed for a busy day: small steps, streaks, visible progress.
   Source: brief sec 4 (offer framing notes), strategy-artifact core angle.

### Timing

The soft-launch flight runs 2026-07-01 to 2026-07-14 per the brief. PR outreach targets this
window. No specific date is presented to media as a "launch date." The outreach framing is the
opening of early access, timed to coincide with the campaign flight.

Note on timing: the exact outreach date within the window is an execution decision confirmed at the
human gate. The media kit should be ready by 2026-06-25 to allow preparation time.

### Channels for PR and earned reach

| Channel | Role |

| Arabic-first press release | Primary earned media asset. Arabic version is primary; English version is a direct-language variant, not a translation afterthought. Routed to copywriter-ar (Arabic) and copywriter-en (English). |

not the press release itself. The press copy is written from this brief, then routed through

### Headline direction

Arabic headline direction (routes to copywriter-ar, not final copy):
"مهارة تبنيها يوماً بيوم: مهارات مهارات متاحة الآن للوصول المبكر"

Note: the above is an angle direction only. It contains "مهارات مهارات" as a placeholder because

English headline direction (routes to copywriter-en, not final copy):
"Build a real skill, one step a day. Skill Paths early access is open on Maharat."

### Body structure

The press release should follow this structure:

1. Lead paragraph: what is happening now. Maharat has opened early access to Skill Paths, a new
   gamified learning format designed for daily habit-building. Arabic-first. One or two sentences.
   Active voice.

2. What Skill Paths is: the format, in plain language. Gamified, bite-sized, streak-based.
   Designed for Arabic-speaking adults with a busy day. No titles, no lineup, no lesson count.
   Two to three sentences. The benefit, not a feature list.

3. The early-access invitation: how to join (signup at the gate, free). The pull is being early
   on a format built for them. No price, no deadline to an unconfirmed date, no "limited seats"
   unless the seat cap is confirmed at the human gate.

4. About Maharat: one short paragraph. Arabic-first, self-development platform, GCC focus, Saudi
   Arabia primary market. No accreditation. No fundraising. No roadmap.

5. Media contact line: placeholder. Actual contact confirmed at the human gate.

6. Boilerplate: short, factual. What Maharat is. No invented claims.

### Tone and mechanics

- Tone: clear, confident, empowering. Thmanyah benchmark. Smart-friend register.
- No em dashes in either language.
- Western numerals only.
- Arabic is primary: written first, best effort, not a translation of the English.
- No tatweel.
- No deficit framing. No "you are missing out" or urgency manufactured from a false scarcity.
- No accreditation language, no roadmap, no price, no title.
- RTL-safe for the Arabic version.

### Length

Arabic: 250 to 350 words. English: 200 to 300 words. Both should be releasable as standalone
documents, not dependent on each other.

## 4. Media list and outreach plan

The media list targets regional Arabic-language and bilingual tech, education, and
self-improvement outlets. The angle for each is calibrated to what is confirmed public. No
personal data (journalist home contact, personal email, personal mobile) beyond what
professional outreach legitimately requires (professional email, published contact).

No tracking links with personal identifiers. If a UTM or tracking parameter is used on the press
release link, it carries only outlet-level attribution (e.g., source=arab-news, not a personal
identifier). Privacy review confirms this before outreach.

### Outlet categories and angles

The specific outlet names and journalist contacts are execution variables that the human gate
approves. The categories and the per-category angle are set here for the outreach plan.

#### Category 1: Arabic-language technology and startup media

Examples of the type (not an exhaustive confirmed list): outlets covering Saudi and GCC tech,
startup culture, and digital products for an Arabic-speaking professional audience.

Angle: a Riyadh-based edtech platform is opening early access to a new gamified learning format
built for Arabic speakers. The format is designed for consistency: small daily steps, streaks,
a method that fits a busy professional's day. The platform serves a market of Arabic-speaking
adults who want to grow skills but find existing formats too long or too English-first.

Key message for this category: product innovation in Arabic-first edtech. The format is the news,
not a funding round.

#### Category 2: Education and upskilling media (Arabic and bilingual)

Outlets and newsletters covering professional development, learning technology, and workforce
skills in the GCC region.

Angle: habit-based micro-learning is a proven format (Duolingo-like design) now applied to
professional self-development in Arabic. Maharat's Skill Paths brings this format to a market
that has lacked a credible Arabic-first option. Early access is the first opportunity for
educators and learners to explore it.

Key message for this category: Arabic-first micro-learning for professional skill-building. The
pedagogical angle is the format's design for habit formation.

#### Category 3: Self-improvement and personal development communities

Arabic-language LinkedIn communities, X accounts, and newsletter writers covering personal
productivity, self-development, and professional growth in the Arab world.

Angle: this is for the person who keeps meaning to build a skill but finds the formats available
too time-consuming or misaligned with an Arabic-first experience. A new option is now open for
early access.

Key message: the everyday learner. Not a formal media pitch; a community invitation.

#### Category 4: Saudi Arabia and GCC general-interest and business media

Bilingual or English-language outlets covering Saudi Arabia and GCC business, digital economy,
and consumer technology.

English angle: Maharat, an Arabic-first self-development platform based in the region, has opened
early access to Skill Paths, a gamified learning format designed for daily habit-building. The
launch targets Arabic-speaking adults in Saudi Arabia and the GCC who want to develop professional
skills in a format that fits their day.

Key message for this category: regional product story with a market-size angle (Arabic-speaking
adult learners, GCC, Saudi primary). Not a funding story. Not a roadmap story.

### Outreach timing

Outreach window: aligned to the campaign flight (2026-07-01 to 2026-07-14). Recommended: pitch
and distribute the press release at or just before the campaign start date so earned coverage
can amplify the paid and organic activity. Exact date confirmed at the human gate.

Pre-distribution media kit readiness target: 2026-06-25.
