## 1. Content plan

### 1.1 Governing angle (from strategy-artifact)

"Build a real skill, one small step a day." The hero is the busy Arabic-speaking adult who has
intent but lacks a method. Maharat is the guide. Early access is the call to adventure. The
arc moves from the ordinary world through the hero's journey to the reward of a skill that
stays. The June social draft (10 posts) established this arc over a 3-week window ending
2026-06-28. This July plan re-times it to 2026-07-01 to 2026-07-14, compresses it to two
focused weeks, and extends it with three additional post formats that were not fully built in
the June draft: a story engagement sequence, a soft countdown story (no firm date), and a
value carousel.

### 1.2 Themes across the 14-day flight

| Theme | Hero's journey beat | Week |
|---|---|---|
| The deferred skill: intent is the seed | Ordinary World | Week 1 |
| The method, not the clock: 5 minutes is enough | Refusal of the Call | Week 1 |
| How it works: streak, step, visible progress | Meeting the Mentor | Week 1 |
| Early access is open: the threshold moment | Crossing the Threshold | Week 1 to 2 |
| The daily habit builds: consistency over perfection | Tests and Trials / The Ordeal | Week 2 |
| You are not alone: a community of learners | Allies | Week 2 |
| The skill is yours now: reward and return | The Reward / The Return | Week 2 |

### 1.3 Formats selected per channel and segment

The July flight adds three named template formats to the single-image and short-video
foundation the June draft used. Each format is mapped to a specific beat in the arc.

| Format | Template ref | Arc beat it serves | Primary channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single image (4:5, 1:1) | n/a (house format) | Ordinary World, Reward, Return | Instagram feed, LinkedIn, X |
| Short video, 9:16 (7 to 12 sec) | n/a (house format) | Call to Adventure, Mentor, Trials, Ordeal | Instagram Reels, TikTok |
| Story engagement sequence (4 beats) | story-engagement-sequence template | Ordinary World + Poll warmup | Instagram Stories, Snapchat |
| Soft countdown story (3 beats, no firm date) | countdown-launch-story template (modified: no date countdown sticker, no unconfirmed date) | Early access opening, threshold | Instagram Stories, Snapchat |
| Value carousel (5 slides) | value-carousel template | Method explanation: the small-step system | Instagram feed (4:5), LinkedIn |

Notes on format choices:
- The countdown-launch-story template is used in modified form. The countdown sticker that
  references a firm date is removed, because no confirmed launch date exists. Story 1 uses
  "الدخول المبكر يفتح قريباً" (early access opens soon) framing, not a specific date or day
  count to an unconfirmed event. The poll and keyword DM beats are retained.
- The value carousel addresses the "how does it work" question (streak and small-step system)
  without naming any Skill Path title or lesson lineup.
- All Arabic copy in overlay slots is provided by copywriter-ar. No Arabic text is baked into
  any generated image.

### 1.4 Motifs

- The streak: an unbroken chain of small daily wins. Visual: emerald markers lighting up in
  sequence. Recurring in posts 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 12, 13.
- The small step: one step, not a leap. Verbal motif in every Arabic caption and visual
  overlay. "خطوة كل يوم" and its variations.
- The path: a single thin emerald line that appears in post 1 and completes in post 13.
  Visual continuity across the 14-day arc. Carries over from the June draft.
- Community pull: used in week 2 only, at the Allies beat. Framed as "you are not alone"
  without a specific learner count until a confirmed figure exists.

## 5. Community engagement guidance (Maharat voice)

### 5.1 Voice in replies

Reply in Modern Standard Arabic with Gulf-familiar wording. Match the Thmanyah tone: warm,
intelligent, plain, never stiff or corporate. Short sentences. Active voice. Empowering, never
scolding or dismissive. English replies to English comments follow the same plain empowering
tone, no em dashes.

### 5.2 What to amplify

- Comments and shares from followers who describe a skill they want to build or a habit they
  have started. Engage with warmth and specificity. Example response direction (routes to
  copywriter-ar for final AR copy): acknowledge the goal, affirm the step, reinforce that
  small steps build real skills.
- "Where do I start" questions. Direct to the signup gate link in bio or the story link
  sticker. Do not invent a specific Skill Path or lesson sequence to send them to.
- Saves and shares on the value carousel (cal #05) and the streak visual posts. Thank and
  encourage, do not add new information not already in the approved post.
- Replies that express excitement about early access. Affirm the move, redirect to the signup
  gate, do not promise features, timelines, or content not confirmed in the brief.

### 5.3 What to escalate (do not reply, escalate to the human gate or content lead)

- Questions about price, plans, or specific costs. Escalate. Do not guess or promise. Do not
  say "it is free" unless the free early-access framing has been explicitly confirmed for the
  public reply context.
- Questions about specific Skill Path titles or the content lineup. Do not invent or confirm
  a title. Escalate.
- Questions about the full launch date. Do not commit to a date. Escalate if pressure is high.
  A safe direction is "we will let you know as soon as it is ready."
- Complaints about a current Maharat product, billing, or account access. Escalate to support.
  Do not address in the reply thread.
- Any media or press inquiry. Escalate to pr-comms.
- Any comment that includes personal information (phone number, email) in a public thread.
  Do not engage publicly. Escalate to the data or privacy lead.

### 5.4 What not to say (hard stops in replies)

- No launch date, even approximate ("soon" is fine, "next week" or any date is not, unless
  Ahmed has publicly confirmed it).
- No Skill Path titles, lesson names, or course lineup. None are confirmed for public naming.
- No claims of accreditation. Completion certificates only, not accredited.
- No pricing, discount, or promo framing unless explicitly approved for the reply context.
- No roadmap, unannounced features, or "we are planning to add" language.
- No fundraising, investor, or company financial information.
- No em dashes in any reply, Arabic or English.

### 5.5 Community engagement rhythm

Check and respond within 24 hours of each post publishing. Posts 07, 08, 11, 12, 13 (gate
CTA posts) should have the fastest response window (target under 4 hours for the first 2 hours
after publish) because they drive signups. Prioritize replies that are one interaction away
from clicking the gate link.
