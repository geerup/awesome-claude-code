### Post 1 of 10 . Ordinary World . Mon 2026-06-08

Caption (AR, primary):
كل يوم ينتهي والقائمة نفسها: مهارة تنوي تتعلمها، ووقت لا يكفي.
لكن النية موجودة، وهي البداية.
قريباً، طريقة جديدة تبني مهاراتك خطوة كل يوم.
تابعنا، القصة بدأت.

Caption (EN variant):
Every day ends with the same list: a skill you mean to learn, and never enough time.
But the intent is already there, and that is where it starts.
Soon, a new way to build your skills, one small step a day.
Follow along. The story begins.

Platforms and format: Instagram feed (4:5), X (16:9), LinkedIn (4:5). Single image.

Creative brief (text-free):
- Concept: a single warm emerald point of light in deep near-black space, the first spark.
  One quiet path line begins at the light and trails off into the dark, unfinished.
- Mood: calm, premium, a held breath before a beginning. Generous negative space.
- Palette: #141414 field, #009975 spark and thin path line, #1A1A1A subtle card panel if a
  panel is used.
- Overlay slot (added in build, not generated): the AR headline "القصة بدأت" lower third,
  EN "The story begins" for the EN version.
- Format specs: IG 1080x1350, X 1600x900, LinkedIn 1080x1350. Keep the spark and headline
  inside the safe area, away from platform UI.
