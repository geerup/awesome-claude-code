- angle: "Learn makeup from one of the region's most trusted names, and make the look your
  own." Empowering, never deficit-framed. The hero is the learner who gains a confident skill,

  makeup artist, on a platform built Arabic-first. No lesson count, no duration, no price
  stated until confirmed.

  generically (1, 3, and 12 months exist), no number stated until price is confirmed.
