# Emails: Bassam Fattouh Teaches Makeup, non-payer lifecycle flow (AR/EN)

Draft. Owned by lifecycle-architect, copy by copywriter-ar (AR primary) and copywriter-en (EN).
Nothing sends. Approval-ready drafts that stop at the human gate. No em dashes. Western
numerals. Arabic-first.

## Envelope

- campaign_id: 2026-06-bassam-fattouh-makeup
- stream: lifecycle messaging (7)
- audience: owned non-paying email contacts. Suppress payers, unsubscribed, hard-bounced.
- flow: 4 messages over about 2 weeks, triggered by entry and engagement, not strictly dated.
- gate_platform: OPEN ITEM. Send wiring blocked until confirmed.
- offer note: no price stated until confirmed. Plans referenced generically. No invented
  lesson lineup. Bassam Fattouh named (public course page).

## Flow logic

| Msg | Trigger | Goal | Branch |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | Entry into flow | Introduce the Masterclass, drive a free play | If plays, skip to E3 |
| E2 | No open or no play after E1 | Re-angle on the skill outcome | |
| E3 | Engaged (opened or played) | Move to subscription | If subscribes, exit flow |
| E4 | Not subscribed after E3 | Last call, value recap | Exit |

Personalization uses first name only where available. No sensitive data in any link. Every
email carries an unsubscribe and the sender identity.
