## audiences

- Prospecting: a lookalike modeled on current beauty and design-and-style subscribers (built at platform
  time from the pixel and customer list), plus interest audiences (makeup, beauty, specific look
  interests). Inclusive sub-audiences for the natural-look and modest-beauty segment.
- Retargeting: site visitors, ad engagers, and free-intro chapter viewers who did not subscribe. Highest
  intent, lowest cost to convert. These also feed the lifecycle layer via email capture.
- Exclusions: existing paying subscribers excluded from prospecting and retargeting.

## bid_strategy

- Prospecting: lowest-cost (volume) until the pixel has enough conversion signal, then move to a target
  CPA once Ahmed sets the target (open item). Optimize to the signup or subscription event, not to clicks.
- Retargeting: target CPA or cost cap from the start, given higher intent.
- Optimization event depends on the event plan (data-tracking-engineer) and the gate platform (open item).

## flighting
