- budget: OPEN ITEM. No budget supplied. The plan is an allocation structure only. No spend is

## Media plan structure (allocation, not absolute)

| Channel | Role | Share of budget | Primary objective | Audience |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Meta and Instagram | Primary | 55 percent | Conversions (gate, subscription) | Interest: beauty, makeup, self care. Lookalikes of owned converters. Retargeting of engagers and gate non-completers. |
| TikTok | Secondary | 20 percent | Reach and video views to retargeting | Interest and behavior: beauty, GRWM. Younger skew. |
| Google and YouTube | Secondary | 20 percent | Intent capture and video | Search: makeup tutorial, learn makeup. YouTube in-stream for the trailer. |
| Reserve | Test and scale | 5 percent | Shift to the best performer mid-flight | n/a |

Shares are a proposal against a budget that is not yet set. They become absolute numbers only
after Ahmed confirms the budget. Lookalike and retargeting audiences depend on the gate platform
and consent, and require compliance-privacy-check before they go live.

- bid: start with lowest-cost or maximize conversions, move to cost cap once the gate fires
  enough events. Confirm the target cost per subscription.
- flighting: 3-week flight 2026-06-08 to 2026-06-28 (assumption). Front-load reach in week 1,
  shift to retargeting and the best creative in weeks 2 and 3.
- measurement: conversion events on the gate and subscription. No personal or sensitive data in
  any tracking parameter. Pixel and CAPI mapping owned by data-tracking-engineer, gated.
