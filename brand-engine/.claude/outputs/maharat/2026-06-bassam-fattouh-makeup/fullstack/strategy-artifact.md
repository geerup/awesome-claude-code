## 1. Objective

Grow B2C subscriptions using the Masterclass "Bassam Fattouh Teaches Makeup" as the lead hook.
Acquire new subscribers through paid and organic social, and convert owned non-payers through
lifecycle email and app push. The Masterclass is the hook, not the product sold; the conversion
is to a paid Maharat subscription that unlocks the class.

The strategy reads on a single promise the offer can actually deliver: a confident makeup look
the learner can create themselves, taught by a leading regional makeup artist. That promise is
grounded in real, cleared lessons, so every downstream stream can lean on it without inventing.

## 3. Audience segments

Owned sizes are planning estimates from the brief and resolve to exact figures at send. No size is
invented; where the brief is silent the size is marked OPEN ITEM, not guessed.

### 3.1 Owned non-payers (entry B and app push)

- Definition: registered Maharat contacts who have not purchased a subscription. Email cut is the
  roughly 18,000 non-paying email contacts (planning estimate, exact at send). App cut is owned
  app users who have not purchased; app audience size is OPEN ITEM. Suppress paying contacts,
  unsubscribed, and hard-bounced. Suppression source to confirm.
- Sub-cut where data supports it: a recency split (recently active versus dormant) so lifecycle
  can pace the flow. Segment only on signals the owned data actually carries; if recency is not
  available at send, the flow runs unsplit and that is flagged.
- Pains and gains:
  - Pain: signed up with intent to grow a skill, never crossed into paying; makeup feels like
    something you either have a knack for or you do not.
  - Gain: a structured, do-it-yourself path from a name they recognize, where the first chapter is
    free to try before any commitment.
- Message angle: warm re-entry. You are already here, here is the look you can create this week.
  Lead with the free intro chapter and one concrete, real lesson (for example The No-Makeup Makeup
  or Day to Night) as proof the path is practical. Empowering, never "you stalled."
  Illustrative AR line (not final copy): "اكتشف أسرار المكياج وابدأ بأول درس مجاني اليوم".

### 3.2 New acquisition by interest (entry A paid, entry C organic)

- Definition: new Arabic-speaking adults, roughly 18 to 35, GCC with Saudi Arabia primary, reached
  cold on paid and organic. Interest cuts toward beauty, makeup, self-presentation, special-occasion
  and event looks, and followers of beauty and lifestyle creators. Owned social base for organic is
  the roughly 180,000 followers (planning estimate). Paid interest targeting detail and sizing is
  set by the performance-marketer against this segment definition; exact targeting is theirs to
  build, not invented here.
- Pains and gains:
  - Pain: wants polished looks for daily life and occasions, currently piecing it together from
    scattered tutorials with no structure and no trusted source.
  - Gain: one trusted regional expert and a clear progression from foundations to statement looks,
    on a platform that fits a busy adult's time.
- Message angle: aspiration plus credibility. Learn makeup from one of the region's most
  sought-after makeup artists, and walk away able to do the look yourself. Hero the recognizable,
  real lessons (Foundation 101, Smokey Eyes, Color Glam, Graphic Metallic Look) as the visible
  curriculum. The free intro chapter is the low-friction entry to plays.

### 3.3 Retargeting (entry A paid, reinforced by lifecycle and push)

- Definition: people who engaged but did not subscribe. Includes class-page and plans-page visitors
  who did not purchase, Masterclass intro players who did not convert, gate completers (email or
  WhatsApp captured) who did not buy, and ad and organic engagers. Built from on-site and platform
  signals; exact audience construction and sizing belong to the performance-marketer and tracking
  engineer once tracking is wired. No size invented here.
- Pains and gains:
  - Pain: interested enough to look, held back at the paid step by uncertainty about value or
    whether they can really do it themselves.
  - Gain: a reminder of the concrete payoff and the low-risk entry, the free first chapter, and the
    real lesson list as evidence of depth.
- Message angle: close the loop. Reassure on do-it-yourself feasibility and surface the breadth of
  the 20-chapter class. State only the public price reference (under $7/month billed annually) and
  only where the asset calls for it. No promo claimed unless Ahmed confirms one.

## 4. Core angle and offer framing

### 4.1 Core angle

"A skill you can do yourself, taught by the best." The campaign sells capability, not correction.
The learner is not behind; they are one structured class away from creating looks they are proud of.
Credibility comes from a leading regional makeup artist, named with clearance. Proof comes from the
real, cleared curriculum, not from claims. Empowering throughout, never deficit-framed.

Anchor facts the angle leans on, all cleared:
- Page promise: master your makeup skills and discover the secret techniques of one of the most
  sought-after makeup artists in the region.
- 20 chapters, chapter 1 "The Talent" is a free intro, a genuine low-friction try-before-you-buy.
- Real, nameable lessons: Foundation 101, The No-Makeup Makeup, The Day to Night, The Everyday Glam,
  The Smokey Eyes, The Color Glam, The Graphic Metallic Look, A Career in Makeup, Final Touches.

### 4.2 Offer framing

- The product is a Maharat subscription, with this Masterclass as the hook. Framing leads with what
  the learner can create, then the path, then the access.
- Price: state only the public reference, unlimited access for under $7/month billed annually, and
  only where the asset requires it. Do not foreground price as the message; foreground the payoff.
- Promotion: none stated. Any trial, first-time discount, or bundle is ASSUMPTION until Ahmed
  confirms. No promo invented or implied.
- Accreditation: never implied. Completion certificates are not accredited.
- Free intro chapter is the framing pivot for the top of funnel: try the first lesson, then subscribe
  to keep going.

Illustrative customer-facing direction, not final copy:
- AR: "تعلّم المكياج من أحد أبرز خبراء المنطقة، وأتقن الإطلالة بنفسك".
- EN: "Learn makeup from one of the region's most sought-after artists, and master the look yourself."
