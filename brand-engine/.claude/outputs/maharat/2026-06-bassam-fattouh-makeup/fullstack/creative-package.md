## Concept framing note

The single campaign angle from strategy is: "a skill you can do yourself, taught by the
best." Every concept here traces to that angle. The creative purpose is not to sell features
but to make the learner feel the payoff before they subscribe. The visual language does that
through real lesson names (Foundation 101, No-Makeup Makeup, Day to Night, Smokey Eyes,
Color Glam, Graphic Metallic) as visible curriculum proof, through Bassam Fattouh's presence
as the credibility anchor, and through the free first chapter as the low-friction door.

All concepts:
- Specify visual constants #141414, #1A1A1A, #009975 on every element.
- Are text-free in any generated or AI-assisted image layer. Copy lands in overlay slots only.
- Require rights-cleared Bassam Fattouh assets for any likeness. Generated likeness is a
  hard stop.
- Leave all overlay slots empty and labeled by language (ar, en).
- No em dashes, no invented lineup, no accreditation mark, no invented price or promo.

## Concepts

### C1 . "The Look Starts Here" (anchor hero concept)

**Rationale.** The new-acquisition segment needs immediate aspiration and credibility. A clean,
editorial instructor portrait says "this is the expert" before a word is read. Pairing the
instructor's presence with two or three real lesson names as visible curriculum proof answers
the "can I really do this myself" hesitation without a long argument. This is the highest
anchor concept, the one that runs at the top of paid and organic.

**Visual intent.** Bassam Fattouh, real rights-cleared Maharat asset, centered or two-thirds
dominant on a near-black field. Not styled as a product graphic, styled as a portrait that
signals professional authority, quiet confidence, uncluttered. A thin emerald bar or hairline
frame at the bottom edge of the composition carries the brand accent without flooding it. One
or two real lesson names (for example "Foundation 101" and "Smokey Eyes") appear as small,
clean text chips or ghosted labels in the lower portion of the frame, text-free in the
generated layer, supplied as overlay slots at build time. No busy graphic elements. The field
breathes. The instructor is the statement.

**Fallback if no approved Bassam Fattouh asset is confirmed.** The concept falls to C2 (the
abstract skill concept). Flag as an open item at the gate.

**Channel routing.** Paid (prospecting, retargeting), organic social (feed, Reels opening
card), email header option E1 to E2.

---

### C2 . "One Step, One Technique" (abstract skill concept)

**Rationale.** When no approved instructor asset is available, or as a companion concept on
lifecycle and push channels where familiarity is already established, the creative shifts from
the person to the act of learning. The non-payer segment already knows Maharat; they do not
need more introduction, they need a reason the next step feels small and doable. A single
emergent form, a brushstroke resolving into clarity, mirrors the strategy's empowering
one-step framing. It is also the safest execution because it requires no cleared likeness.

**Visual intent.** Text-free abstract motion or still. A single emerald brushstroke or soft
curved arc draws from the left edge of the near-black field and resolves cleanly, settling
with a faint glow. The #1A1A1A card plane sits behind the stroke as a grounding surface. No
face, no hands, no product packaging. The palette is the message: #141414 field, #009975
stroke, #1A1A1A base. Motion version (7 to 12 seconds): the stroke draws on, holds for two
beats, a subtle pulse on the emerald, then cuts clean to black. Still version: the stroke at
the held moment, with the field well above and below it for overlay headroom.

**Channel routing.** Paid (when C1 assets are unconfirmed), organic Reels and TikTok,
lifecycle email header fallback, app push companion still.

### C3 . "See What the Class Covers" (curriculum proof concept)

**Rationale.** The retargeting segment has already seen the top of funnel and has not
converted. The hesitation the strategy names is uncertainty about depth and do-it-yourself
feasibility. The most direct answer is to show the curriculum in motion: a calm, uncluttered
cascade of the real lesson names as the visual payoff. This does not require instructor
photography and directly addresses the "is this for me" question with concrete chapter names
the learner already recognizes from the class page. Empowering because it shows progression,
not correction.

**Visual intent.** Text-free composition in the generated or motion layer, with lesson names
supplied as overlay slots in build. The visual structure is a vertical or horizontal procession
of #1A1A1A cards on a #141414 field. Each card occupies generous space, one card per lesson
name. The lead card carries an emerald #009975 accent bar at its top or left edge. The
procession is unhurried, no stacking or clutter. In a motion cut (12 to 18 seconds for feed
video), each card enters cleanly and settles, replaced by the next. In a static carousel, each
card is one slide. The final card in both cases points toward the free intro chapter as the
entry invitation. Generated layer is empty of text; all lesson-name chips are overlay slots
labeled [lesson name ar] and [lesson name en] at build time by the designer.

**Lesson names to populate (confirmed, cleared, in fill order based on strategy emphasis).**
Foundation 101, The No-Makeup Makeup, The Day to Night, The Smokey Eyes, The Color Glam,
The Graphic Metallic Look. Additional confirmed chapters (The Everyday Glam, A Career in
Makeup, Final Touches) are available as depth reinforcement slots if the format supports more
cards.

**Channel routing.** Paid retargeting (carousel and video), organic social carousel, lifecycle
email inline module (E3 to E4).

### C4 . "Class Trailer" (video content concept)

**Rationale.** The strategy specifies YouTube as a secondary paid channel and organic social
for top-of-funnel reach and credibility. A class trailer cut answers both. It is the highest
credibility format because it shows the actual instructor teaching, demonstrates the real
curriculum in motion, and delivers the aspiration through footage rather than assertion. This
is the format that most directly earns plays of the free intro chapter, the campaign's
top-of-funnel KPI. It is also the master asset from which shorter paid cuts and Reels cuts
can be repurposed.

**Visual intent.** Footage-led, structured against the class-trailer-structure template.
All on-screen words are overlay slots. Footage is real, rights-cleared Bassam Fattouh class
material only, never generated. If footage is not available, this concept is blocked at the
gate and the campaign runs on C1 through C3 until assets are confirmed.

**Structural direction (adapts the class-trailer-structure template to this class).**

Segment 1 (hook, 0 to 5 seconds): an opening moment from the class footage. A teaching
action, a brush movement, a direct-to-camera line, real instructor energy. No words baked
in. On-screen slot [ar hook line] and [en hook line] overlay in build.

Segment 2 (authority card, 5 to 10 seconds): full-screen #141414 title card. Instructor
name, role or designation. Slots [ar authority line 1] and [en authority line 1]. Use only
facts marked cleared in the instructor profile. Do not invent an award, a figure, or an
accreditation.

Segment 3 (curriculum scroll, 10 to 22 seconds): the real lesson names appear as overlay
text on editorial footage from the class. Slot sequence, one line per beat:
[lesson ar 1 / en 1], [lesson ar 2 / en 2], [lesson ar 3 / en 3], [lesson ar 4 / en 4].
Confirmed lesson names for these slots in priority order: Foundation 101, The No-Makeup Makeup,
The Smokey Eyes, The Graphic Metallic Look. Additional available slots if pacing allows:
The Day to Night, The Color Glam. Emerald accent mark animates between each slot.

Segment 4 (closing line, 22 to 30 seconds): a closing instructor moment from the footage.
The feel is invitation, not pitch. Slot [ar close line] and [en close line].

Segment 5 (end card, 30 to 35 seconds): full-screen #141414 with Maharat brand mark.
Slots [ar end card: Bassam Fattouh teaches makeup, start for free on Maharat] and
[en end card equivalent]. No price, no promo.

**Total target length.** 35 seconds (confirm per paid format requirements; a 15-second cut
for paid pre-roll and a 30-second organic cut can both be repurposed from this master with
the designer using the Blotato workflow when the credential is confirmed, or by manual edit
until then).

**Channel routing.** Organic social (YouTube, Reels, TikTok master cut), paid YouTube and
pre-roll (short cut), organic feed teaser still pulled from segment 3.

## Image and video prompts (text-free, English only)

The prompts below describe the visual layer only. No Arabic text, no baked-on headlines.
All prompts output to the designer for build-ready execution.

### Prompt P1 (for C1 hero, editorial portrait, when instructor asset is available)
Not applicable as a generated prompt. C1 requires a real Bassam Fattouh photograph or class
still sourced from confirmed Maharat rights-cleared assets. The designer sources and frames
the asset per the visual intent above. This slot is blocked until asset availability is
confirmed.

### Prompt P2 (for C2 abstract brushstroke, still version)
A premium minimalist abstract composition. Near-black background, hex #141414, fills the
entire frame with generous empty space above and below center. At the center of the frame,
a single clean curved brushstroke in deep emerald, hex #009975, arc from the left quarter
to the right three-quarter mark of the frame, slightly upward and then level, as if drawn
by a confident hand with a wide brush. The stroke has soft organic texture at its edges, not
a hard vector line, but settled, unhurried. A flat near-black card plane in #1A1A1A sits
behind the stroke, occupying the middle third of the frame vertically, giving a subtle
elevation. No face. No product. No text. No numbers. High-end studio quality. No watermark.
Aspect ratio 4:5 for this still.

### Prompt P3 (for C2 abstract brushstroke, motion brief)
Motion brief for the designer. Begin with a fully dark #141414 field. At frame 12 the emerald
stroke (same geometry as P2) begins to draw from left to right in 1.2 seconds, as if painted
in real time. The stroke settles, then at frame 90 a gentle soft-glow pulse on the emerald
intensifies for 0.4 seconds and fades. The frame holds for 1.5 seconds. Then a clean cut to
#141414 black. Total duration 7 seconds. No text baked in. Overlay slots applied in post.
Output format 1080x1920 for Reels and Story. Also output 1080x1080 square.

### Prompt P4 (for C3 curriculum cards, still version)
A premium, calm composition on a near-black #141414 background. Three or four rectangular
cards in #1A1A1A, each occupying distinct generous space, arranged vertically with even
breathing room between them. The top card has a thin emerald #009975 horizontal bar at its
very top edge, acting as a color accent. All other cards are plain #1A1A1A. Each card interior
is empty, clean space for text overlay at build time. No text baked into the image. No
product imagery. No faces. Generous padding. Quiet, premium, editorial. Aspect ratio 4:5.

### Prompt P5 (for C3 curriculum cards, carousel slide base)
A single rectangle card in #1A1A1A centered on a #141414 field. Left edge of the card has
a thin vertical emerald #009975 stripe, approximately 4 pixels wide. Card occupies the center
70 percent of the frame both horizontally and vertically. Card interior is entirely empty, for
text overlay at build. No text. No face. No product. Square 1080x1080.

### Prompt P6 (for C4 trailer, end card matte)
A full-frame flat matte in near-black #141414. A small, clean emerald #009975 horizontal
accent bar occupies the lower fifth of the frame, slim, about 6 pixels tall, spanning
full width. The Maharat wordmark or brand mark is centered in the upper two-thirds. Overlay
slots for end card copy will be applied at build. No text baked in. 1080x1920 for Reels.
Also 1920x1080 for YouTube. No watermark.
