## 1. Keyword Research

This section documents the keyword map per store and per language. It is desk research grounded
in the confirmed class content, audience definition, and store mechanics. It is not yet validated
against live search volume or ranking data; that validation waits on ASO tool approval.

### 1.1 Research rationale

The campaign angle is capability: a skill you can do yourself, taught by a leading regional
makeup artist. The learner is searching for structured makeup learning, not scattered tutorials.
They trust a named regional expert. They are on a subscription platform. Store search intent
clusters in three zones:

- Direct class or instructor intent: people who have heard of Bassam Fattouh or the class and
  search by name. This is a relatively small but high-intent cluster, and the instructor name is
  cleared for use.
- Makeup learning intent: people searching for how to learn makeup, apply foundation, do smokey
  eyes, or build a routine. This is the largest cluster and maps directly to the confirmed lesson
  titles.
- Platform and self-development intent: people searching for Arabic self-development apps,
  online classes in Arabic, or skill-building apps in the GCC. This is Maharat's broader category
  anchor.

Arabic-first throughout. English variants serve bilingual GCC users and EN-locale store listings.

### 1.4 Store field and character limits

App Store (iOS):
- Title: 30 characters max. Shared across all locales; the primary locale (AR) drives the title
  choice, with EN in the EN locale.
- Subtitle: 30 characters max per locale.
- Keyword field: 100 characters per locale, comma-separated, no spaces after commas, no
  duplicates between title, subtitle, and keyword field.
- Description: 4,000 characters max. Not directly indexed by the App Store algorithm but visible
  to users and affects click-through.
- Long description is not surfaced to users in the same way; prioritize title, subtitle, and
  keyword field for indexing.

Google Play:
- Title: 30 characters max.
- Short description: 80 characters max. Indexed.
- Long description: 4,000 characters max. Indexed by Google Play algorithm; keyword placement
  in the first 167 characters is highest-weight.
- No separate keyword field. Keywords must appear naturally in title, short description, and
  long description.

All character counts apply to rendered text, not source bytes. Arabic characters are multi-byte
but each counts as 1 character in these limits.

## 4. Store A/B Experiment Plan

Experiments are planned and prepped here. They do not run until the human gate approves and
store console access is confirmed. Each experiment is tied to the campaign strategy angle and
linked to the success metrics from the strategy artifact.

### 4.1 Experiment 1: Subtitle test (App Store, AR locale)

- Variable tested: subtitle copy angle.
- Variant A (control): platform-wide subtitle. Direction: "دروس مكياج ومهارات يومية" (makeup
  lessons and everyday skills). Keeps the platform broad.
- Variant B (treatment): instructor-led angle. Direction: "تعلم مع بسام فتوح" (Learn with
  Bassam Fattouh). Uses the named instructor as the pull signal.
- Hypothesis: during the campaign flight, the named-instructor variant will lift the conversion
  rate from store page view to install among users with beauty and makeup intent, because the
  instructor name is a known credibility signal in the GCC target market.
- Success measure: install conversion rate (store page views to downloads) in the AR locale,
  compared across variants. Linked to the primary success metric: subscription conversion. An
  install uplift with no downstream subscription uplift is a signal, not a win.
- Duration: minimum 7 days of statistically equivalent traffic before reading results.
  Recommend running through at least half the campaign flight.
- Gate: App Store Connect product page optimization. Requires console access confirmed.

### 4.2 Experiment 2: Short description test (Google Play, AR locale)

- Variable tested: the lead sentence in the Google Play short description.
- Variant A (control): platform-led. Direction: something in the register of "تطبيق ماهارات
  للتعلم والتطور" (Maharat app for learning and growth).
- Variant B (treatment): class-led with free intro hook. Direction: something in the register
  of "تعلم المكياج مع بسام فتوح، الدرس الأول مجاني" (Learn makeup with Bassam Fattouh, first
  lesson free).
- Hypothesis: surfacing the named instructor and the free intro chapter in the first line of
  the short description will increase install conversion rate from the Google Play listing among
  users in the makeup-interest segment during the campaign flight.
- Success measure: install conversion rate on Google Play AR locale. Same downstream link to
  subscription conversion.
- Gate: Google Play Console listing experiments. Requires console access confirmed.

### 4.3 Experiment 3: Screenshot frame 1 visual test (App Store, both locales)

- Variable tested: the hero visual in screenshot frame 1.
- Variant A: instructor-portrait frame (requires confirmed rights-cleared Bassam Fattouh
  portrait). If imagery is not confirmed, this variant cannot run; the experiment collapses to
  one variant and is dropped.
- Variant B: lesson-grid frame (a clean card layout showing 4 confirmed lesson names from the
  curriculum, no portrait).
- Hypothesis: the instructor portrait will outperform the lesson-grid for high-intent users who
  recognize the name, but the lesson-grid may outperform for cold traffic who respond to
  structured curriculum proof.
- Success measure: tap-through rate from search results to the store listing page (impression
  to page view). Linked to install rate and downstream subscription conversion.
- Dependency: requires confirmed rights-cleared imagery. OPEN ITEM. If assets are not confirmed,
  this experiment is deferred and flagged to the human gate.

## 5. Reviews Response Policy

### 5.1 Principles

The reviews response policy is grounded in the Maharat voice: clear, modern, confident, and
empowering. Every public response is customer-facing and must pass brand-qa-reviewer before it
is used. No response template goes live without that gate.

Tone rules:
- Thank the reviewer by the gesture, not by their username or personal data.
- Empowering, never defensive. Acknowledge feedback and move to what the app or team can do.
- No roadmap in responses. Do not promise features, timelines, or content that has not been
  announced.
- No accreditation implication. If a user asks whether the completion certificate is accredited,
  the response states clearly that it is a Maharat completion certificate and does not imply
  external accreditation.
- No invented content. Do not name upcoming classes, Skill Paths, or instructors not yet
  confirmed public.
- No pricing commitments in reviews. If a user asks about price, direct them to the plans page
  (maharat.com/en/plans or maharat.com/ar/plans). Do not confirm or deny any promotion.
- No em dashes. No tatweel. Western numerals.

### 5.2 Response routing

Positive review (4 to 5 stars):
- Respond warmly and briefly. Acknowledge what they valued (learning, the instructor, a specific
  class). Invite them to explore more.
- Escalate if: the review contains a public claim about the platform that is incorrect (for
  example an accreditation claim). Do not correct publicly without legal or comms review.

Negative review (1 to 3 stars), technical issue:
- Acknowledge the issue plainly. Direct to support (support channel to be confirmed; do not
  invent a URL). State that the team is looking into it.
- Escalate if: the issue is a billing, payment, or subscription error. Route to Ahmed or the
  relevant team owner before responding publicly.

Negative review, content or instructor feedback:
- Acknowledge the feedback respectfully. Do not argue.
- Do not promise changes to the curriculum or new content.
- Do not name Bassam Fattouh negatively in responses; never quote or amplify a negative claim
  about the instructor publicly.
- Escalate to Ahmed if: the review alleges factual errors in the class content or makes a
  public complaint about the instructor.

Negative review, pricing or value:
- Acknowledge the perspective. Direct to the plans page. Do not confirm or deny any promotion
  or discount in a public response.
- Escalate if: the review references a specific pricing commitment that does not match the
  public reference (less than $7/month, billed annually).

Spam or abusive reviews:
- Do not engage. Flag the review for platform reporting. Escalate to Ahmed.

### 5.3 What never to say in a public review response

- Any implication that the completion certificate is accredited or officially recognized by an
  external body.
- Any mention of unannounced Skill Paths, upcoming classes, or instructors not yet publicly
  confirmed.
- Any specific promotion, discount, or trial not yet publicly confirmed by Ahmed.
- Any roadmap item or product timeline.
- Any personal or sensitive data about the reviewer.
- No em dashes, no tatweel, Western numerals only.

### 5.4 Escalation threshold

Route to Ahmed for a decision before responding publicly when:
- A review makes a claim (accreditation, pricing, content accuracy) that could create legal or
  reputational exposure if the response is wrong.
- A review mentions the instructor by name in a negative or misleading context.
- A review alleges a billing or subscription error.
- A review has a high helpfulness score or is featured prominently in the listing (high
  visibility means the stakes of the response are higher).
