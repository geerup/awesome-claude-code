## 1. Content plan

### 1.1 Strategic angle (inherited from strategy-artifact.md)

"A skill you can do yourself, taught by the best." The campaign sells capability, not
correction. The learner is not behind. They are one structured class away from looks they are
proud of. Credibility comes from a leading regional makeup artist, named with clearance.
Proof comes from the real, cleared 20-chapter curriculum. Empowering throughout, never
deficit-framed.

Organic social carries the top of funnel for the new-acquisition segment (section 3.2 of the
strategy). It introduces the Masterclass, heroes the free intro chapter and recognizable lesson
names, builds retargeting pools, and routes the most ready directly to the signup gate.

## 5. Community engagement guidance

### 5.1 Voice and register

All replies and community interactions use the Maharat brand voice: clear, modern, intelligent,
empowering, never stiff or corporate. Tone benchmark is Thmanyah. Replies are Arabic-first where
the audience member wrote in Arabic. English replies where the member wrote in English. No
mixing unless the context calls for it naturally.

Short, warm, confident. One idea per reply. Active voice. No hype. No sales pressure.

### 5.2 What to amplify (prioritise for likes, replies, and re-shares)

- Comments and replies that express curiosity or aspiration: "where do I start," "I have always
  wanted to learn this," "which lesson first." Reply with a direct, warm pointer to the free
  intro chapter. Example register (not final copy): "Chapter 1 is the free intro, start there,
  the rest follows naturally."
- Comments that ask about specific lessons by name (Smokey Eyes, Foundation 101, etc.). Reply
  naming the lesson and confirming it is in the class. Do not invent lesson content.
- Positive reactions to Bassam Fattouh's name or reputation. Acknowledge warmly, keep it brief.
- Saves and shares of the myth-buster and skills roundup carousels. Amplify by liking and
  replying to any comment on those posts.

### 5.3 What to escalate (do not guess, route up)

- Pricing questions, subscription questions, billing questions. Reply: "We will get back to you
  shortly" and route to the team. Do not state a price in a public comment. The public reference
  (under $7/month billed annually) is the only price in scope, and only if confirmed for public
  use in replies, which is an open item for Ahmed.
- Complaints about the platform, app errors, or account issues. Route to support. Do not
  troubleshoot publicly.
- Questions about certificate accreditation, qualification recognition, or professional
  certification. Do not engage publicly. Route to the team. Never confirm or imply accreditation.
- Questions about upcoming classes, other instructors, or the product roadmap. Reply: "Stay
  tuned for more" and nothing else. No roadmap, no unannounced plans.
- Negative or hostile comments about Bassam Fattouh personally. Do not engage. Route to Ahmed.
- Any press inquiry, brand partnership inquiry, or media request. Route immediately to pr-comms.

### 5.4 What not to say (hard stops in community replies)

- No price quotes beyond the confirmed public reference, and only if Ahmed confirms it is safe
  to state in replies.
- No lesson count, course duration, or module detail beyond the confirmed public lineup.
- No accreditation language of any kind.
- No fundraising, no roadmap, no unannounced plans, no release dates for other classes.
- No personal or sensitive data in any reply or DM, and no tracking parameters in links shared
  in replies.
- No em dashes, no tatweel, Western numerals only.
- Do not confirm whether a discount or promotion exists unless Ahmed has confirmed one. If asked,
  reply: "Check the plans page at maharat.com/en/plans for current pricing."

### 5.5 Engagement timing

Reply to all direct questions within 4 hours during the campaign flight, prioritising the 24
hours after each post goes live when engagement velocity is highest. Stories replies and DMs
triggered by the keyword mechanic (ORG-07) require the comment-to-DM automation to be set up
and approved first (gated tool decision, open item).
