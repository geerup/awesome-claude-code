## 1. Channel mix

### 1.1 Meta and Instagram (primary prospecting and retargeting)

Role: primary acquisition and retargeting engine.

Rationale: the strategy-artifact identifies the core acquisition segment as Arabic-speaking adults
roughly 18 to 35 in the GCC with Saudi Arabia primary, with interest cuts toward beauty, makeup,
self-presentation, and special-occasion looks. Meta and Instagram carry the densest concentration
of this audience in the GCC, offer interest and lookalike targeting in Arabic, and support the
video, carousel, and story formats that suit the step-by-step visual nature of a makeup class.
The free intro chapter is a natural hook for a short-form video creative that can run in-feed and
in Stories without asking for commitment upfront. Retargeting layer closes the loop on site
visitors and gate non-completers.

### 1.2 TikTok (secondary reach and video seeding)

Role: upper-funnel reach and video play volume; feeds the retargeting pool.

Rationale: the strategy-artifact notes a beauty and GRWM (get ready with me) interest cluster
that skews toward a younger segment of the 18 to 35 range. TikTok's in-feed video format is a
natural match for short makeup lesson clips and before/after creative. The primary value here is
building awareness and video-play retargeting audiences. It is not the conversion engine; Meta
holds that role. TikTok creative should be short, vertical, and lead with visual outcome before
any platform or subscription mention. The free intro chapter clip is the best candidate for this
format.

### 1.3 Google Search (secondary intent capture)

Role: capture high-intent queries at the moment of active search.

Rationale: the strategy-artifact names intent around learning makeup and the instructor's name as
a signal this channel can intercept. Search is not the volume channel for this campaign; it is
the precision channel. People actively searching "learn makeup," "makeup course Arabic," or
"Bassam Fattouh Maharat" are already in a decision frame. A well-structured keyword set here
converts at a lower volume but at a higher rate than cold social. Budget allocation is kept
modest relative to Meta because the search volume for Arabic-language makeup courses in the GCC,
while real, is narrower than the interest-based social audience.

### 1.4 YouTube (secondary reach and trailer placement)

Role: mid-funnel video for warm and semi-warm audiences; reinforces the class as a credible,
premium product.

Rationale: YouTube in-stream runs the Masterclass trailer (if a rights-cleared asset is
confirmed) or a brand cut against beauty, self-development, and Arabic-content audiences.
YouTube's audience in the GCC is large and skews toward longer video consumption, which suits a
class-trailer format better than TikTok. This channel is most efficient in the second half of
the flight, after Meta and TikTok have seeded awareness and built retargeting pools that YouTube
can reinforce.

YouTube placement is conditional on the rights-cleared trailer asset being confirmed (OPEN ITEM,
brief section 7). If no cleared asset exists at launch, the YouTube allocation shifts to the
reserve until one is available.

### 1.5 Reserve

Role: mid-flight reallocation to the best-performing channel.

Rationale: a standing reserve allows the plan to respond to early performance signals without
requiring a new budget approval. The reserve is not pre-assigned to any channel; it goes to
whichever channel is delivering the lowest cost per gate completion or subscription conversion at
the end of week 1.

The brief carries no confirmed budget. All figures below are proportional. Absolute amounts are
applied by paid-build-engineer only after Ahmed confirms the budget. The total across all

| Channel | Phase 1 (week 1, reach and seeding) | Phase 2 (weeks 2 to 3, convert and retarget) | Overall share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meta and Instagram | Higher weight, prospecting-heavy | Shifts to retargeting and best creative | 50 percent |
| TikTok | Higher weight, reach and video seeding | Reduced, retargeting only | 20 percent |
| Google Search | Steady | Steady or slight increase on intent volume | 15 percent |

| Reserve | Held | Released to best performer at end of week 1 | 5 percent |

Notes on the split:

- Meta at 50 percent reflects its role as the primary conversion engine, its audience density in

- TikTok at 20 percent is seeding and awareness; it is not asked to convert directly.

- YouTube at 10 percent is conditional on the trailer asset. If the asset is not confirmed before
  launch, this 10 percent rolls into the reserve until the asset is available, and the reserve
  becomes 15 percent temporarily.

## 3. Audience strategy

No personal data is included in any audience definition below. All definitions are segment
descriptors for platform targeting construction. Exact audience sizing on each platform is
subject to confirmation via read-access platform tools, which are documented here but not yet
wired or approved. Consent basis for retargeting and lookalike audiences requires
compliance-privacy-check before any audience goes live.

### 3.1 Meta and Instagram

Prospecting audiences:

- Interest cluster 1 (beauty and makeup core): interests in makeup artistry, beauty tutorials,
  cosmetics, skincare, beauty brands. Saudi Arabia and GCC geo. Age 18 to 35. Arabic-language
  settings.
- Interest cluster 2 (self-presentation and occasions): interests in weddings, events,
  special-occasion looks, fashion, and personal styling. Same geo and age band.
- Interest cluster 3 (self-development adjacent): interests in online learning, self-improvement,
  personal development. Same geo and age band. Use this as a secondary or overlap exclusion
  test.
- Broad advantage-plus audience: let Meta's algorithm optimize broadly within the geo and age
  parameters once the gate and subscription events have enough signal. Activate after the first
  50 gate conversions.

Lookalike audiences:

- Lookalike of existing Maharat subscribers or converters (1 percent similarity, Saudi Arabia geo).
  Source audience requires consent compliance review before construction.
- Lookalike of gate completers (email or WhatsApp captured) once the gate is live and has
  accumulated enough seeds (minimum 100, ideally 1,000). This is the highest-value lookalike for
  the conversion objective.

Retargeting audiences:

- Class page visitors (maharat.com class page URL) in the last 30 days who did not complete the
  gate. Requires pixel firing confirmed by data-tracking-engineer.
- Plans page visitors (maharat.com/en/plans and /ar/plans) in the last 30 days who did not
  subscribe.
- Video viewers: 25 percent and 75 percent video view thresholds on Meta video ads and organic
  posts. Segment separately for the message angle; 75 percent viewers get the closer/retargeting
  concept, 25 percent viewers get the mid-funnel value concept.
- Gate completers who did not subscribe: captured via the gate platform pixel or CRM integration
  once the gate platform is confirmed (OPEN ITEM). This segment receives Concept D (retargeting,
  gate non-completers) from 03-paid-ads.ar-en.md.

Exclusions:

- Current paying subscribers (suppress from all acquisition campaigns).
- Existing gate completers who have already subscribed (suppress from retargeting).
- Frequency cap: 3 impressions per user per day across all Meta placements to limit negative
  feedback rate.

### 3.2 TikTok

Prospecting audiences:

- Interest cluster: beauty, makeup, GRWM content, skincare, cosmetics. GCC geo, Saudi Arabia
  primary. Age 18 to 30 (tighter age band than Meta, reflecting TikTok's user distribution).
- Creator/hashtag affinity: users who engage with Arabic beauty content and makeup tutorial
  hashtags. Use TikTok's interest and behavior targeting to reach this cluster.

Retargeting audiences:

- Video engagers (watched 50 percent or more of a TikTok ad or organic post): retarget with a
  short, outcome-focused creative that names the free intro chapter. Keep retargeting creative
  under 15 seconds.

Exclusions:

- Current subscribers (match via hashed email list once the gate platform is confirmed and
  consent basis is clear). Compliance-privacy-check required.

### 3.3 Google Search

Keyword groups (for construction by paid-build-engineer, final set to be reviewed by
seo-specialist):

- Brand keywords: "Bassam Fattouh," "Bassam Fattouh Maharat," "بسام فتوح مهارات." Bidding
  priority: high. These queries signal explicit intent and should have the tightest match types
  and the strongest CTAs.
- Class-specific keywords: "learn makeup online," "makeup course Arabic," "تعلم المكياج،"
  "دورة مكياج." Match types: phrase and exact.
- Category keywords: "makeup tutorial," "makeup artist course," "online beauty course,"
  "مدرسة مكياج،" "فن المكياج." Match types: phrase. Monitor for irrelevant traffic and add
  negatives quickly.
- Intent keywords: "how to do makeup," "makeup for beginners," "كيف أتعلم المكياج." Use as
  an observation layer first; promote if cost per click and conversion rate support it.

Audience overlays: apply an in-market audience overlay for online education and self-improvement
to improve Quality Score and bid efficiency.

Negative keywords: exclude queries related to makeup products, brushes, cosmetics retail, and
makeup for hire (to avoid serving ads to people shopping for products or professional services
rather than learning).

### 3.4 YouTube

Audience targeting:

- In-market: online education, self-development.
- Interest: beauty, makeup tutorials, lifestyle.
- Keyword targeting: YouTube channels and videos in the beauty tutorial space (Arabic language
  preference).
- Remarketing (YouTube): users who watched the Maharat class page trailer or visited the Maharat
  channel. Requires data-tracking-engineer to confirm YouTube remarketing tag placement.

## 4. Bid strategy

No target CPA has been confirmed in the brief (OPEN ITEM). The bid strategy below is structured
to work without a hard CPA target in the initial phase and to transition to a cost-cap structure
once enough conversion events accumulate. The hard CPA target is filled in by paid-build-engineer
as a campaign setting only after Ahmed confirms the number.

### 4.1 Phase 1, week 1 (learning and seeding)

Objective: generate gate events (email or WhatsApp capture) and class-page visits. This phase
prioritizes learning-phase completion over CPA, which is not yet reliable without data.

- Meta and Instagram: Maximize conversions (gate completion event). Do not set a cost cap yet.
  Allow the algorithm to spend freely to collect conversion signals and exit the learning phase.
  Meta's learning phase requires roughly 50 optimization events per ad set per week; set ad set
  budgets accordingly once the absolute budget is known.
- TikTok: Maximize video views in week 1 for the seeding phase. Switch to reach objective if
  CPV rises above acceptable levels. No conversion bid until the TikTok pixel has accumulated
  events.
- Google Search: Maximize clicks on the brand and class-specific keyword groups. Transition to
  Target CPA once the campaign has enough conversion data (generally 30 or more conversions in a
  30-day window).
- YouTube: CPV (cost per view) bidding. Awareness objective. No conversion bid on YouTube in
  phase 1.

### 4.2 Phase 2, weeks 2 to 3 (conversion push)

Objective: drive subscriptions. Retargeting and best-performing prospecting creative push toward
subscription conversion.

- Meta and Instagram: Introduce a cost cap per gate completion once the gate has fired at least
  50 events. Fill in the cap value using the Ahmed-confirmed CPA target. If CPA is not yet
  confirmed, maintain maximize-conversions bid and flag the missing target as an open item in
  the performance readout.
- TikTok: Shift to maximize conversions against gate or subscription event if pixel volume
  supports it. If volume is insufficient, maintain video views and retargeting reach.
- Google Search: Target CPA if 30-plus conversions available; otherwise remain on maximize
  clicks and flag.
- YouTube: Maintain CPV; shift budget from YouTube to Meta if the trailer asset is not confirmed
  or if YouTube CPV is high relative to Meta performance.

### 4.3 Reserve release (end of week 1)

After the first performance readout (end of week 1), release the 5 percent reserve to the
channel with the lowest cost per gate completion. This reallocation is a proposal to the human
gate, not an autonomous spend action. The analytics-reporter performance readout triggers this
proposal.

## 5. Flighting

Assumed window: 2026-06-08 to 2026-06-28. Three weeks. Start date and end date are ASSUMPTION;
confirm with Ahmed. If the window shifts, the phase proportions below apply to the confirmed
window, not to fixed calendar dates.

### Week 1: 2026-06-08 to 2026-06-14 (reach and seeding)

Objective: build awareness, seed retargeting pools, collect enough conversion events to exit
Meta's learning phase, and validate creative.

- Meta and Instagram: launch all 4 concepts from 03-paid-ads.ar-en.md as distinct ad sets.
  Prospecting-heavy. Free intro chapter hook is the lead creative. CTA is "Sign Up" or "Learn
  More" leading to the class page, not directly to the plans page (let the page do the sell
  first).
- TikTok: launch the short-form video creative (Concept B or a purpose-cut clip) with
  video-view objective. No conversion bid yet.
- Google Search: launch brand and class-specific keyword groups. Monitor daily for irrelevant
  traffic and add negatives.
- YouTube: launch trailer (if confirmed) or hold YouTube budget in reserve until the asset is
  cleared.
- Retargeting: not yet large enough to activate in week 1 (pools need 1 to 2 days minimum).
  Activate retargeting ad sets from day 3 onward, as audiences build.

Pacing: spend evenly across the week. Do not front-load day 1, as learning-phase algorithms
respond poorly to high early spend before event signal accumulates.

### Week 2: 2026-06-15 to 2026-06-21 (optimize and convert)

Objective: shift spend toward the best-performing creative and audiences; activate retargeting
at scale; introduce conversion bid where event volume supports it.

- Meta and Instagram: pause the lowest-performing ad set from week 1 (based on cost per gate
  completion). Scale the top 2 performers. Activate lookalike audiences if seed size is
  sufficient. Retargeting ad sets (Concept D) are now active and spending.
- TikTok: evaluate CPV from week 1. If video completion rate and retargeting pool are sufficient,
  shift some TikTok budget to retargeting. If not, maintain video-view objective.
- Google Search: evaluate conversion volume. If 30-plus conversions recorded, test Target CPA.
  Otherwise maintain maximize clicks.
- YouTube: increase weight if trailer asset is confirmed and CPV is efficient. Otherwise hold or
  redirect to Meta.
- Reserve release: release the 5 percent reserve to the best-performing channel, subject to
  human gate approval of this optimization proposal.

### Week 3: 2026-06-22 to 2026-06-28 (close and retarget)

Objective: maximize subscription conversions from warm audiences; do not expand to new cold
audiences unless the reserve has not been deployed and week 2 performance supports it.

- Meta and Instagram: conversion-focused. Retargeting is the primary mode. Class page and plans
  page visitors get the subscription value message (Concept C). Gate non-completers get Concept D.
  25 percent video viewers who have not converted get a mid-funnel concept.
- TikTok: retargeting only in week 3. Pause cold prospecting on TikTok unless week 2 data shows
  strong video-to-gate conversion from the platform.
- Google Search: maintain. Add any new negative keywords surfaced in weeks 1 and 2.
- YouTube: wind down or hold at week 2 budget level. No scaling in week 3.
- Frequency watch: check Meta frequency metrics daily in week 3. If frequency exceeds 5 per user
  per week, reduce ad set budgets and refresh creative rather than burning the audience.

## 6. In-flight optimization triggers (proposals through the human gate)

All optimization proposals below are triggered by analytics-reporter performance readouts. None
are autonomous actions. Each is a proposal that waits at the human gate before any budget or bid
change is executed.

### Trigger 1: Creative fatigue

Signal: CTR on the top creative drops by 30 percent or more week-over-week, or frequency
exceeds 5 per user per week on Meta.
Proposed action: rotate in the next-ranked creative concept from 03-paid-ads.ar-en.md. If all 4
concepts are in rotation and CTR is still falling, flag to creative-director for a new asset
before extending the flight.

### Trigger 2: Learning phase stuck on Meta

Signal: the Meta ad set has not exited the learning phase by day 7 (fewer than 50 optimization
events in 7 days).
Proposed action: consolidate ad sets to concentrate event volume. Reduce the number of active
ad sets and combine audiences that are close in definition. Alternatively, broaden the
optimization event to a higher-funnel action (video view or class page visit) until gate events
accumulate. Both options go to the human gate before implementation.

### Trigger 3: TikTok underperformance

Signal: TikTok CPV is more than 2x the Meta CPM-adjusted equivalent, or the TikTok retargeting
pool after week 1 is too small to activate (fewer than 1,000 users).
Proposed action: pause TikTok prospecting and reallocate the TikTok share to Meta. Keep TikTok
retargeting running if the pool is large enough. Proposal goes to the human gate.

### Trigger 4: Reserve release decision

Signal: end of week 1 performance readout from analytics-reporter.
Proposed action: release the 5 percent reserve to the channel with the lowest cost per gate
completion. The release is a one-sentence budget proposal with the destination channel and
justification, routed to the human gate.

### Trigger 5: CPA exceeded (once target is confirmed)

Signal: actual cost per subscription is more than 20 percent above the Ahmed-confirmed target
CPA for two consecutive days.
Proposed action: reduce budget on the offending channel by 20 to 30 percent and redirect to the
best-performing channel. Alternatively, tighten the audience (remove the broadest interest
cluster) and reduce bid cap. Specific move depends on the readout at that point.

### Trigger 6: CPA undercut (efficiency opportunity)

Signal: actual cost per subscription is more than 20 percent below the Ahmed-confirmed target
CPA.
Proposed action: increase spend on the overperforming channel within the confirmed budget ceiling.
Do not exceed the confirmed total budget without a new approval.

### Trigger 7: YouTube asset not confirmed by launch

Signal: rights-cleared trailer asset is not available by 2026-06-08.
Proposed action: hold YouTube budget in the reserve. Do not run YouTube with uncleared or
generated creative. Surface to the human gate for a decision on whether to wait for the asset
or fold the YouTube share into Meta.
