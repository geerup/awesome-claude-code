## The angle (one idea, tuned per segment)

Learn makeup the right way, step by step, from one of the region's most recognized artists. Looks you
can actually do yourself, from a natural no-makeup look to full glam. Then keep learning on Maharat.
Empowering, never deficit-framed. Beauty as confidence, never correction. Feminine address.

Segments (from the strategy-artifact): 1 beauty-beginners-everyday, 2 advancing-enthusiasts,
3 natural-look-and-modest-beauty (inclusive, veiled women and mature skin), 4 beauty-subscriber-lookalike
(paid seed), 5 warm-retargeting-and-free-intro-viewers.
