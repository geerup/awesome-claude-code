indicators (free-intro plays, landing signups and signup rate, CTR, cost per landing signup) from the

the human gate. This plan, and that checkpoint, never pause an ad set, never shift a budget, and never
swap a live creative. A human decides; a human acts. Spend beyond the 10,000 total is never proposed.
