### CONCEPT A: "The Craft Surface"

**Concept ID:** concept-craft-surface

**Serves angle:** Learn makeup the right way, step by step, from one of the region's most
recognized artists. Looks you can actually do yourself.

**Visual description.** The frame holds a flat, dark premium surface, near-black #141414,
with makeup tools and materials arranged with deliberate, generous space. Brushes, pigment
pots, a single open compact, small paint-like strokes of blended color, a palette corner.
The emerald accent #009975 appears as a single tonal element: a swatch, a label edge, a
compact face plate, a single gemstone-toned eyeshadow pod. No instructor. No hands. Pure
craft objects in a premium editorial still-life register. Think a high-end beauty editorial
spread, not a product catalogue. Card surface areas #1A1A1A read as elevated platforms or
shadow separators. Lighting is controlled, directional, soft-top with a rim of warm amber
to separate objects from the near-black ground.

**Motion language.** Slow reveal: the camera drifts infinitesimally across the surface or
a brush rotates one degree per second. Nothing quick. Nothing energetic. Quiet authority.

**Typography note.** No type in the image. Copy overlaid in build: RTL Arabic as the primary
layer, then the English variant. The emerald accent in the visual echoes the Maharat brand
color and creates a natural anchor point for the headline overlay block.

**Rationale.** A still-life of craft objects signals mastery without showing a person, does
not require the instructor's likeness, and is scalable across every channel. The visual
language says "this is serious craft" for advancing-enthusiasts and beauty-beginners alike.

**Channel routing.** Paid (Meta feed, TikTok, YouTube pre-roll), organic (Instagram feed,
blog hero), lifecycle (email header), WhatsApp/app visual.

### CONCEPT B: "The Technique Hands"

**Concept ID:** concept-technique-hands

**Serves angle:** Looks you can actually do yourself, from a natural no-makeup look to full
glam. Confidence through technique.

**Visual description.** An extreme close-up of a hand holding a professional makeup brush,
applying or blending product on a skin surface. The person is anonymous: no face, no
identifiable features, no jewelry that could identify anyone. The hand holds the brush with
a light, practiced grip. The brush bristles are mid-motion, catching soft directional light
from the upper left. The skin surface shows a clean, luminous finish, not a product-heavy
finish. Background falls to near-black #141414. The emerald accent #009975 appears as a
subtle tonal note: a brush ferrule, a faint rim of light, or a product swatch in the
bottom-right periphery. Card surface #1A1A1A appears as a secondary surface or shadow area.
No faces. No recognizable people. Anonymous hands only.

**Motion language.** A slow, controlled brush stroke, 2 to 3 seconds looped. The hand
enters from the right edge and the bristles pull from right to left across skin, suggesting
Arabic-native reading direction.

**Typography note.** No type in the image. Copy overlaid in build. The upper third of the
frame is kept fully clear for the Arabic headline overlay. The lower fifth is cleared for
the CTA strip.

**Rationale.** An anonymous technique close-up shows the skill being practiced without
requiring any specific person, sidesteps the rights question entirely, and speaks directly
to the segment wanting to learn what the hand is doing. The hand motion from right to left
is culturally resonant for the RTL-primary audience.

**Channel routing.** Paid (Meta Stories, Reels), organic (Instagram Reels, TikTok), paid
(YouTube pre-roll), organic (Stories), lifecycle (email header alternate), WhatsApp visual.

### V-01: Brush Reveal, 5 to 7 seconds (Reels and TikTok)

**Prompt ID:** prompt-motion-brush-reveal
**From concept:** concept-craft-surface
**Channel:** paid and organic (Reels, TikTok)

**Shot description.** The camera starts in a macro close-up of brush bristles, slightly out
of focus, on a near-black surface. Over 5 seconds, the camera pulls back very slowly (a
controlled dolly backward) until three brushes of different sizes come into full sharp focus,
arranged in a fan on the near-black surface. The emerald ferrule accent point on the largest
brush catches the key light at the end of the pull, creating a single brief light flare in
#009975 tone.
