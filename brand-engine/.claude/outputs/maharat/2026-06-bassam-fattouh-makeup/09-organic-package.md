# organic-package: Bassam Fattouh Teaches Makeup

Produced by organic-social from the strategy-artifact and the QA-passed copy and creative. Reasoning for the
plan; posting is a gated action behind the human gate. Zero media cost. Entry point C: organic distribution
across the owned accounts (about 180,000 followers as a planning estimate) as an acquisition path that feeds
the free intro and the signup gate, then lifecycle.

## content_plan (themes mapped to the angle and segments)

- The free first step: clip the free intro moment, route to watch it. (beginners, retargeting)
- One look, demystified: a single technique from a chapter (no-makeup-makeup, foundation for veiled women,
  everyday glam in 15 minutes), shown step by step. (natural and modest, beginners)
- Range reel: a fast montage of the looks the class teaches. (advancing)
- The artist: a short, respectful framing of 25 plus years of craft from a real supplied asset. (lookalike, trust)

## repurposing_notes

- One source (the class trailer and chapter footage) becomes many: Reels, Stories, a carousel, and Shorts.
  Tool choice for repurposing (for example Blotato, already adopted) runs gated; Arabic captions are real text,
  never baked into a generated image. A human design check stays on any asset carrying a Bassam likeness.

## publish_on_approval
