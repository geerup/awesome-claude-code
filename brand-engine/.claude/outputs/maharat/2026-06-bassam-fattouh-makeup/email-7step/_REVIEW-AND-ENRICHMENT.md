## Part 1: review of the current sequence

Verdict: the 7 emails are clean, on-voice, and house-style correct, but they are thin. They
promote the class without ever teaching anything, so they read as seven variations of the same
"learn makeup from a pro" line. A non-payer who already watched the free chapter has seen the
promise. To move them to subscribe, the sequence has to show the substance behind it. The value
is sitting in the brief and the class and the emails do not use it.

| Email | Current state | The gap |
|---|---|---|
| E1 Welcome | "Makeup is a skill you can learn." | No hook beyond the generic promise. Does not use the cleared "his first-ever online class" framing or the scope (everyday to full glam). |
| E2 Why this skill | "He shares his secrets, practical and clear." | Asserts value without a single concrete example. The strongest social angle (skill, not a pile of products) is unused. |
| E3 What you will learn | A generic 5-line list (foundations, colors, application, finishing touch, any occasion). | The single biggest miss. The brief confirms 20 real lessons and the class teaches specific, nameable techniques. None appear. This email should carry the curriculum. |
| E4 Free first lesson | "Start the first lesson free," unnamed. | The brief confirms chapter 1 is "The Talent," a free intro. Naming it makes the email concrete and the ask smaller. |
| E5 Credibility | "One of the most sought-after artists," repeated. | Same authority line as E2 and the page. Adds nothing new. Could show he teaches mindset and confidence, not only technique. |
| E6 Momentum | "Soon you will create your own look." | Generic. Does not anchor to a real outcome (a specific look, time and money saved). |
| E7 Subscribe | "Choose the plan on the page," vague close. | Tightened to a value close (full class plus the growing library) and a clear plans-page CTA. No price stated, per the 2026-06-19 decision. |

### 2c. Real teaching specifics from the class (use as "what you learn", do not over-claim)

Each is a true teaching moment in the class, tied to its lesson. Use them to make the curriculum
concrete. Frame as what the learner will be able to do, not as a secret revealed in the email.

- Prep the skin before makeup: a 3-step routine, cleanse, moisturize, sunscreen (Look Flawless).
  The class line is that good skincare is the base of flawless makeup.
- Build your kit: the few products and tools that actually matter, and how to care for the brushes
  (Your Makeup Kit Essentials).
- Choose color by you: your skin undertone and eye color guide the eyeshadow and lipstick that
  suit you (Your Color Palette).
- A timeless concealer technique and a clean base (Foundation 101, The No-Makeup Makeup). The
  class teaches the technique; the email names that it exists, it does not reveal it.
- Small pro habits, for example applying under-eye product with the ring finger (No-Makeup Makeup).
- The looks, by name: the no-makeup makeup, day to night, everyday glam, the smokey eyes, the
  color glam, the graphic metallic look, finished with the final touches.
- Mindset: the class includes "Anxiety Is Your Friend," confidence is taught alongside technique.
  The class voice is "do not be afraid," beauty as confidence, never correction.

Off-limits from the same source: the playful "lipstick is like a facelift" line reads as
appearance-correction, which the brand voice forbids (beauty as confidence, not correction). Do
not use it. No verbatim quotes attributed to Bassam from internal docs; paraphrase the philosophy.

## Part 3: the rewrite plan, email by email

The 7-step arc holds. Each email now carries one real, specific piece of value.

- E1 Welcome: announce his first-ever online class, everyday looks to full glam, 20 lessons. Invite
  to start. One clear idea: the artist you could never book now teaches you, step by step.
- E2 Why this skill: the skill-not-products angle. You learn the method, not just steps, and skip
  years of trial and error. Concrete, not just "secrets."
- E3 What you will learn: the curriculum email. Lead with the real arc: prep and a clean base,
  choosing color for you, the no-makeup makeup, day to night, everyday glam, the smokey eyes, the
  color glam, the graphic metallic look, the final touches. A real list, drawn from the 20 lessons.
- E4 Free first lesson: name it. Start with "The Talent," chapter 1, free. One lesson to feel the
  approach before any commitment.
- E5 Credibility and mindset: a leading regional name teaching his first online class himself, step
  by step, no gatekeeping, and he teaches confidence too (the class includes a lesson on exactly
  that). Authority plus reassurance.
- E6 Momentum: the everyday payoff. A look you do yourself for any occasion, the no-makeup makeup
  for the day, a smokey eye for the evening, time and money saved, confidence that stays.
- E7 Subscribe: the value close, the full class plus the growing library, choose the plan on the
  plans page. No price or figure stated (2026-06-19 decision).
