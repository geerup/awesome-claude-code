### angle

Core message: learn makeup the right way, step by step, from one of the region's most recognized
artists, looks you can actually do yourself, from a natural no-makeup look to full glam. Then keep
learning on Maharat.

Rationale:
- Empowering, never deficit-framed. It leads with what the learner can create and become, in their
  own hands, not with anything they lack. Beauty as confidence, never correction (Bassam voice pack).
- True to the offer and fully evidenced. The hook is a premium masterclass from a recognized artist,
  with 25 plus years of craft, a real 20-chapter lineup, and a free intro chapter anyone can watch.
  The draw is the artist and the craft, both confirmed on the live page.
- Range is the differentiator. One class spans no-makeup-makeup, everyday glam, smokey, color, and
  editorial, plus inclusive guidance for veiled women and mature skin. The single idea (do it
  yourself, the right way) tunes per segment without fragmenting.
- It does not overclaim. The lineup, duration, and free chapter are stated only as published. No
  accreditation, no career-outcome promise (technique and confidence only), no client or brand names.

Per-segment tuning (one core idea):
- beginners: lead with the everyday look they can do themselves, the free intro as the easy first step.
- advancing: lead with range and pro technique (smokey, color, editorial).
- natural and modest beauty: lead with looks that fit how they want to present, taught with respect.
- lookalike (paid): the sharpest value framing, recognized artist plus all-access subscription.
- retargeting and free-intro viewers: answer the hesitation, point back to the value already sampled.

Illustrative customer-facing lines (direction only, not final copy):
- "تعلّمي المكياج خطوة بخطوة، من أحد أشهر خبراء التجميل في المنطقة. إطلالات تصنعينها بنفسك."
- EN direction: "Learn makeup the right way, step by step. Looks you can actually do yourself."

### offer_framing

- The hook: the masterclass "Bassam Fattouh Teaches Makeup", named exactly as published, from a
  recognized regional artist, design and style category. The artist, the craft, and the range are the draw.
- The proof: a real free intro chapter ("The Talent", 2:47) anyone can watch before deciding. This is
  the lowest-friction entry and the strongest single proof point.
- The value: 20 chapters, 2h 53m of step-by-step technique, spanning natural to glam to editorial, with
  inclusive guidance. Framed as skills the learner creates with, not a gap she fixes.
- The path to paid: the masterclass is the entry into a Maharat subscription, all classes for one price.
  Public list value (a confirmed public fact, may be referenced): unlimited access for less than
  $7/month, billed annually. Any campaign-specific promotion is an open item; none is invented.
- Competitive alternative (what the reader does today): free social tutorials (scattered, uneven, rarely
  from a recognized regional authority), another beauty or generic learning app, or nothing.
  - Beats free social: one trusted, coherent method from a leading artist, versus guessing across clips.
  - Beats another app: recognized regional expertise on an Arabic-first platform, one subscription across skills.
  - Beats nothing: turns a wish into a skill the learner owns and reuses, on her own terms.
- Guardrails in the frame: no accreditation claim, the real title and published lineup only, instructor
  named only because the published page confirms him (subject to the catalog confirmation at the gate).

### channel_plan

- Paid (primary): Meta and Instagram (Reels and Stories first, beauty is visual and the audience is

- SEO and content: capture makeup-learning and how-to intent in Arabic and English, an editorial
