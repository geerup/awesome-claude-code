## Strategy draft (overall, email-focused)

- Objective: first Maharat subscription from owned non-payers, with the makeup masterclass as the
  hook. This is the owned-list complement to the full-funnel paid strategy in
  `../01-strategy-artifact.md`.
- Hook and angle: learn makeup the right way, step by step, from one of the region's most
  recognized artists, looks you can do yourself, from a natural look to full glam. Beauty as
  confidence, never correction (Bassam voice pack).
- Proof and entry: the free intro chapter "The Talent" is the lowest-friction proof. Every email
  can point a hesitant lead to watch it before deciding.
- Offer (confirmed public facts only): the masterclass is the entry to a Maharat subscription,
  unlimited access to every class for less than 7 dollars a month, billed annually. No
  campaign-specific promotion is invented.
- Promise discipline: technique and confidence only, never professional-career outcomes. No client
  or brand names. The real title "Bassam Fattouh Teaches Makeup" only. No accreditation.
- Success metric: first subscription attributed to the flow. Leading indicators: free-intro plays,
  click-through, signup rate. Target and dates are a brief ASSUMPTION, not invented here.

## Ideal leads from the list (audience)

Per `segmentation-logic` (the team's six-list architecture), the flow audience is the owned
non-payers with a makeup or beauty fit:

Audience size resolves from live Ortto data at send. No size invented.

## The sequence: NON-PAYER pattern, 4 messages (the correct number)

Per `templates/sequence-standards.md`, an owned non-payer toward a first subscription is the
NON-PAYER pattern: 4 to 5 messages (entry, value, offer, branch on engagement, optional last call).
This flow is the 4-message core; a 5th last-call is added only if the brief defines a real window
and reason (none invented).

| # | id | Trigger | Intent | One CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | msg-1-entry | enters non-payer flow | What you can build: do your own makeup, step by step. Point to the free intro. | Watch chapter 1 free |
| 2 | msg-2-value | 2 to 3 days after msg-1 | Concrete value: the range and inclusivity (natural to glam, mature skin, veiled women). | See what you will learn |
| 3 | msg-3-offer | 2 to 3 days after msg-2 | Offer plain: the subscription, every class, under 7 dollars a month billed annually. | Start now |
| 4 | msg-4-branch | after msg-3, branch on engagement | Openers and clickers: nudge to the offer. Non-openers: subject-line retry, not a louder pitch. | Watch chapter 1 free |

- Cadence: every 2 to 3 days, morning local send (about 8 AM to 10 AM), inside the brief send
  window. Never daily. Cadence and window are brief inputs; the values above are the standard band,
  to confirm.
- Behavior layer: time-based foundation above, with triggers on top (a lead who watches the free
  intro can branch early to msg-3 offer).
- Single-email anatomy (every message): subject 20 to 60 characters, front-loaded; preheader 40 to
  90 characters that extends the subject; exactly one primary CTA; consented personalization only,
  never personal data in a URL.
