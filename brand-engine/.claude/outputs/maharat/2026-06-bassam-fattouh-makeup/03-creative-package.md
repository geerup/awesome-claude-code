## prompts[] (text-free, no Arabic baked in, no generated likeness)

- For C1 and C3, no generative image of Bassam or any person is produced. The visual is composed from a
  real supplied asset (portrait or class still) on the brand surface. Generative prompts are limited to
  abstract or product-adjacent backgrounds only (texture, light, surface), never a face or a look.
- Background prompt (text-free, abstract only): "near-black premium surface #141414, soft single emerald
  #009975 light accent, generous negative space, no text, no people". Copy is overlaid later in build.
- Generative tool choice runs through build-vs-buy-eval; Arabic capability is the decisive filter; no tool
  is adopted without approval. Any asset carrying a face or a look is a real supplied asset, not generated.
