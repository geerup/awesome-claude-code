# Visual briefs: Bassam Fattouh Teaches Makeup

Draft. Owned by creative-director, execution by designer, then design-qa and brand-qa-reviewer.
Nothing publishes. No em dashes. Western numerals. Arabic-first.

## Rules that bind every asset here

- Brand visual constants: background near-black #141414, card surfaces #1A1A1A, primary accent
  emerald #009975. Premium, uncluttered, generous space, accent as a highlight not a flood.
- No Arabic text baked into any generated image. All headlines are build-time overlay slots,
  added in Canva or Figma by the designer. This is because generative tools mangle Arabic
  script.
- Any portrait, likeness, or class footage of Bassam Fattouh must be a real, rights-cleared
  Maharat asset. Never generate his likeness. If no approved asset exists, fall back to the
  abstract or product-still concepts (V2, V3) and flag the gap.
- Western numerals only in any rendered text. RTL must render correctly for Arabic overlays.
- No accreditation marks or implied-accreditation visuals.

## Asset set

### V1 . Hero, the instructor

- Use: paid Concept A and D, social posts 1, 3, 5, 8, email header option.
- Source: real rights-cleared Maharat photography or video still of Bassam Fattouh. Not
  generated.
- Composition: Bassam Fattouh framed cleanly against a near-black field, emerald accent as a
  thin frame or lower bar. Premium, editorial, uncluttered.
- Overlay slots (build): AR headline (for example "بسام فتوح يعلّمك المكياج"), EN equivalent,
  optional CTA chip in emerald. Keep overlays inside the platform safe area.
- Formats: IG 1080x1350, Story and Reels 1080x1920, X 1600x900, LinkedIn 1080x1350.

### V2 . Abstract, the skill

- Use: paid Concept B, social posts 2, 6, when no instructor asset is available.
- Concept: text-free, premium abstraction of "method and confidence". For example a single
  emerald brushstroke forming a clean arc across the near-black field, or soft emerald light
  describing the shape of a face without depicting a real person. No real likeness.
- Motion (if video, 7 to 12 seconds): the stroke draws on, settles, soft glow, brand mark.
- Palette: #141414 field, #009975 stroke, #1A1A1A base plane.
- Overlay slots (build): AR and EN headline, lower third.
- Formats: 1080x1920 video, 1080x1350 feed.

### V3 . Library and value

- Use: paid Concept C, social posts 4, 7.
- Concept: text-free motif of "one step opens many". A single emerald card on near-black opens
  into a calm grid of #1A1A1A cards, suggesting a growing library without naming any title.
  Do not render specific course titles or invented lineup.
- Motion (if video): one card expands into the grid, emerald accent ripples across, holds.
- Palette: #141414 field, #1A1A1A cards, #009975 accent on the lead card.
- Overlay slots (build): AR and EN headline, optional CTA chip.
- Formats: 1080x1350 feed and carousel cards, 1080x1920 video, 1600x900 X.

### V4 . Email header (optional)

- Use: emails E1 to E4.
- Concept: a slim, premium header. Either a cropped real Bassam Fattouh asset (V1 source) or
  the V2 abstract stroke. Text-free, headline overlaid in the email build.
- Palette: brand constants. Keep it light, email loads fast.
- Formats: responsive header, about 1200x480, with a mobile-safe crop.
