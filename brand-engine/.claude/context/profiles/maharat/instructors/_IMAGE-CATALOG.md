# Instructor image catalog (the approved-image registry)

One row per real, rights-cleared instructor image. This is the executable form of the rule that
campaigns use real author images and never a generated likeness (context/instructors/README.md,
the briefs, and outputs/.../05-visual-prompts.md "HARD RULE: NO GENERATED LIKENESS"). A designer
or agent resolving a "REAL ASSET REQUIRED" slot filters this catalog by slug, placement, and
faces_present, and pulls the Drive id. Generated images never get a row here, so "pull from the
catalog" can never return a generated likeness. That is the enforcement, not a restated rule.

Google Drive is the source of record. This file carries ids and metadata only. Bytes are pulled
on demand by `scripts/drive_image_sync.py` into the gitignored cache `assets/instructors/<slug>/`,
which is empty on a fresh clone and never committed. The slug to folder map is `_IMAGE-FOLDER-MAP.md`.
The deterministic gate is `scripts/image_catalog_check.py <slug>`.

## How to use it (findability)

Filter the rows. The common query for a paid hero is
`slug=<slug> AND faces_present=yes AND orientation=portrait`, preferring `retouched=yes` and
`preferred=yes`. For Bassam the `15052024_BassamBG_141414` set is the hero candidate: the
instructor already on the brand near-black #141414. Per-placement picks live in each
`<slug>/assets.md` working sheet.

Placement taxonomy, mapped from the aspect ratios in the 05-visual-prompts library Part 4:

| Aspect ratio | orientation | typical image_type | example placement |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1:1 | square | headshot | feed, in-app card |
| 4:5 | portrait | portrait | paid hero (Meta feed) |
| 9:16 | portrait | portrait, b-roll | Stories, Reels, TikTok |
| 16:9 | landscape | class-still | YouTube thumbnail, blog hero |
| 2:1 | landscape | class-still | email header |

`suggested_placements` is computed from orientation + image_type + faces_present. A row may only
fill an instructor-likeness slot when `faces_present: yes`.

## Schema (closed vocabularies)

- image_type: portrait, headshot, half-body, class-still, cover, lifestyle, product-detail, b-roll
- orientation: portrait, landscape, square (or "auto, verify" / "unverified" until confirmed)
- retouched, preferred, faces_present: yes, no (or "auto, verify" / "unverified" until confirmed)
- preferred: exactly one yes per stem group (the prefer-retouched dedup). The retouched twin wins;
  its superseded siblings carry the winner's fileId in superseded_by.
- rights_status: human-confirmed (approved vs internal-only). Seeded "confirm with Ahmed", never
  invented. faces_present and orientation are "auto, verify" until a human or a vision read confirms.

## Open items (surface at the human gate)

- Google Drive MCP adoption into `settings.json` `enabledMcpjsonServers` is a build-vs-buy plus
  Ahmed decision, the same gate as Higgsfield. NOT done here. The enumeration that seeded this
  catalog ran read-only in an MCP session and was cached to `scripts/cache/drive-enum-<slug>.json`.
  `.mcp.json` and `settings.json` were deliberately NOT modified.
- Rights status per image (approved for marketing vs internal-only) is confirmed by a human, not
  invented. Every row is seeded `rights_status: confirm with Ahmed`.
- faces_present and orientation: the Drive `read_file_content` vision read returned empty for these
  image files this session, so face and orientation are "auto, verify" (unverified). A human or a
  working vision read sets them before a row fills an instructor-likeness slot.
- BANK OF STILLS is a secondary source. Default: catalog PHOTOGRAPHY, record the BANK OF STILLS id
  in `_IMAGE-FOLDER-MAP.md` for later. Cataloguing BANK OF STILLS for all classes is a quick confirm.
- Very large originals (a Bassam .tif is 165 MB) are catalogued but `fetch` skips them by default
  and pulls the jpg/png preferred variant instead.
- Mona Ataya has NO retouched photography set. Her `02 - PHOTOGRAPHY` holds HIGHRES (CR3 camera-raw,
  mimeType application/octet-stream, not image/* and not web-usable, so NOT catalogued) and LOWRES
  (195 jpg selects, retouched=no). Her catalog rows are the LOWRES jpgs, and her hero pick comes from
  LOWRES until a retouched or on-brand #141414 export exists. Flag for Ahmed.
- Cedric Haddad's `RETOUCHED` folder holds only two subfolders: ROMARIO (clean retoucher pass) and
  FACEAPP (AI FaceApp-processed twins of the same frames). The FACEAPP AI-edited likenesses are
  deliberately NOT catalogued (the registry is real photography only, no generated or AI-edited
  likeness ever gets a row). Only the 5 clean ROMARIO frames are catalogued. Because the shared
  `stem_key` strips trailing 1-3 digit suffixes, those 5 distinct frame numbers collapse into one
  stem group, so the gate flags one as preferred and the other four as superseded by it; the four are
  distinct usable frames, pull any by fileId.
