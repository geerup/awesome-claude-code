## Bio in two lines

Serial entrepreneur and one of the region's most recognized businesswomen: co-founder of
Bayt.com (2000) and founder and CEO of Mumzworld (2011), the leading mother-and-child
e-commerce platform in MENA. Palestinian-Lebanese, based in the UAE, built her companies
while raising 3 children.
