# 01-company-brief: who Maharat is (stable facts)

Source of truth for company facts. Stable, not campaign data. If a campaign needs a number
that is not here and not in the brief, stop and ask. Do not invent it.

## The company

Maharat is an Arabic-first, gamified self-development platform. The goal is to be the go-to
platform for self-development and upskilling in the Arab world.

## Formats

- Masterclasses: premium video, regional experts.
- Skill Paths: gamified, bite-sized, Duolingo-like. Built but not yet launched. Do not
  invent titles or the content lineup, and do not announce a launch.
- PDF guides.
- Completion certificates. Not accredited. Never imply accreditation.

## Audience

Arabic-speaking adults, roughly 18 to 35. Core market the GCC, primary market Saudi Arabia.

## Money model

- B2C subscriptions are the engine. Freemium, with an email or WhatsApp gate.
- Plans: 1, 3, and 12 months. Prices and promotions are per-campaign inputs, never assumed.
- Also B2B and B2G, sponsorships, CSR and grants.

## Owned audience (approximate, for sizing and segmentation)

- About 23,000 email contacts.
- Of those, about 18,000 are non-paying. This is the first-build audience.
- About 5,000 paying: about 4,000 single-class buyers, about 1,000 subscribers.
- About 180,000 social followers.

Treat these as approximate planning figures. The exact resolved audience size for a send
comes from the live data at send time and is recorded in the lifecycle package.

## What not to claim

- No accredited certificates.
- No invented Skill Path titles, no instructor names without confirmation.
- No fundraising, roadmap, or unannounced plans in customer-facing output.
