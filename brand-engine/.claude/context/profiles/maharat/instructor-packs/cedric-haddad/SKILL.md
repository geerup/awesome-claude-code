# Marketing Cedric Haddad

## Segments
1. Women rebuilding everyday style. Angle: real women, real wardrobes, his words. Hooks: empathy, direct promise.
2. Dress-for-the-moment buyers (interviews, weddings, new role). Angle: confidence engineered through style. Hooks: pain-question.
