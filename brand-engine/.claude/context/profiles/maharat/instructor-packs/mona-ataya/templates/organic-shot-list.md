# Instructor shoot shot-list template (stream 3, organic)

Source pattern: [Mona Ataya Shoot Shot List](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Sr7kGkxAosvUuq2DiZp2Ic8kZPOPcW5L8rrXxH9WfLE/edit),
extracted 2026-06-04. This is the reusable shape of an instructor organic shoot for any
roster instructor. Creative-director uses it to brief shoots and to know what footage
exists when concepting (the categories below are what a filmed instructor folder
typically contains).

## The three categories

### 1. Hero shots (the premium, cinematic layer)
- Slow-mo walk onto set from behind
- Close-up flipping through script pages; over-shoulder script review
- Macro of pen underlining key words in notes
- Slow-mo wardrobe adjust (blazer, scarf)
- Wide from behind, framed in set lights (silhouette)
- Side profile mid-explanation, hands gesturing; close-ups of hands on a point
- Natural close-up smile after finishing a line
- Slow pan around the instructor while speaking

### 2. BTS and relatable (the human layer)
- Mic being clipped on; makeup final touches
- Slate clap with the instructor's name
- Candid laugh between takes
- Adjusting jewelry or glasses before filming
- Reset moment: sipping coffee, reviewing notes
- Walking onto set, to camera or looking around

### 3. Filler and detail (the edit glue)
- Studio lights coming up from black
- Empty chair with script, glasses, pen (improvise with set props)
- Focus pull from blurred notes to a sharp key phrase
- Set pan shots

## How campaigns use this

- Hero: paid ads, trailers, premium announcement posts.
- BTS: organic social, lifecycle emails (the relatable beat), stories.
- Filler: transitions in any edit.

When briefing a new shoot, start from this list and add instructor-specific shots tied
to their domain (an entrepreneur with a pitch deck, a musician at an instrument). When
concepting from existing footage, confirm with the team which categories were actually
filmed and where the footage lives; do not assume every listed shot exists.
