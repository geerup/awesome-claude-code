# Marketing Mona Ataya (the product skill)

Read first: `context/instructors/_CATALOG.md` (status), `context/instructors/mona-ataya.md`
(facts, the claims table is binding), `voice.md` (register and hooks), the active brief

## What the product is

An entrepreneurship masterclass plus themed playlist videos. Confirmed playlist themes:
confidence, time management, leadership, communication, risk-taking, problem-solving,
mentorship, inspiration, discipline. The class promises (from the one-liners): idea to
investor-ready plan, validation and value proposition, sustainable scaling, funding and
pitching, team building, navigating fundraising bias as a woman founder. Class title and
lesson list: to confirm, never invented.

## Segments and angles

Three segments the content itself supports, each with its angle and proof:

1. Aspiring founders (has an idea, has not started). Angle: from idea to a plan
   investors take seriously. Proof: Bayt.com and Mumzworld track record (claims 1, 2).
   Hooks: pain-question and direct promise.
2. Women founders and working mothers. Angle: she did it while raising 3 children, and
   teaches how to navigate the bias. Proof: claims 2, 5, 8, 9. Hooks: empathy opener,
   provocation reframe, credential drop. This segment is unique to her on the roster;
   when a campaign can only feature one angle, this is the differentiator.
3. Stuck small-business owners (running, not growing). Angle: scale without breaking
   what you built, to the point of a real exit. Proof: over 50M raised, Tamer Group
   acquisition (claims 3, 10). Hooks: stat shock (verified rows only), myth-flip.

## What the class delivers vs what copy may promise

Copy promises learning and tools, never outcomes. Fine: "تتعلم كيف تبني خطة عمل تقنع
المستثمرين". Not fine: implying the learner will raise money, get acquired, or succeed.
Empowering framing, no deficit framing of the learner, no accreditation implication.
