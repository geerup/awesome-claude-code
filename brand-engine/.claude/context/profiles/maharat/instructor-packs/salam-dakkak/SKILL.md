# Marketing Salam Dakkak
Read first: catalog, context/instructors/salam-dakkak.md, voice.md, the brief.

## Segments
1. Home cooks. Angle: restaurant-level dishes from your own kitchen, 20 plus recipes. Hooks: direct promise, sensory.
2. Heritage lovers. Angle: the dishes that carry a story, cooked right. Hooks: identity, nostalgia.
3. Aspiring chefs (especially women). Angle: learn from the region's most recognized female chef. Hooks: credential, empowerment.

## Promise discipline
Promise recipes, technique, and confidence in the kitchen; never restaurant success or career outcomes. Use the Best Female Chef line as Maharat's listing phrases it; do not attribute it to a specific award body until row 4 is confirmed.

## Stream routing
- 4 copy: recipe-count and credential lines per voice.md.
- 3 creative: food is the most visual product on the roster; brief dish-forward creative, confirm footage inventory.
- 7 lifecycle: a recipe-teaser arc (one dish story per email) into the class offer.

## Blockers
Official class title and URL to confirm. Row 4 specifics held back. Content docs not yet located in the folder. Catalog status pending confirmation despite public listing.
