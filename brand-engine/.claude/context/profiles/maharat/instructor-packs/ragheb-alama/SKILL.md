# Marketing Ragheb Alama
Read first: catalog, context/instructors/ragheb-alama.md, voice.md, the brief.

## Segments
1. Aspiring singers and musicians. Angle: learn performance from 40 plus years on stage. Hooks: credential, direct promise.
2. Songwriters. Angle: the craft behind hits (hooks, maqam choice, dialect). Lead magnet: Songwriting Starter Pack.
3. Curious listeners. Angle: understand the soul of the music you love. Lead magnet: Maqamat or Genre Map.

## Promise discipline
Teach craft and understanding; never promise fame, hits, or a career. Lead magnets are the conversion path: cheatsheet, then class CTA (the docs already end with it).

## Stream routing
- 6 conversion: the three cheatsheets are ready gate assets for the email or WhatsApp signup.
- 4 copy: pull from cheatsheet language (already bilingual) via voice.md, fixing numerals and dashes.
- 7 lifecycle: a 3-email value arc maps to the three cheatsheets, then the class offer.
