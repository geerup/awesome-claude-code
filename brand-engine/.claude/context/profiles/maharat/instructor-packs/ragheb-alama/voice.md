# Ragheb Alama: voice
Source: the cheatsheets (educational register, already close to MSA) plus public persona.

## How he sounds
Warm authority. A master explaining simply: short definitions, concrete song examples, encouragement over gatekeeping. The cheatsheets teach "the soul of Arabic music", and that framing (soul, feeling, craft) is his lane.

## What he would not say
No mocking other genres or artists, no become-famous promises, no technical gatekeeping.
