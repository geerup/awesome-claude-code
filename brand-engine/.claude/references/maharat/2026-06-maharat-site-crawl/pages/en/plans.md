## Frequently Asked Questions

General

### What is Maharat?

![](https://www.maharat.com/img/down-arrow.svg)

Maharat is a premium online learning platform reimagining education in the Arab world. Built by Arabs, for Arabs. We offers immersive, chaptered classes from top industry experts and playlists for bite-sized, focused learning. Whether you want to dive deep or learn on the go, Maharat is made for curious minds of all ages and professions.

### How is Maharat different from other online learning platforms?

![](https://www.maharat.com/img/down-arrow.svg)

Maharat aspires to provide the best online classes in the Arab World. We bring the most successful Arab minds, provide the best possible production quality, and develop highly engaging curriculums to give our users the greatest classes possible. Our goal is to educate, entertain, and inspire the Arab World.

### What is included in a 6/12 Maharat subscription?

![](https://www.maharat.com/img/down-arrow.svg)

A Maharat membership gives you unlimited access for 6 or 12 months to everything on the platform - including current and upcoming classes, curated playlists, and regularly updated bonus content.
You’ll also receive exclusive downloadable PDFs and resources for each class, along with the chance to participate in special giveaways - including the opportunity to meet your favorite instructors!

### What is included in a Maharat single-class purchase?

![](https://www.maharat.com/img/down-arrow.svg)

A single-class purchase gives you lifetime access to that class only, perfect if you want to explore one specific topic. If you’d like access to all classes, playlists, bonus content and more, you can upgrade anytime to a Maharat membership.

### Why should I join Maharat?

![](https://www.maharat.com/img/down-arrow.svg)

Because learning should feel inspiring, not overwhelming. Maharat gives you access to powerful knowledge, taught by leading Arab voices, in a format that fits your pace and your purpose. Whether you want to grow in your career, gain personal confidence, or explore new ideas, Maharat helps you do it through immersive classes and fast, focused playlists.

### Where and how can I watch Maharat?

![](https://www.maharat.com/img/down-arrow.svg)

Anywhere! You can watch Maharat classes on your web browser using any device, including your laptop, phone, or tablet. You can also download the Maharat app on iPhone and Android for a smoother on-the-go experience. If you prefer a bigger screen, you can use Chromecast or AirPlay to watch on your Smart TV.

Maharat Playlists

### What are Maharat Playlists?

![](https://www.maharat.com/img/down-arrow.svg)

A Playlist is a collection of short, curated lessons pulled from Maharat classes. Each Playlist focuses on one theme or topic and is designed for quick, focused learning. Perfect when you're short on time but want valuable insights.

### Are Playlists included in my membership?

![](https://www.maharat.com/img/down-arrow.svg)

Yes. All Playlists are included with any 6 or 12-month Maharat membership, along with full access to all classes, bonus content, and workbooks.

### What’s the difference between a Playlist and a Class?

![](https://www.maharat.com/img/down-arrow.svg)

Classes are full-length, structured courses made up of several chapters and hours of content. Playlists are shorter and more flexible, made for quick wins and focused learning, using curated clips from existing classes.
