## Note on dedup method

Distinct is judged by subject line (the representative is the most recent send of that subject).
Audience variants that share a subject are assumed to share content and are collapsed. If a
specific audience variant is needed in full, its asset id is listed in the "Other sends" column
and can be pulled individually with `get_asset_html`.
