# benchmarks/: the engine against the field

This folder benchmarks the Maharat Marketing Engine against authoritative, real-world
templates and frameworks pulled from the web. One doc per domain. Each doc reviews outside
sources, lists what best-in-class templates include, maps our coverage, names the gaps with a
priority, notes where our discipline is stronger, and ends with concrete recommendations.

These are comparison and recommendation documents. They do not change any skill, template, or
SOP. Applying a recommendation is a separate, reviewed step, approved by Ahmed, exactly like
any other change to the engine.

No em dashes, no tatweel, Western numerals, English-first awareness, in every doc here.

## How to read a recommendation

Each recommendation names the specific skill or template file to change, the concrete element
to add or adjust, the priority (High, Medium, Low), and the source that backs it. A
recommendation never overrides a brand guardrail: where an outside template conflicts with the
Maharat rules (English-first, no em dashes, no invented values, the human gate), the rule wins
and the doc says so.

## What this pass deliberately preserves

The benchmark respects what makes the engine the engine: campaign-agnostic briefs, no invented
offers or titles, English-first voice, the binary quality gates, and the human gate before any
send, publish, or spend. Outside templates are mined for structure and completeness, not for
permission to drop a guardrail.
