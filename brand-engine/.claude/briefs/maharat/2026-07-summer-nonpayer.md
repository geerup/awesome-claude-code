A full-stack summer campaign for Maharat generally, with the roughly 18,000 non-paying email
contacts as the primary target and new acquisition running alongside. The hook is the breadth

## 2. Background and context

- why_now: Summer is a high-intent window for self-development in the GCC: longer free hours,
  travel and downtime, a natural "use this season to build something" moment. Maharat has a
  large owned base of non-payers who registered with intent but never crossed into paying. A
  seasonal, roster-led push is a low-cost way to re-engage them and to acquire new subscribers
  while interest is high.
- business_problem: About 18,000 owned contacts signed up and never subscribed. They know the
  brand, they have not paid. The campaign exists to convert and re-engage them, and to grow
  B2C subscriptions overall across the summer.
- prior_results: none as a dedicated summer campaign. Related prior work exists as drafts (the
  non-payer email flow brief 2026-06-nonpayer-email, and per-instructor runs). No past numbers
  invented; stream 9 has no prior report-artifact to inherit yet.
