# Template: Teaser hype video (counter and reveal)

A reusable structure for a short, high-energy teaser that builds anticipation with a rising
counter and stacked stats, then a withheld reveal and a CTA. Extracted and normalized from the
Maharat organic copy doc. Campaign-agnostic scaffold for creative-director and the designer.
Structure and direction only. On-screen words are overlay slots filled by copywriter-ar (AR
primary) and copywriter-en. Arabic is never baked into a generated image. Nothing publishes.

- Format: short video, 9:16 (add 4:5 or 1:1 cuts as needed).
- Visual constants: #141414, #1A1A1A, emerald #009975. The numbers are big and bold in emerald
  on near-black. Real, rights-cleared footage only.
- Energy: quick cuts, a counter that rises, music that builds to the reveal.

## Frames

### Frame 1, opening hook
- Visual: quick shots across the subjects or instructors. A counter starts at 000 and rises.
- Super slot (AR/EN): [N]+ [unit, for example journeys]. Western numerals.

### Frames 2 to k, stat stack (one stat per frame)
- Visual: montage that intensifies with each stat.
- Super slot per frame (AR/EN): [N]+ [unit]. Examples of units: releases, lessons, practical
  tips, minutes. Use confirmed figures only, never invented counts.

### Frame, suspense build
- Visual: cut toward black, the counter keeps rising toward "infinite".
- Super slot (AR/EN): [the open-ended payoff, for example opportunities].

### Final frame, reveal and CTA
- Visual: music peaks, clean branded background.
- Super slot (AR/EN): [the teaser question or reveal line, for example "can you guess what is
  coming?"], then the CTA. Launch claims follow the guardrails: no unannounced plans, no
  invented date. Use the confirmed framing from the brief.
- CTA slot: [the action, for example follow for the reveal, or DM [KEYWORD] for early access].

## Slots to resolve from the brief

- The stat figures and units (confirmed only), the reveal framing, the CTA and any [KEYWORD].
- No invented count, no unannounced plan, no invented launch date.

## Routing and gates

- Direction here, visuals executed by the designer. On-screen words to copywriter-ar and
  copywriter-en.
- Gate stack: skill eval, arabic-copy-qa, english-copy-qa, design-qa, brand-qa-reviewer, then
  the human gate to publish. Add compliance-privacy-check if a lead-gen CTA is used.
