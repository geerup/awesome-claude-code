## The principles (keep)
1. One goal per push: each notification carries exactly one job and one tap target.
2. Micro-storytelling: a push is a one-line story with a hook, not a system message.
3. Thumb-stop standard: if the first three words do not stop the scroll, rewrite.
4. Mindset timing: send to the moment, not the clock alone. Their examples: the
   late-afternoon slump around 5pm, the 9pm wind-down scroll. Map sends to user
   mindstates, then localize times per market.
5. Weekly rhythm: a planned cadence beats ad-hoc blasts; the calendar is the strategy.
6. Personality is allowed: their launch stunt framed early access as "we released it
   early by mistake". Playful is on-brand when the product moment supports it; it
   still passes the same gates (house style, claims, human gate).

## Engine adaptation
Push lives inside stream 7 sequencing (a push can replace or reinforce an email step,
never duplicate it the same day). English-first renders; one CTA; no urgency theater
without a real deadline from the brief.
