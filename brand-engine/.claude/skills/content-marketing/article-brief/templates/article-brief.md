# Article brief template

One block per article. This is a brief, not the copy. The article is authored later by
copywriter-ar or copywriter-en. One target query and one intent per article. One CTA.
All example values are illustrative only. Replace them. Do not invent offers, prices, Skill
Path titles, or instructor names. If the brief needs one and the source brief is silent, stop
and ask. English-first, English in parallel, Western numerals only, no em dash, no tatweel.

## Article brief block

```
id:            <article-<slug>>
target_query_ar: <single Arabic query, RTL>
target_query_en: <single English query>
intent:        informational | navigational | commercial | transactional
outline:
  h1:          <one H1 matching intent>
  h2:          [<h2>, <h2>]
  h3:          [<h3>]
internal_links:
  - anchor_text: <anchor>   target_url: <url>   why: <related cluster or page>
cta:           <exactly one action, tied to the angle and conversion path>
language_plan: ar-first, en in parallel, RTL noted
notes:         <values blocked on the brief, intent kept singular>
```

## Illustrative example (replace before use)

```
id:            article-daily-learning-habit
target_query_ar: كيف ابني عادة تعلم يومية
target_query_en: how to build a daily learning habit
intent:        informational
outline:
  h1:          ابنِ عادة تعلم تبقى معك
  h2:          [لماذا الخطوة الصغيرة تنجح, كيف تبدأ اليوم]
  h3:          [نصائح لوقتك المزدحم]
internal_links:
  - anchor_text: المسارات   target_url: /skill-paths   why: related cluster
cta:           ابدأ مسارك الأول
language_plan: ar-first, en in parallel, RTL noted
notes:         none
```

## Checklist before handoff

- One target query and one intent.
- Outline has one H1 and a clear H2 and H3 structure.
- Internal links carry anchor text and a reason.
- Exactly one CTA, tied to the angle and conversion path.
- English-first, English in parallel, RTL noted.
- This is a brief, the copy is written by the copywriters.
- No em dash, no tatweel, Western numerals only.
- No invented offer, price, Skill Path title, or instructor name. No accreditation implication.
- Empowering framing, never deficit-framed.
