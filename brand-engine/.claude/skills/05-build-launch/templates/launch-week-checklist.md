# Launch week checklist (mined from a real launch)

Source: [Launch Week Checklist](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1CYSdNmHIZINhTMHKvfV6Sn5oqJivhp9lWgzB8sZ5oHQ/edit)
(17 PLAYLISTS, Oct 2025): the team's own checklist for the Playlists launch. Extracted
2026-06-04. This is what launch readiness actually meant at Maharat; stream 5 adopts it
as the default checklist and adapts per brief.

## The checklist (as practiced, generalized)
1. Hero creative set: hero video, a cutdown, and a bumper.
2. Media plan phase 1 approved and loaded.
3. Tracking: Looker cleanup done, end-to-end tracking test passed BEFORE spend.
4. Lifecycle rails live: WhatsApp, Email, and ManyChat flows built, then TEST ALL FLOWS.
5. Landing page live and QA'd.
6. Organic: launch posts and captions scheduled.
7. PRL (press release) written plus translation.
8. Instructor coordination: text the instructor teams 2 days before launch.

## What this confirms about the stack (facts)
WhatsApp flows, email flows, and ManyChat were all ACTIVE channels as of Oct 2025; the
launch treated flow-testing as a release gate. The engine's human gate plus
verification stack mirrors a practice the team already had.

## Engine adaptation
Each line maps to a stream owner: 1 creative-director, 2 paid-build-engineer,
3 analytics-reporter (tracking test is a hard gate), 4 lifecycle-architect plus
conversion-engineer, 5 conversion-engineer, 6 creative plus copy, 7 copywriter-ar,
8 human-gate (Ahmed). Nothing ships while line 3 or 4 is red.
