## Reply patterns (by message type)

| Message type | On-brand reply shape | Must not claim |
|---|---|---|
| Question (how it works) | Clear, plain, empowering. Point to the next step. | No invented Skill Path title or lineup. |
| Compliment | Warm, brief, human. Invite the next step. | No accreditation, no roadmap. |
| Complaint | Acknowledge, take it to DM or support, no defensiveness. | No promise not confirmed in the brief. |
| Pricing or offer question | Only what the brief confirms. Otherwise point to the page. | No invented price, offer, or promotion. |
| Where do I start | Empowering, concrete first step toward the signup gate. | No invented title, no launch date. |

## Escalation rules

| Trigger in the message | Route to | Before any public reply |
|---|---|---|
| Data collection, PDPL, privacy | compliance-privacy-reviewer | yes |
| Accreditation question | compliance-privacy-reviewer | yes |
| Instructor name, fundraising, roadmap, unannounced plan | compliance-privacy-reviewer | yes |
| Sensitive or distressed message | a human | yes, no auto-reply |

A compliance-touching reply attaches the compliance-privacy-reviewer verdict before it
reaches the human gate.

## Never say (hard-stop list for any reply)

- No promise or implication of accreditation.
- No unannounced plan, roadmap, fundraising, or launch date.
- No invented Skill Path title, content lineup, or instructor name.
- No offer, price, or target not confirmed in the brief.
- No personal or sensitive data echoed into a public thread or a tracking parameter.

## Open items

- Anything unresolved (account not confirmed, escalation owner, support handoff path).

## Guardrails check before handing to the hub

- A public reply is a gated action; the human gate decides.
- Western numerals only (0 to 9). No em dashes, no tatweel. Empowering, never deficit-framed.
