# Template: Value carousel (lead magnet or guide)

A 5-slide educational carousel that teaches one useful thing and converts with a comment-to-DM
lead magnet. Campaign-agnostic scaffold. Structure only. Captions by copywriter-ar (AR primary)
and copywriter-en. Nothing publishes.

- Format: carousel, 4:5 or 1:1. 5 slides.
- Bilingual: choose "Bilingual" (AR and EN on one slide) or "AR/EN separate" (parallel slides).
  Arabic is an overlay slot, never baked into a generated image.
- Visual constants: #141414, #1A1A1A, emerald #009975. Premium, uncluttered.

## Slides

### Slide 1, title and hook (bilingual)
- Copy slot (AR): [hook question, MSA, for example "how do you build a strong [topic]?"].
- Copy slot (EN): [hook question].

### Slide 2, pain or invite (bilingual)
- Pattern: the struggle is not a lack of effort or talent, it is a lack of clarity or a system.
- Copy slot (AR): [reframe the problem as a clarity or structure gap, empowering].
- Copy slot (EN): [same, parallel].

### Slide 3, what is inside (AR and EN separate)
- Goal: list what the guide or framework delivers. Concrete, scannable.
- Copy slot (EN): [intro line] then 4 to 6 bullets: [item 1] [item 2] [item 3] [item 4] ...
- Copy slot (AR): [intro line] then the same bullets in MSA.

### Slide 4, visual slide (bilingual)
- Goal: one memorable visual, for example a labeled map or framework.
- Copy slot (AR): [the visual title, MSA].
- Copy slot (EN): [the visual title].
- Visual: the actual framework or map, on brand constants. Designer builds it.

### Slide 5, outcome and CTA (bilingual)
- Pattern: stop [the wrong way], stop [the other wrong way], start [the confident way].
- Copy slot (EN): [outcome lines]. Comment [KEYWORD] to get the full guide.
- Copy slot (AR): [outcome lines, MSA]. [اكتب [KEYWORD] للحصول على الدليل الكامل].
- CTA note: [KEYWORD] highlighted in a pill shape or underline. Brief input, never invented.

## Slots to resolve from the brief

- [topic], the guide contents, [KEYWORD], the accounts in scope.
- No price or offer in this format unless the brief adds one. No invented lineup or title.

## Compliance

- The DM link and the gate it lands on carry no personal or sensitive data in the URL. The
  collection point discloses its collection. Comment-to-DM automation is gated.

## Routing and gates

- Copy to copywriter-ar and copywriter-en. Visuals to creative-director and the designer.
- Gate stack: skill eval, arabic-copy-qa, english-copy-qa, design-qa, compliance-privacy-check
  on the lead-gen step, brand-qa-reviewer, then the human gate to publish.
