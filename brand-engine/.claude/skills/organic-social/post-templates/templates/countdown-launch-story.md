# Template: Countdown or launch story

A 3-beat story sequence for a drop, launch, or early-access push. Builds urgency through a
countdown, pulls an interaction, then converts with a keyword lead-gen. Campaign-agnostic
scaffold. Structure only. Captions by copywriter-ar (AR primary) and copywriter-en. Nothing
publishes.

- Format: stories. Build in 9:16 and 4:5. In 9:16 leave space for the interactive sticker.
- Bilingual: AR primary, EN parallel. Arabic is an overlay slot, never baked in.
- Visual constants: #141414, #1A1A1A, emerald #009975. Premium, uncluttered. Do not use the
  bright source backgrounds. The number is big and bold in emerald on near-black.
- Launch claims follow the guardrails: no unannounced plans, no invented date. Use the
  confirmed launch framing from the brief (for example soft launch or early access).

## Beats

### Story 1, countdown intro
- Goal: signal that something is coming and when.
- Copy slot (AR): [N] [أيام متبقية]. [one-line launch line, MSA, confirmed framing].
- Copy slot (EN): [N] days left. [one-line launch line, confirmed framing].
- Sticker note: leave space for the countdown sticker in 9:16. Use Western numerals for [N].

### Story 2, engagement mechanic
- Goal: a low-effort poll that also segments interest.
- Copy slot (AR): [poll question, MSA, for example "which area do you want to grow first?"].
- Copy slot (EN): [poll question].
- Poll options (2 to 4): [option A] | [option B] | [option C] | [option D].
- Sticker note: 9:16 only, leave space for the poll sticker. Do not bake the title or options.

### Story 3, CTA lead-gen
- Goal: capture the most ready with a keyword.
- Copy slot (AR): [be first, one line]. [أرسل لنا كلمة [KEYWORD] للحصول على [the asset, for
  example the early-access link]].
- Copy slot (EN): Be the first to join. DM us [KEYWORD] to get [the asset].
- CTA note: [KEYWORD] is highlighted in a pill shape or underline. The keyword is a brief
  input, never invented.

## Slots to resolve from the brief

- [N] countdown number, [KEYWORD], the confirmed launch framing, the asset the keyword
  delivers, the accounts in scope.
- No price unless the brief confirms one. No invented launch date.

## Compliance

- The link the DM delivers, and the gate it lands on, carry no personal or sensitive data in
  the URL. The data-collection point must disclose its collection. Comment-to-DM or DM-to-link
  automation is a gated tool and platform decision, not assumed.

## Routing and gates

- Copy to copywriter-ar and copywriter-en. Visuals to creative-director and the designer.
- Gate stack: skill eval, arabic-copy-qa, english-copy-qa, design-qa, compliance-privacy-check
  on the lead-gen step, brand-qa-reviewer, then the human gate to publish.
