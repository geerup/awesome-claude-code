# Template: Story engagement sequence

A 4-beat Instagram or social story funnel that opens on tension and warms the audience through
participation. Campaign-agnostic scaffold. Structure only. Captions are written by
copywriter-ar (AR primary) and copywriter-en, referenced by variant id. Nothing publishes.

- Format: stories, 9:16 (add a 4:5 or 1:1 cut where the format allows).
- Bilingual: AR primary, EN parallel. Arabic is an overlay slot, never baked into a generated
  image.
- Visual constants: #141414 background, #1A1A1A surfaces, emerald #009975 accent. Premium,
  uncluttered. Do not use the bright source backgrounds.
- Engagement stickers are native; leave on-screen space and never bake the poll or slider
  options into the art.

## Beats

### Story 1, the hook
- Goal: stop the scroll with a sharp, relatable question.
- Copy slot (AR): [hook question, MSA, one line].
- Copy slot (EN): [hook question, one line].
- Visual: clean title-card style on brand background.

### Story 2, the tension or chaos
- Goal: name the messy reality the viewer recognizes. Short, stacked lines.
- Copy slot (AR): [3 to 4 short lines that build the tension, then one line that lands "and
  none of it connects"].
- Copy slot (EN): [same, parallel].
- Visual: text builds line by line, brand background.

### Story 3, the poll
- Goal: pull a low-effort interaction.
- Copy slot (AR): [poll question, MSA].
- Copy slot (EN): [poll question].
- Poll options (2 to 4): [option A] | [option B] | [option C] | [option D].
- Sticker note: leave space under the question for the poll sticker. Do not render the poll
  title or options as baked text.

### Story 4, the pain point
- Goal: sharpen the core problem and hint at the path out. Empowering, never deficit-framed.
- Copy slot (AR): [the real problem in one or two lines, framed as a clarity gap, not a
  personal failing].
- Copy slot (EN): [same, parallel].
- Optional mechanic: emoji slider on a confidence question. Leave space, no baked title.
- Soft hand-off: this sequence warms toward a value carousel or a CTA story, it does not hard
  sell here.

## Routing and gates

- Copy to copywriter-ar and copywriter-en. Visuals to creative-director and the designer.
- Gate stack: skill eval, arabic-copy-qa, english-copy-qa, design-qa on visuals,
