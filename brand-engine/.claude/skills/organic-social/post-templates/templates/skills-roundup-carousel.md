# Template: Skills roundup carousel

A carousel that bundles several skills, themes, or classes into one "starter kit" or seasonal
roundup, each on its own slide with a watch line and a concrete outcome, then a CTA.
Campaign-agnostic scaffold. Structure only. Captions by copywriter-ar (AR primary) and
copywriter-en. Nothing publishes.

- Format: carousel, 4:5 or 1:1. A cover, one slide per skill or theme, a CTA slide.
- Bilingual: AR primary, EN parallel. Arabic is an overlay slot, never baked in.
- Visual constants: #141414, #1A1A1A, emerald #009975. Premium, uncluttered.

## Slides

### Slide 1, cover (bilingual)
- Copy slot (AR): [the roundup name, for example your [year] starter kit], MSA. [one-line
  promise].
- Copy slot (EN): [the roundup name]. [one-line promise].

### Slides 2 to N, one skill or theme per slide
- Pattern per slide: the skill, who to watch, the outcome.
- Skill line (AR/EN): Skill [n]: [the skill in plain words].
- Watch line (AR/EN): Watch: [INSTRUCTOR], [INSTRUCTOR]. Only confirmed-public instructors,
  per the standing rule. Use placeholders until confirmed.
- Outcome line (AR/EN): [2 to 3 concrete outcomes, comma separated, empowering].
- Use Western numerals for the skill number.

### Slide N+1, CTA (bilingual)
- Pattern: start with one skill, build the whole [period].
- Copy slot (EN): Start with one skill. Build your whole [period]. [CTA, for example comment
  [KEYWORD] for [OFFER] if the brief sets an offer].
- Copy slot (AR): [same, MSA]. [اكتب [KEYWORD] للحصول على [OFFER]].

## Slots to resolve from the brief

- The skills or themes, the [INSTRUCTOR] names (confirmed-public only), the outcomes,
  [KEYWORD], [OFFER] and [period]. No offer or discount is invented; it is a brief input.
- Never invent a Skill Path title or the content lineup.

## Compliance

- Any [KEYWORD] lead-gen link and gate carry no personal or sensitive data in the URL and
  disclose collection. Comment-to-DM automation is gated.

## Routing and gates

- Copy to copywriter-ar and copywriter-en. Visuals to creative-director and the designer.
- Gate stack: skill eval, arabic-copy-qa, english-copy-qa, design-qa, compliance-privacy-check
  if a lead-gen or offer CTA is used, brand-qa-reviewer, then the human gate to publish.
