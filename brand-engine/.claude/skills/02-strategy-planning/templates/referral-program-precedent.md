# Referral program precedent (mined research)

Source: [Referral Program Strategy Research](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Q2rTCq10pRuOOl9HOOO39Ox2P_Gbn-I2u_2fs2Hh8L4/edit)
(Growth research, Oct 2025). Extracted 2026-06-04.

## The design the team researched
Double-sided referral: the referrer gives 20 percent off, and earns 20 percent off
(give 20, get 20). Benchmark-driven (consumer subscription norms), symmetric so the
share message stays generous rather than self-serving.

## Status
Research existed as of Oct 2025; whether the program shipped is unconfirmed (queued).
If live, it belongs in the lifecycle arc (a referral beat after activation) and in the
post-purchase email footer. If not live, it is a ready growth play for stream 2 to
propose with the research already done.

## Engine adaptation notes
- The Gifters list (segmentation template) is the natural seed audience: people who
  already share Maharat.
- Offer mechanics are brief inputs; 20/20 is the researched default, not a fixed rule.
- English-first share copy must make the give-side lead: generosity frames better than
  discount-hunting in the brand voice.
