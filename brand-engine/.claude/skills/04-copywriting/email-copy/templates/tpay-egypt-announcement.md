## Why this doc matters beyond its content
REGISTER PRECEDENT: this production email is written in Egyptian colloquial for an
Egypt-targeted send (its words, not ours), while the failed-payment transactional email
is formal MSA. So the team already practices geo and purpose based register: MSA for
brand and transactional, local dialect for geo-targeted promos. This is direct input to
the open register-policy question; it suggests the policy is a matrix (purpose x geo),
not a single rule. Queue item updated.

## Structure (keep)
3 subject variants plus 1 preview line; greeting; the news in one sentence; the partner
and wallet list; numbered how-to steps (5); a trust line (security); a single CTA.

TPAY and Meeza enable e-wallet payment for Egypt. Adds to Stripe, Apple IAP, Google
Play in the stack map; the cart and checkout event taxonomy should include the wallet
path. Confirm current status with the team.
