# App launch emails (production copy, mined)

Sources: [App Launch Emails](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ir6gOSV4oncNonByO6sonnTKJia0yAnNwHGc2CetFbY/edit)
and [App Description](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1LYAN1_XlO15kyGB7LeL60iyVDHDU6iEJiV-7RRcnZak/edit)
(App Launch campaign, 2024). Extracted 2026-06-04.

## What shipped (keep as the launch-email pattern)
- Bilingual announcement email: the news in one line (the Maharat app is here), then
  the benefit set (watch anywhere, pick up where you left off, classes and playlists
  in your pocket), then a single store CTA per platform.
- Launch promo mechanic: a named code, APP20, for 20 percent off, time-bound. The
  pattern to reuse: short memorable code, one number, one deadline, stated once.
- The App Description doc is the store-listing copy ("library of Arab Greatness",
  instructor names as the hook, premium positioning): the canonical source when any
  asset must describe the app. Its public Play Store version matches.

## Adaptation rules
- Promo codes and percentages come from the brief only; APP20 is a precedent, not a
  live offer.
- Store CTAs split by platform when the channel knows the device; otherwise one
  smart link.
- Instructor names in app-positioning copy still pass the catalog status gate.
