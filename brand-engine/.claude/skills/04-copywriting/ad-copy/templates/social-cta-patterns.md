# Template: Social CTA and caption patterns

Reusable CTA and caption patterns for organic social, extracted and normalized from the Maharat
organic copy doc. For copywriter-ar (AR primary) and copywriter-en. Patterns and slots only,
not final copy. Nothing publishes.

These patterns pair with the post templates in
`skills/organic-social/post-templates/templates/`. The keyword and any offer are brief inputs,
never invented.

## The keyword lead-gen patterns

The core mechanic is a single keyword that triggers a DM with a link or asset. Keep the action
singular and the keyword highlighted in a pill shape or underline.

| Intent | EN pattern | AR pattern (MSA) |
|---|---|---|
| Lead magnet or guide | Comment [KEYWORD] to get the full guide. | اكتب [KEYWORD] للحصول على الدليل الكامل. |
| Early access or launch | DM us [KEYWORD] to get an exclusive launch link. | أرسل لنا كلمة [KEYWORD] للحصول على الرابط الحصري. |
| Class or playlist reveal | Reply [KEYWORD] for early access. | أرسل كلمة [KEYWORD] للحصول على الدخول المبكر. |
| Offer | Comment [KEYWORD] for [OFFER]. | اكتب [KEYWORD] للحصول على [OFFER]. |

- [KEYWORD] examples seen in the source: GUIDE, START, READY, BRAND, CAMPAIGN. The actual
  keyword is a brief input.
- [OFFER] is a brief input. Never invent a discount, price, or promotion.

## Caption structure

- Lead with the hook or the outcome, not the brand. One idea per line.
- Empowering, never deficit-framed. The reader is capable and needs clarity or a system.
- One clear CTA at the end. Do not stack competing CTAs.
- Bilingual: AR primary, EN parallel, or AR and EN separate per the format.

## Outcome-led phrasing (reusable shapes, fill per campaign)

- "Stop [the wrong way]. Stop [the other wrong way]. Start [the confident way]."
- "[Capability] that shows: [outcome 1], [outcome 2], [outcome 3]."
- "Start with one [unit]. Build your whole [period]."

Keep these as shapes. The specifics come from the strategy-artifact and the brief.

## Hard rules

- Keyword, offer, price, instructor name, and class title are brief inputs, never invented.
- No accreditation implication, no unannounced plans, no roadmap.
- The DM link and the gate it lands on carry no personal or sensitive data in the URL, and the
  collection point discloses its collection. Comment-to-DM automation is a gated tool decision.
- No em dashes, no tatweel, Western numerals only, empowering framing never deficit-framed.
