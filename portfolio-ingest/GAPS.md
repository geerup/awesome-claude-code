# GAPS.md - Positioning Gap Audit

Read the way a skeptical hiring manager at an AI-forward, developer-facing
company would. The thesis being audited: **"AI-native marketing executive
who builds agentic systems."**

---

## 0. The headline problem (read this first)

The thesis is not supported by the source material. Sixteen years of
evidence prove a **social/content/organic-growth executive with unusually
good measurement discipline** - they prove nothing about AI-native work or
agentic systems. There is not one AI tool, agent, workflow, repo, or shipped
automation in any imported file. The current site does not even contain the
word "AI" outside "Data & AI Masters" (a Canonical product event).

If the portfolio claims "builds agentic systems" today, the first interview
question ("show me one") ends the conversation. Either build and ship the
/lab artifacts first, or position on what the evidence already supports and
let the AI-native claim ride as trajectory, not track record.

## 2. Metrics on the site lacking baseline, window, or attribution

Recorded as-is per rule 4; each needs a source, a qualifier, or deletion.

1. **"$5M+ enterprise pipeline influenced" (hero + CV)** - no source file, no
   window, no definition of "influenced." Highest-risk number on the site.
2. **"100M+ annual cross-platform impressions" (2024)** - plausible from
   platform tables (Facebook alone: 162,098,943 in 2024) but no aggregate
   export states it.
3. **"-28% CAC" (PAYD, CV)** - appears nowhere in source files.
4. **"Six-figure ARR" from PAYD co-launch partnerships (CV)** - no source.
5. **"7M followers in 6 months"** - no analytics artifact; cumulative vs net
   unclear; YouTube baseline contradicted (site: 125K→1M; sources: 300K→1M).
6. **"870%" SEO growth** - 14K→122K is 771% growth (8.7x). "870%" reads as
   the multiple stated as a delta. Also: no analytics source named.
7. **"$2.8M+ earned media" (Mindvalley)** - no EMV methodology or window.
8. **"+20% MAU / -18% CAC" (Goodwall)** - no absolute baselines; "all
   organic" overstates a blended paid+organic programme (source says so).
9. **"+50% pipeline / +400% CTR" (Agiliux)** - no baselines; "+35% APAC
   acquisition" appears verbatim in BOTH the PAYD and Agiliux sources -
   verify it is two facts, not one number double-counted.
10. **"2.58M audience" (cases-data.js)** vs 2,709,827 (Dec 2024 tracker) vs
    "2.81M" (hero) vs "3M+" (CV, same page) - four values in circulation;
    pick the tracker number and date-stamp it.
11. **"481,686 LinkedIn engagements 2024 (+169%)"** - mid-year deck figure;
    the full-year Sprout export says 974,046. Publish one, cite it.
12. **Canonical end date** - site CV says 2022-2024; recommendations dated
    May-Jul 2025 say "3 years, until 2025"; trackers run to April 2025.
    A reference check trips on this.
13. **"Roles led: 8 / Industries: 6 / Brands shipped: 40+"** stat block -
    fine as framing, but "40+ brands" is the agency's portfolio breadth,
    not brands he led.

## 3. Claims with no linkable proof

Structural finding: **the site contains zero outbound proof links.** Every
claim below is real per sources but unlinked:

- Growthhacker case study (69% reply rate) - external citation, unlinked.
- Foundr Magazine authored article - unlinked.
- Forbes / SCMP / Thrive Global / Bernama / The Star / Vulcan Post
  placements - all unlinked.
- TEDx talk, WTO keynote, DMICON keynote, DMLS HK chairing - no recordings
  or event pages linked.
- YouTube Gold Play Button - no channel link or photo.
- "70+ media features" (Canonical) - no list.
- LinkedIn recommendations - quoted but not linked to the profile.
- A+M Agency of the Year 2016 - no announcement link.
- "View all 12 recommendations on LinkedIn ↗" exists as text; confirm the
  href works.

Cheapest wins, in order: Growthhacker URL, Foundr URL, 2-3 press URLs,
TEDx video, LinkedIn profile deep-link. An afternoon of link-gathering
converts six claims into proof.

## 4. Projects framed as "thing I made" rather than "problem I solved"

- **Maharat** (site + cases-data.js): pure description - role, team, market,
  zero tension, placeholder metrics. Recommendation: CV-line only, no case
  study, until one defensible metric exists.
- **Early career / Insight Magazine**: framed as biography ("where the
  pattern started") - acceptable as a coda, but it is a life story, not a
  case.
- **Mindvalley social (7M)**: reads as a result announcement; the underlying
  problem (no team, no strategy, flat organic) is in the source but buried
  under the number on the site.
- **Falcon**: framed as a client logo wall + aggregate stats. The solved
  problems (Deliveroo community turnaround, Ringgit Plus conversion) are
  stronger than the logo wall and are personally attributable - lead with
  those, drop or clearly label the agency aggregates ($19M, -45% CAC,
  +38% conversion, 8M followers).
- **20 Years of Ubuntu**: currently a highlights reel on the site; the
  actual case (milestone-content failure mode, community-first decision) is
  the seniority signal and should lead.

## 5. Verdict before Phase 6

The Canonical material is genuinely strong and survives scrutiny; the
2017-2021 material is claims-rich and proof-poor; the /lab wing and the
AI-native thesis have no supporting artifacts at all. Recommended order of
operations: fix the 13 metric items above, gather the cheap proof links,
decide the positioning question in section 0, ship one P1 artifact - then
design. A prettier version of the current claims would inherit all of these
holes.
