# Canonical - A social function built from zero, with Salesforce-attributed pipeline

**Rank: 1 of 5 · Pillars: P2 (technical audience at scale), P3 (defensible demand gen), P1 (partial - built the tracking system, not just the content)**

## Hook

I built Canonical's entire social and content function from scratch and wired it to Salesforce - social MQLs went from 36 to 785 in a single year, and the audience reached 2.81M by April 2025.

## Context

When I joined Canonical in 2022, the publisher of Ubuntu had no structured social media function. Content was inconsistent, untracked, and disconnected from marketing pipeline. There was no content calendar, no influencer programme, and no process for tying social to MQLs or revenue.

## Tension

For an enterprise open source company selling to developers and sysadmins, social was a brand cost centre - and there was no tracking architecture that could prove or disprove that. Leadership had no way to see whether any of it mattered commercially.

## Mandate

I was accountable for building the function end to end: strategy, production, distribution, measurement, and the influencer programme. A colleague's on-the-record recommendation describes the arc: "he built our social media strategy from scratch, turning it from a one-person operation to a full team that works like a clock" (Marina Khachatryan, Senior Marketing Manager, Canonical).

## Decisions

- **Salesforce integration from day one.** I integrated the function with Salesforce so every social MQL was attributable and reported to leadership. This is the decision the rest of the case rests on: without it, the 785 number would not exist as evidence.
- **Relationship-based creator network instead of paid amplification.** I built a 48-creator network in the hardware, homelab and open source micro-communities where Canonical's developer and sysadmin audience actually lives (ExplainingComputers, Jeff Geerling, Lawrence Systems, Level1Linux and 44 more), on access and relationships rather than a rate card. [Inference: the rejected alternative - paid placements - is stated in my Mindvalley influencer source, not the Canonical one.]
- **Platform-specific jobs.** LinkedIn, X, YouTube, Facebook and Instagram each got a distinct purpose, format mix and success metric, rather than one cross-posted feed.

## Execution

I built a content production system that generated 4,000+ assets across video, thought leadership, partner activations and live event coverage. I directed social and video for 20+ global product launches with Dell, Intel, NVIDIA and Framework. I launched a LinkedIn Newsletter that grew organically to 109K subscribers by September 2024. Mid-2024 I introduced the carousel format (33 new carousels vs 5 previously) which became the top-performing content type. I presented the annual strategy to the commercial team at the Prague 2023 sprint and MC'd Ubuntu Summit 2024.

## Outcome

- Social MQLs: **36 to 785 in 2023** - Salesforce-tracked, reported in the Vancouver Sprint 2024 internal review. The year before that (2022 vs 2021), MQLs had grown 8.8x.
- Audience: **2,813,262 by April 2025** (Sprout Social tracker), with 305K net new followers in 2024 (+25% vs 2023).
- Organic engagement rate: **1.3% in 2022 to 5.18% across 2024** (Sprout).
- LinkedIn: from 90K followers when a colleague joined in 2022 to **500K+ by May 2025** (his words, on the record); Sprout tracker shows 551,962 in April 2025.
- Social-referred website sessions: **621K in 2022**, the first measured year.
