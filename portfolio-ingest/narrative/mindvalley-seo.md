# Mindvalley - 870% organic traffic growth in eleven months

**Supporting case (/work archive) · Pillars: P3 (partial)**

## Hook

I rebuilt Mindvalley's SEO from noise into an acquisition channel: 14K to 122K monthly organic visits in 11 months.

## Context

Organic traffic was 14K monthly visits - negligible for a $40M e-learning company with a massive social presence. Content existed, but there was no SEO architecture, no systematic link building, no outreach strategy.

## Tension

Organic was not a meaningful acquisition channel while the company's whole content engine kept publishing into the void.

## Mandate

I audited and rebuilt on-page SEO, built the off-page outreach operation, and coordinated with PR and social.

## Decisions

- **Run all three levers in parallel** - on-page rebuild, high-reply outreach (the same 69% reply-rate operation as the PR case), and PR-driven authority links - rather than sequentially. "When all three compound, the growth curve is non-linear."

## Execution

Rebuilt the content hierarchy around high-intent keywords tied to Mindvalley's courses and author ecosystem; monthly measurement and adjustment on keyword movement.

## Outcome

**14K to 122K monthly organic visits in 11 months (2018-2019).** Note before publishing: 14K to 122K is a 771% increase (8.7x); the "870%" phrasing reads as the multiple rather than the delta - NEEDS_HUMAN to pick the honest phrasing.
