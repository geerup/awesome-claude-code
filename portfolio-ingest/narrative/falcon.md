# Falcon Agency - 40+ global brands at agency pace

**Supporting case (/work archive) · Pillars: P3 (partial), P4 (partial)**

## Hook

Three years at the A+M Agency of the Year 2016, working across 40+ global brands - L'Oreal, Deliveroo, Uber, Motul, Manulife, Canon, Heinz - is the muscle memory behind every marketing engine I have built since.

## Context

Falcon Agency / 16Two was a full-service digital agency across Malaysia and the GCC. I joined as Digital Marketing Manager in 2014. In any given week the desk held a consumer FMCG brand, a FinTech app, a luxury automotive client and a QSR chain.

## Tension

Breadth was the job. The skill was not deep vertical expertise but orienting fast in an unfamiliar category, identifying what a brand actually needed (not what it asked for), and producing work that held up commercially.

## Mandate

Content strategies, social management, paid campaigns and client knowledge sessions across the portfolio. Important honesty note: some aggregate numbers attached to this era (USD 19M budgets, -45% CAC, +38% conversion, 8M followers under management) are agency-level totals, not personal attributions - see GAPS.md.

## Decisions

- **Made influencer storyboarding a standard agency deliverable** - first built for L'Oreal's ExOil / ShineWithConfidence campaigns (Tammy Tay storyboard, ~$85K USD across 4 phases) at a time when it was not standard practice.

## Execution

- Deliveroo Singapore (2016): full social audit, community management, UberEATS competitive analysis, and the CatchTheRoo viral campaign developed to a v9 pitch (projected 1.5M reach, $31K SGD).
- Great Eastern Life: RM 350,536 budget against a 5,000-lead KPI in 6 months with three-persona age-band targeting.
- Ringgit Plus: CITI, HSBC, PBBK and MAYB bank campaigns over multiple months.
- Predictive budget models for FinTech clients; five-part GDLP client knowledge programme (GDN, digital landscape, omni-channel, Facebook, CRO).

## Outcome

Personally attributed, from campaign reporting:

- Deliveroo SG fans: **5,129 to 12,355 (+140.89%) in 3.5 months**, 16.8M monthly impressions, 4.46% average ER (9.66% peak), 78.95% question response rate.
- Ringgit Plus: **+5% page conversion on paid traffic**, with a personal commendation from the client executive team.
- FinTech ROAS: **+22% over baseline** via predictive budget modelling.
