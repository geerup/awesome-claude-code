# Ubuntu Summit - Earned media grew 15x in two years

**Rank: 2 of 5 · Pillars: P4 (brand and narrative for technical products), P2 (technical audience)**

## Hook

I turned Canonical's flagship conference from a modest media moment into its largest annual earned-media event: AVE from $518,810 in 2022 to $7,930,540 in 2024, reach from 56M to 857M, on the same editorial plan across organic social, YouTube and press.

## Context

Ubuntu Summit is Canonical's annual gathering of Ubuntu contributors, developers and community. When I took over social and content strategy in 2022, coverage was conventional: the event happened, some posts went out, the moment passed.

## Tension

Conference coverage is easy to do badly - recap photos and a keynote recording that disappear within 24 hours and leave no lasting organic footprint. The brief was to turn a 3-day event into a weeks-long content engine that generated sustained earned media.

## Mandate

I owned the Summit's social and content strategy for all three years (2022, 2023, 2024), and MC'd the 2024 edition.

## Decisions

- **Treat coverage as content production, not documentation.** The explicit alternative - documentation-style coverage - was the failure mode I was correcting.
- **Hero-to-snacks model.** Full sessions filmed for YouTube, then clipped into short-form for social, so one production budget fed two distribution systems.
- **Three-phase editorial plan.** Pre-event speaker series and countdowns through the 109K-subscriber LinkedIn Newsletter; real-time coverage during; recordings, compilations and press coordination after.

## Execution

Each year the plan got more sophisticated, and the earned-media numbers track that evolution directly. The 2024 breakout was not planned as one: a live Framework Laptop RISC-V hacking session became Canonical's second-highest-performing YouTube video of the year - 34,678 views, 1,120 watch-hours, 456,821 impressions, 4.56% CTR, all organic - because it served real developer curiosity rather than product promotion.

## Outcome

Meltwater year-on-year, Summit 2022 vs Summit 2024:

- Mentions: **284 to 946** (+233%)
- Total reach: **56,087,571 to 857,355,723** (+15.3x)
- AVE: **$518,810 to $7,930,540** (+15.3x)
- Engagement: **1,037 to 7,986** (+670%)
- Max single-mention reach: **8,693,654 to 127,227,100** (+14.6x)

Sprout Social tag tracking (April 2024 to April 2025): 84 Summit-tagged posts, 618,704 impressions, 9.4% cross-network engagement rate, LinkedIn at 15.53%.
