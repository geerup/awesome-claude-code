# Mindvalley - $2.8M+ earned media and a 69% journalist reply rate

**Supporting case (/work archive) · Pillars: P4 (partial)**

## Hook

I built Mindvalley's PR function end to end and ran journalist outreach at a 69% reply rate - a figure notable enough that Growthhacker published it as a public case study.

## Context

Mindvalley was a $40M e-learning company that was functionally a best-kept secret in most Western and Asian media markets: loyal community, growing social, no authoritative press.

## Tension

Without tier-1 coverage the brand lacked validation for enterprise buyers, new audiences and talent.

## Mandate

I built the function: media tiers by region, story angles, outreach, press junkets to the KL headquarters, the founder's media appearances (including BERNAMA Radio), and the ROOTS PR agency retainer (RM 203,520 per year).

## Decisions

- **Research-first outreach over volume.** Every pitch was researched, personalised and tied to a live story angle in the journalist's beat. Spray-and-pray was the explicitly rejected alternative - "a 69% reply rate isn't a volume play, it's a research play."
- **PR compounds with SEO.** Placements were treated as backlinks too; a Forbes placement validated the brand and reinforced organic rankings simultaneously.

## Outcome

- Earned media value: **$2.8M+ over 2018-2020** (methodology NEEDS_HUMAN).
- Reply rate: **69%**, externally cited by Growthhacker.
- Placements: Forbes, SCMP, Thrive Global, Bernama, The Star, Vulcan Post.
- Authored feature in Foundr Magazine: "How to Get Subscribers on YouTube."
