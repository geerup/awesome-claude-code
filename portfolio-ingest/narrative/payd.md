# PAYD - A FinTech marketing function from zero, at 2x the benchmark open rate

**Supporting case (/work archive) · Pillars: P1 (partial - built the stack), P3 (partial)**

## Hook

I built PAYD's marketing function from nothing - brand, martech, funnels - and ran email at a 50% open rate, roughly double the 20-25% B2B SaaS benchmark.

## Context

PAYD (JustPayd) is a Kuala Lumpur earned-wage-access startup - a category most APAC employers and employees had never encountered. When I joined as Head of Marketing in 2021 there was no marketing function, no email system, no messaging architecture.

## Tension

EWA is category creation with a two-sided funnel: educate a new financial model against payday-loan alternatives while converting HR and finance leaders AND their employees - across APAC markets that differ in language, payment culture, regulation and digital maturity.

## Mandate

Build the infrastructure, own brand and martech, produce measurable acquisition growth on lean budgets.

## Decisions

- **Two parallel funnels, not one.** Employer-facing (HR/finance) and employee-facing (activation) tracks, because the B2B2C model acquires employers first and employees through them.
- **Localise per market.** "APAC is not a single market" - campaigns were adapted for language, payment culture and regulatory framing rather than run pan-regionally.

## Execution

Set up brand identity, messaging framework, website copy, email infrastructure (Ortto), content templates and social presence; implemented the CRM and tracking stack that gave PAYD its first structured funnel view.

## Outcome

- Email open rate: **50%** vs a 20-25% B2B SaaS benchmark.
- APAC customer acquisition: **+35%** without proportionally scaling spend (baseline NEEDS_HUMAN).
