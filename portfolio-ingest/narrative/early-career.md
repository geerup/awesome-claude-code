# Early career - Cairo, Dubai, Kuala Lumpur (2010-2014)

**Supporting case (/work archive, foundation) · Pillars: none directly**

## Hook

Four years of the work nobody assigns a title to - writing, editing, community managing, running paid campaigns - including growing Qatar Happening into the 12th-largest Facebook page in Qatar, organically.

## Context

Early-Facebook-era MENA social media, when organic reach was real and community management was a performance discipline rather than a support function.

## Tension

No playbooks existed. Every strategy started from a blank document.

## Mandate

- MizaTech / Socialeyez (Cairo/Dubai, 2010-2013): managed social for a MENA brand portfolio; integrated paid campaigns up to $2,000/day; 20% Mailchimp CTR against a ~3% average.
- Joud (Cairo, 2013-2014): built the Facebook community strategy from scratch for a 200+ SKU lifestyle brand - weekly plans, JoudNESS competition mechanics, newsletter, hard KPIs (10+ daily inquiries, 3+ daily orders - targets; attainment NEEDS_HUMAN).
- Insight Magazine (Dubai/KL, 2012-2014): lead journalist and editor, cover to cover.

## Decisions

- Wrote the first full community playbook at Joud - order response rules, competition logistics, engagement mechanics, brand voice. The source notes it is structurally similar to the editorial frameworks built at Canonical and Mindvalley a decade later.

## Execution

See mandate - this era is the execution.

## Outcome

- Qatar Happening: **12th-largest Facebook page in Qatar, organic only** (ranking source NEEDS_HUMAN).
- **20% email CTR** via Mailchimp vs ~3% industry average.
