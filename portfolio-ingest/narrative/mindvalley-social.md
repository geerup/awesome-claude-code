# Mindvalley - Zero to seven million followers in six months

**Supporting case (/work archive) · Pillars: P3 (partial)**

## Hook

I grew Mindvalley's combined social audience to 7M+ followers in six months, fully organic, earning a YouTube Gold Play Button along the way.

## Context

Mindvalley, a $40M e-learning company, had active accounts but no unified strategy: Facebook at 300K, YouTube at 300K (site copy says 125K - NEEDS_HUMAN to reconcile), Instagram at 50K. Organic was flat.

## Tension

There was no team structure behind content and no system for turning followers into leads.

## Mandate

I built the platform-specific content strategy, recruited and structured the social team where there had not been one, and launched the KOL programme (see the influencer case).

## Decisions

- Platform-specific strategies run simultaneously - distinct formats, cadences and growth tactics per channel - rather than one cross-posted feed.

## Execution

Coordinated social with PR, SEO and events so every major moment had a cross-channel amplification plan.

## Outcome

- Total audience: **7M+ within six months** (window and cumulative-vs-net basis NEEDS_HUMAN).
- Facebook **300K to 2M** · YouTube **to 1M with Gold Play Button** · Instagram **50K to 250K**.
- Promoted **3x in 3 years** (2017-2020).
