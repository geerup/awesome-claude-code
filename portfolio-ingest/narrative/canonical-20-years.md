# 20 Years of Ubuntu - 14.4% engagement on a ~5% platform

**Rank: 3 of 5 · Pillars: P4 (brand and narrative for technical products), P2 (technical audience)**

## Hook

I directed Canonical's biggest brand moment of 2024 - Ubuntu's 20th anniversary - and anchored it on the community instead of the company: 2,096,233 LinkedIn impressions at a 14.4% engagement rate against a roughly 5% platform benchmark.

## Context

Ubuntu turned 20 in 2024. The anniversary was the highest-investment content production of Canonical's year, planned as its flagship brand campaign.

## Tension

Milestones are easy to get wrong. They tend to produce self-congratulatory content that talks about the company rather than its community - and open source audiences punish exactly that. The campaign had to resonate with the community, get shared organically, and still carry Canonical's brand.

## Mandate

I developed and directed the campaign across all social channels.

## Decisions

- **Anchor on the community, not the company.** The campaign framed 20 years as a shared achievement of contributors. The rejected alternative was the default: a company-centric milestone reel. This one decision, made in the brief, is what the 14.4% engagement rate traces back to - "when the audience sees themselves in the story, they share it."

## Execution

I produced a flagship video (Ubuntu 24.04 LTS Noble Numbat / 20 Years) designed for organic sharing - the most-produced video asset of the year. We ran a coordinated publish across LinkedIn, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and X with platform-specific formats, and amplified UGC from the open source community and Canonical employees - hundreds of community pieces extended reach beyond Canonical's own audience.

## Outcome

From the October 2024 internal results deck and the YouTube 2024 uploads export:

- LinkedIn: **2,096,233 impressions, 302,426 engagements, 14.4% engagement rate** during the campaign period (vs a ~5% platform benchmark - benchmark source NEEDS_HUMAN).
- Flagship video on YouTube: **378,699 views, 1,044,354 impressions, 5,441 watch-hours** in 2024 - the single most-viewed Canonical video of the year.
- Cross-platform video views: **392,349** in the campaign period; 1.8M+ total campaign impressions by September year-to-date.

## Proof

- Social update October 2024 deck and YouTube Uploads 2024 export (named in projects.json provenance).
- NEEDS_HUMAN: public link to the flagship video and examples of the community UGC.

## Reflection

The stated lesson: the highest-performing content is the most resonant, not the most polished. The honest limit of the case: "hundreds of UGC pieces" is a soft count, and the campaign rode a genuinely historic milestone - the framing decision was mine, the occasion was not.
