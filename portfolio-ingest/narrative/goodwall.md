# Goodwall - Community-led acquisition: +20% MAU, -18% CAC

**Supporting case (/work archive) · Pillars: P3 (partial)**

## Hook

At a 1M-member Gen Z platform, I shifted acquisition from paid installs to community activation: monthly active users up 20% in three months while blended CAC fell 18%.

## Context

Goodwall is a Geneva-headquartered social impact platform for students and young professionals across 150+ countries. When I joined as Marketing Director in 2019, it had crossed 1M members.

## Tension

Acquisition cost was climbing and MAU was not keeping pace with sign-ups. The 10-person cross-functional team (web, content, email, paid, app) had no centralised marketing strategy.

## Mandate

Grow MAU, reduce CAC, turn the existing community into a distribution asset - and build the strategy layer the team was missing.

## Decisions

- **Activate existing users instead of buying new ones.** I reduced reliance on Facebook and Instagram install ads and rebuilt the content architecture around return triggers - achievement milestones, seasonal campaigns (GEW Launch, Climate Action, Halloween, Thanksgiving, Changemaker), social moments designed for members to post, share and invite peers. The prior approach - scaling paid installs - was the explicitly rejected default.

## Execution

Built the top-level digital strategy, the 2019 user personas, and the marketing roadmap in Roadmunk; led the team of 10 with shared roadmap, calendar and brief templates.

## Outcome

- MAU: **+20% in 3 months** (absolute baseline NEEDS_HUMAN).
- CAC: **-18% monthly** (blended; baseline NEEDS_HUMAN).
- Members: **1M+ at joining to 1.3M+ at exit** (2019-2020).
