# Agiliux - InsurTech pipeline +50% on content, not budget

**Supporting case (/work archive, alternate for top five) · Pillars: P3**

## Hook

I rewrote an InsurTech SaaS company's site and ads in insurance-operations language instead of SaaS feature language: pipeline up 50% and ad CTR up 400% with no increase in spend.

## Context

Agiliux is an APAC InsurTech SaaS (CRM and policy management for insurance agencies). When I joined as Head of Marketing in 2020 the product was solid but marketing was early-stage: no content architecture, no nurture system, and ads generating clicks that did not convert.

## Tension

Insurance is a trust industry. Buyers need to believe the vendor understands compliance pressure, operational complexity and the realities of running an agency or underwriting desk in APAC. Generic SaaS copy was not converting clicks into pipeline - and the budget was not going to grow.

## Mandate

Fix content quality and targeting, build the lead generation system, grow qualified pipeline without proportionally scaling spend.

## Decisions

- **Fix the language, not the budget.** "+400% CTR without more spend is almost always a creative and targeting problem, not a budget problem." Ads were rebuilt to speak to insurance-specific pain points and matched one-to-one with landing pages - same language, same promise - to cut drop-off between click and conversion. The rejected alternative was a budget increase.
- **Intent-qualified lead magnets over generic form fills** - content offers insurance professionals would actually exchange an email for.

## Execution

Full website rewrite (v1.1, v1.2) across Customer Engagement, Insurance Agencies and Insurance Companies pages plus a Business Health Check tool; email nurture across a 14,550-contact database; attribution framework for content-to-pipeline decisions.

## Outcome

- Pipeline: **+50%** via lead magnets and the content programme (baseline NEEDS_HUMAN).
- Ad CTR: **+400%** with no spend increase.
- Email: **36.13%-40.46% open rates vs a 17.07% industry benchmark** (2.1-2.4x); CTR 1.64%-1.76% vs 1.50% benchmark.
