## T1. The 2024 story is efficiency, not volume
- LinkedIn: engagements 290,445 -> 974,046 (+235%) on FEWER posts (849 -> 783)
- YouTube: engagements 17,857 -> 55,379 (+210%) on +33% videos (197 -> 262)
- Facebook: record 162,098,943 impressions on -35% posts (600 -> 392)
The strongest new narrative in the data: the system got better, not bigger.

## T2. LinkedIn is the commercial engine, and it compounds
- Engagement per post (derived): 349 (2022) / 342 (2023) / 1,244 (2024) /
  1,994 (Q1 2025). Computation: engagements / posts from the Sprout YoY table
  (203,327/582 · 290,445/849 · 974,046/783 · 271,164/136).
- Net adds accelerate every year: +46,753 / +139,519 / +201,377.
- Share of Sprout-tracked net adds (derived, 5 platforms): 40% (2022) ->
  62% (2023) -> 63% (2024). 2023 corroborated by the Vancouver deck
  ("143,204 - almost half of our total growth").

## T3. Each platform has a distinct, visible job
- Facebook: reach engine - 63.6% of 2024's 254.8M impressions - on a static
  legacy audience (1,241,328 followers, +145 net in Jan 2025).
- LinkedIn: engagement + pipeline (T2).
- Instagram: activated 2024 - impressions 8,187,057 -> 74,758,842 (+813%),
  followers +3,006 -> +27,916.
- X: managed decline - posts cut 40% (1,007 -> 607), impressions halved with
  the platform (11.1M -> 5.5M), followers still +81,624 in 2024.
- YouTube: quality investment (T1).
This makes the site's "platform-specific strategy, each with a distinct
purpose and success metric" claim provable in one chart.

## T4. Growth is event-led
Monthly tracker spikes align with campaign moments: April 2024 +36,339
(Ubuntu 24.04 LTS launch month), December 2024 +56,350 (record month - 20
Years tail + year-end), then Q1 2025 acceleration (+28,355 / +36,233 /
~+40,000). Brand moments move the baseline.

## T5. Momentum outlived 2024
Fifteen consecutive growth months to the 2.81M peak (April 2025).
Q1 2025 LinkedIn: 271,164 engagements in three months - annualises past 1M,
and Q1 alone exceeded full-year 2022 (203,327).

## T6. Honest caveats (keep visible)
- Facebook 2023 impressions trough (53.7M between 120.8M and 162.1M years)
  is unexplained in the extracts - do not chart 2023 FB without a note.
- Facebook audience is legacy-static; X reach declined with the platform.
- Employee advocacy launched Jan 2025 and is nascent (2-3 shares/month,
  824 peak UTM clicks) - present as "what's next", never as a result.
- Instagram 2022 row (14.7M impressions on 17 posts, 1,801 engagements)
  reads anomalous - possibly paid or tracking artifact; avoid charting
  IG 2022, compare 2023 vs 2024 only.

## T7. State of Social (Prague 2023) supplies the strategy frame
Not numbers - framing: the 4Es content framework (Engage, Educate,
Entertain, Empower) and the thesis "Social media is a Busy Square - not a
Megaphone". Plus the 631K social-sessions KR target. Use as epigraphs for
the deep-dive section.
