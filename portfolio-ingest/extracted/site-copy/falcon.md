## Challenge
Breadth was the job. In any given week: consumer FMCG, FinTech app, luxury automotive, QSR chain. The skill wasn't deep vertical expertise — it was the ability to quickly understand a brand's commercial reality and produce work that served it.

## What I did
- L'Oréal — ExOil / ShineWithConfidence full influencer storyboard and scripts. First time influencer storyboarding built into agency process as a standard deliverable.
- Deliveroo Singapore — CatchTheRoo viral campaign (pitch v9, 1.5M projected reach, $31K SGD). Full social audit v1+v2, community management, UberEATS competitive analysis. 5,129 → 12,355 fans (+140.89%) in 3.5 months.
- Ringgit Plus — +5% page conversion on paid traffic across CITI, HSBC, PBBK, MAYB campaigns. Personal commendation from client exec team.
- Great Eastern Life — RM 350,536 budget, 5,000-lead KPI in 6 months, three-persona age-band targeting.
- Performance muscle. Predictive budget models for FinTech clients — +22% ROAS over baseline.
- Client knowledge programme. Five-part GDLP series: GDN, digital landscape, omni-channel, Facebook, CRO. Plus internal onboarding for new team members.
