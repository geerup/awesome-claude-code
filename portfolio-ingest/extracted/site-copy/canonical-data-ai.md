## Challenge
Canonical needed to grow awareness and generate qualified pipeline for data and AI infrastructure (Charmed Kafka, OpenSearch, MongoDB, MicroCloud) — in a market dominated by hyperscalers, without a massive event budget or household-brand recognition.

## What I did
- Conceived and produced Data & AI Masters — a virtual event series for practitioners and technical decision-makers.
- Organic social promo hit 153,000 impressions and 7.5% ER vs 5% benchmark — targeted to developer and infrastructure audiences.
- LinkedIn event page drove 426 registrations, 249 day-1 attendees, and 147 hours of in-platform watch time.
- Email campaign to 120,000 addresses averaged 20.8% open rate vs 12% monthly benchmark. Last-call email hit 23.5%.
- Produced 20+ reusable video sessions — repurposed for demand gen, social, and sales enablement through Q4.
