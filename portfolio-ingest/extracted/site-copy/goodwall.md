## Title
+20% MAU. -18% CAC. 150+ countries. All organic.

## Lede
A Geneva-HQ social impact platform for Gen Z with 1M members. The task: make growth sustainable, reduce acquisition cost, turn the community into a distribution asset.

## By the numbers
- **+20%** - Monthly Active Users in three months
- **-18%** - Monthly CAC reduction
- **1M → 1.3M** - Members · 150+ countries
- **10** - People — cross-functional team led

## Challenge
Impressive raw numbers but rising CAC and MAU not keeping pace with sign-ups. Team was cross-functional (10 people across web, content, email, paid, app) with no centralised strategy.

Youth platforms live or die on network effects — retention requires content and community mechanics that actually work.

## What I did
- Organic community activation. Rebuilt content architecture around triggers that brought users back — milestones, seasonal campaigns, social moments. Climate Action and GEW Launch were designed for members to post, share, invite peers.
- Acquisition shift. Reduced reliance on paid installs. Newsletter, press release programme and community-led campaigns brought organic install traffic. Spending to reach new users became activating existing users to recruit their networks.
- Infrastructure. Built top-level digital strategy (Keynote deck), Goodwall 2019 personas, marketing roadmap (Roadmunk). Shared understanding of who we targeted and how every piece of content fit the acquisition-activation-retention loop.
- Cross-functional coordination. Led 10 people. Built roadmap, calendar and brief templates so functions executed coherently.
