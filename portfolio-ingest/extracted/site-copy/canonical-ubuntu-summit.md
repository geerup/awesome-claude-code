## Challenge
Conference coverage is easy to do badly: a few recap photos, a keynote recording, done. The coverage disappears within 24 hours and leaves no lasting organic footprint.

The brief: turn a 3-day event into a weeks-long content engine and a media moment that generated sustained earned media coverage.

## What I did
- Pre-event: speaker announcement series, session previews, community countdown content. Promoted through organic social and the 109K LinkedIn Newsletter so technical community, press and partners knew weeks in advance.
- During: real-time social, session highlights, behind-the-scenes. Hero-to-snacks model — full sessions filmed for YouTube, clipped into short-form. The Framework RISC-V live session at Summit 2024 became the channel's second-highest-performing video of the year.
- Post-event: full recordings published, highlight compilations, community reactions, press coordination. The Summit tag tracked 84 posts generating 618K impressions and 9.4% cross-network ER — LinkedIn specifically hitting 15.53%.
- Each year the content strategy got more sophisticated. The earned-media numbers tracked that evolution directly.
