## Challenge
Insurance is a trust industry. Buyers need to believe the vendor understands their world — compliance pressure, operational complexity, pain points of running an agency or underwriting desk in APAC. Generic SaaS copy wasn't cutting it.

Ads generated impressions and clicks, but CTR-to-pipeline conversion was poor. The brief: fix content quality and targeting, build a lead-gen system, grow pipeline without scaling spend.

## What I did
- Website rewrite. Full copy rebuild across Customer Engagement, Insurance Agencies, Insurance Companies pages (v1.1, v1.2). The site had to do commercial work, not decorate.
- Lead-magnet architecture. Content offers insurance pros would exchange an email for. Replaced generic form fills with intent-qualified leads.
- Ad rebuild. +400% CTR came from speaking directly to insurance-specific pain points, matched one-to-one with landing pages. Same language, same promise — less drop-off between click and convert.
- Email nurture. 14,550-contact database. Open rates 36.13–40.46% vs 17.07% industry benchmark — 2.1–2.4× above. CTR 1.64–1.76% vs 1.50% benchmark.
- Pipeline tracking. Attribution framework so decisions were data-driven, not intuition-based.
