## Title
50% email open rate. +35% APAC acquisition. Built from zero.

## Lede
Kuala Lumpur-based earned-wage-access startup. No marketing function, no martech, no messaging architecture. Built the full acquisition engine and doubled B2B email benchmarks.

## By the numbers
- **50%** - Email open rate vs. 20–25% B2B SaaS benchmark
- **+35%** - APAC customer acquisition growth
- **From zero** - Marketing function, martech, brand, content
- **B2B2C** - Two-sided acquisition — employer + employee

## Challenge
No marketing function, no email acquisition system, no content or messaging architecture. EWA is a category-creation problem — educating an audience about a new financial model that competes with payday loans.

APAC isn't a single market — a campaign needs to function across significantly different financial cultures, regulations, and digital maturity.

## What I did
- Built the function from scratch. Brand identity, messaging framework, website copy, email infrastructure (Ortto), content templates, social presence.
- Email acquisition. Lead capture and nurture for both sides of the B2B2C model — employer-facing (HR/finance) and employee-facing. 50% open rate — more than double B2B SaaS benchmarks — from subject-line discipline, segmentation and content that actually answered questions.
- APAC localisation. Campaigns adapted for language, payment culture, regulatory framing — drove a +35% acquisition increase without proportionally scaling spend.
- Martech and automation. Selected and implemented the stack — CRM, Ortto, tracking — giving PAYD its first structured view of the funnel.
- Brand and identity. Directed brand identity alongside product positioning — critical where trust is the primary purchase driver.
