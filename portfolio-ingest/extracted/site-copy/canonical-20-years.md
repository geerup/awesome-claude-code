## Challenge
Ubuntu's 20th anniversary was a meaningful milestone. Milestones are easy to get wrong — they produce self-congratulatory content that talks about the company rather than its community.

The goal: something that would resonate with the open source community, get shared organically, and serve as Canonical's biggest brand moment of 2024.

## What I did
- Developed and directed the campaign across all social channels — the highest-investment content production of the year.
- Anchored on the community, not the company. Framed 20 years as a shared achievement of contributors.
- Produced a flagship video (Ubuntu 24.04 LTS Noble Numbat / 20 Years) designed for organic sharing — the most produced video asset of the year.
- Ran a coordinated publish across LinkedIn, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and X with platform-specific formats.
- Amplified UGC from the open source community and Canonical employees — hundreds of community pieces extended reach organically.
