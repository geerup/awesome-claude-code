## Challenge
Mindvalley was a $40M e-learning company — functionally, a best-kept secret in most Western and Asian media markets.

Loyal community and growing social, but no authoritative press coverage to validate the brand to enterprise buyers, new audiences, and talent.

## What I did
- Built the PR function end-to-end. Defined target media tiers by region — Malaysian national press, SE Asian business, global personal-development and wellness, technology trade.
- Identified story angles most likely to land: Vishen Lakhiani's iconoclast founder profile, unconventional company culture, the self-development/productivity intersection.
- Built journalist outreach with a 69% reply rate — cited by Growthhacker as a public benchmark. Every pitch researched, personalised, tied to a live story angle in the journalist's beat.
- Arranged press junkets and media visits to KL HQ. Managed Vishen's media appearances including BERNAMA Radio.
- Coordinated PR with SEO, influencer and social — a Forbes placement wasn't just earned media, it was a backlink compounding the SEO effect.
