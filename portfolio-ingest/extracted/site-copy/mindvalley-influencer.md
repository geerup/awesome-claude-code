## Challenge
Influencer marketing didn't exist as a department. Ad hoc creator relationships, no programme, no strategy, no measurement, no defined relationship model.

The brief: a programme that was cost-effective, measurable, and scalable — in a category (personal growth) perfectly aligned with the creator economy.

## What I did
- The zero-cost model. Negotiated relationship-based agreements giving creators access to Mindvalley's most valuable asset — its faculty. Creators could interview Vishen, access quests and masterclasses, present content as something they genuinely engaged with.
- Platform strategy. YouTube for long-form and organic conversions. Instagram for micro content and live-driven actions. Facebook for warm-audience partner posts. Strategic platforms — bloggers, Reddit, Pinterest — activated for niche and authority.
- OKR framework. Brand awareness, traffic, lead gen — three measurable objectives, every partnership evaluated against them.
- Measuring the unmeasurable. Tracked subscriber spikes on partner-video publish days. Example: 'Upgrade Your Consciousness' drove a 9.2% new-subscriber spike to the Mindvalley channel.
- Campaigns shipped: #iAmEnough Uncompromised Life, Superbrain by Jim Kwik, event coverage programme for A-Fest, Mindvalley University and Summit.
