870% organic growth in eleven months — 14K to 122K visits.

## Lede
A site with content but no SEO architecture. Rebuilt on-page, ran a 69%-reply outreach operation, compounded with PR. Organic went from noise to acquisition channel.

## By the numbers
- **870%** - Organic traffic growth in 11 months

- **Featured** - Public Growthhacker case study

## Challenge
14K monthly visits — negligible for a $40M e-learning company with a massive social presence.

The site had content but no SEO architecture, no systematic link building, no outreach strategy. Organic wasn't a meaningful channel.

## What I did
- Audited on-page across the site. Rebuilt the content hierarchy around high-intent keywords tied to Mindvalley's courses and authors.
- Built an off-page outreach operation targeting media, bloggers and podcasters in personal development and wellness — 69% reply rate, cited by Growthhacker as a notable benchmark.
- Coordinated with social and PR so high-authority placements reinforced organic ranking signals.
- Measured and reported monthly. Adjusted based on keyword movement and traffic data.
