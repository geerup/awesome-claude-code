## Title

## Challenge
Active accounts, no unified strategy. Facebook at 300K, YouTube at 300K, Instagram at 50K. Organic was flat. There was no system for turning followers into leads.

## What I did
- Built a platform-specific content strategy for each channel simultaneously — distinct formats, cadences, audience growth tactics per platform.
- Recruited and structured a social media team where there hadn't been one.
- Launched and ran a KOL/influencer programme from zero — signed the first partners, built the ROI tracking framework, scaled to a network reaching 10M people, profitable month one.
- Coordinated social with PR, SEO and events so every major moment had a cross-channel amplification plan.
