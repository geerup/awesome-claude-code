## Challenge
Canonical had no structured social media function. Content was inconsistent, untracked, and disconnected from marketing pipeline.

There was no content calendar, no influencer programme, no process for tying social to MQLs or revenue.

## What I did
- Defined strategy across LinkedIn, X, YouTube, Facebook and Instagram — each with a distinct purpose, format mix and success metric.
- Built a full content production system generating 4,000+ assets including video, thought leadership, partner activations and live event coverage.
- Integrated the entire function with Salesforce so social MQL contribution was measurable and reported to leadership.
- Built a 48-creator influencer network in the hardware, homelab and open source communities — the exact micro-communities where Canonical's developer and sysadmin audience lives.
- Directed social and video strategy for 20+ global product launches with Dell, Intel, NVIDIA and Framework. MC'd Ubuntu Summit 2024 and ran social coverage 2022–2024.
