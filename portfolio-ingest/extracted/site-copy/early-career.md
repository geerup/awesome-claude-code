## Challenge
Before Falcon, Mindvalley, all of it. Four years across Cairo, Dubai and KL. This is where the pattern started.

## What I did
- MizaTech / Socialeyez — Cairo/Dubai, 2010–13. Managed social for a MENA brand portfolio in the early-Facebook era when organic reach was real. Grew Qatar Happening to the 12th-largest Facebook page in Qatar — organic only. Ran integrated paid campaigns up to $2,000/day. 20% Mailchimp CTR.
- Joud — Cairo, 2013–14. Lifestyle and home-goods brand, 200+ SKUs, 6+ retail points. Built the FB community strategy from scratch: weekly plans, JoudNESS competition mechanics, newsletter, engagement rules. Hard KPIs — 10+ daily inquiries, 3+ daily orders. First full community playbook — structurally similar to editorial frameworks built at Canonical and Mindvalley a decade later.
- Insight Magazine — Dubai/KL, 2012–14. Lead journalist and editor. Cover-to-cover editorial ownership. Writing, commissioning, editing, producing. This is where the writing discipline came from — magazine deadlines are different from content-marketing deadlines; the product is permanent and the standard is higher.
