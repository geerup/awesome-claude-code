02

Ubuntu Summit earned media - 15× in two years

Turned a 3-day conference into Canonical's largest annual earned-media event: AVE from $519K to $7.9M and reach from 56M to 857M in two years.

OrgCanonical · Ubuntu Summit
RoleSocial and content strategy lead, all three years; MC in 2024
Window2022 - 2024

Context

Ubuntu Summit is Canonical's annual flagship conference - the largest gathering of Ubuntu contributors and community. In 2022 it was a significant event but a modest media moment.

Tension

Conference coverage is easy to do badly: recap photos and a keynote recording that disappear within 24 hours, leaving no lasting organic footprint. The brief was to turn a 3-day event into a weeks-long content engine with sustained earned media.

Decisions

Hero-to-snacks model: film full sessions for YouTube, clip into short-form for social.
RejectedDocumentation-style coverage (recap photos plus a keynote recording) - explicitly the failure mode to avoid.
WhyEvent coverage delivers compounding returns when treated as content production, not documentation.
Three-phase editorial plan: pre-event speaker series and countdowns via the 109K LinkedIn Newsletter; real-time coverage during; recordings, compilations, and press coordination after.
WhyGenerate a media moment, not just social posts - press, community, and partners primed weeks in advance.

Execution

  • Pre-event: speaker announcements, session previews, community countdowns via organic social and the newsletter.
  • During: real-time posts, session highlights, behind-the-scenes; full sessions filmed and clipped.
  • Post-event: recordings, highlight compilations, community reaction content, press coordination.
  • The Framework Laptop RISC-V live session became Canonical's second-highest-performing YouTube video of 2024.

First influencer department - zero budget, ROI-positive month one

Built Mindvalley's first influencer department on relationship-based agreements instead of paid placements: 10M+ network reach at peak. The same relationship model was reused at Canonical five years later.

OrgMindvalley
RoleBuilt and led the KOL / influencer function
Window2017 - 2020

Context

Influencer marketing didn't exist as a department - ad hoc creator relationships, no programme, no measurement, no relationship model. The personal-growth category was perfectly aligned with the creator economy.

Tension

Build a programme that was cost-effective, measurable, and scalable - without a placement budget.

Decisions

Zero-cost model: relationship-based agreements giving creators access to Mindvalley's faculty (founder interviews, quest and masterclass access) instead of paying for posts.
RejectedPaid placements - explicitly rejected.
WhyAuthentic creator engagement converts better; creators present content they genuinely engaged with.
Concentrate on YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook where conversion was proven; activate bloggers, Reddit, Pinterest only strategically.
RejectedSpreading across all platforms equally.
WhyYouTube had the highest organic conversions and time-on-message; IG for micro content and lives; FB for promotable warm-audience partner posts.
OKR framework: 5% monthly share-of-mention increase, 5% site-visit increase from creators, 1,000 masterclass sign-ups per campaign.
RejectedVanity reach metrics as the programme KPI.
WhyEvery partnership evaluated against measurable objectives.

Execution

  • Signed first partners, built ROI tracking, scaled the network to 10M+ reach.
  • Campaigns: #iAmEnough (Uncompromised Life), Superbrain by Jim Kwik, event coverage (A-Fest, Mindvalley University, Summit).
  • Attribution technique: tracked YouTube subscriber spikes on partner-video publish days - one video drove a 9.2% new-subscriber spike in a day.
