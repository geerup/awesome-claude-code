Canonical · Data & AI Masters Case 04 of 05 2024 Conceived and produced the event; built the full marketing programme

A virtual event that earned its seat on channels we already owned.

In 2024 Canonical needed awareness and qualified pipeline for its data and AI infrastructure line - Charmed Kafka, OpenSearch, MongoDB, MicroCloud - in a market dominated by hyperscalers, without a large event budget or a household brand name. I conceived Data & AI Masters and ran its promotion entirely through owned channels. Every number on this page names its analytics export.

01 · The problem

The products deserved a seat. The marketing had to earn one.

Canonical's data and AI infrastructure line was competing in a market dominated by hyperscalers, without a large event budget and without a household brand name in the data space.

Registrations had to come from channels Canonical already owned - no paid reach to lean on.

02 · The call

Each channel got exactly one job.

Social for awareness and trust; LinkedIn for community-native conversion; email for urgency and close.
WhyThe operating belief, stated plainly in the source: virtual events succeed or fail at the promotion layer.
OpenNEEDS_HUMAN: whether paid promotion was considered and rejected - the site claims "zero paid spend"; the case source does not state the fork
03 · The run

Owned channels, worked hard.

  • Organic social. 153,000 impressions at a 7.5% engagement rate against a 5% benchmark, targeted at developer and infrastructure audiences.
  • LinkedIn event page. 426 registrations, 249 day-1 attendees, 147 hours of in-platform watch time.
  • Email. 120,000 deliveries at a 20.8% average open rate against Canonical's 12% monthly benchmark; the last-call email hit 23.5%.
  • Reusable content. Produced 20+ video sessions, repurposed for demand gen, social and sales enablement through Q4 2024.

Honest caveat

The number a demand-gen hiring manager asks first is the one this case is missing: what did those 4,731 registrations become in pipeline? Registrations are not MQLs. Until that is confirmed, this is honestly top-of-funnel demand capture with exceptional channel efficiency - not attributed revenue.
