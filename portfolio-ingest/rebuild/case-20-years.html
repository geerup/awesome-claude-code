Canonical · 20 Years of Ubuntu Case 03 of 05 2024 Developed and directed the campaign across all social channels

Ubuntu turns 20 - anchored on the community, not the company.

The 20th anniversary was the highest-investment content production of Canonical's year, planned as its flagship brand campaign. Milestones are easy to get wrong - open source audiences punish self-congratulation. One framing decision produced a 14.4% LinkedIn engagement rate against a roughly 5% platform benchmark. 01 · The problem

Milestones talk about the company. Communities leave.

Anniversary campaigns tend to produce self-congratulatory content that centres the company rather than its contributors - and open source audiences punish exactly that.

The campaign had to resonate with the community, get shared organically, and still carry Canonical's brand. Those three can pull against each other.

02 · The call

One decision, made in the brief.

Anchor 20 years on the community as a shared achievement of contributors - not on Canonical.
RejectedThe default company-centric milestone reel.
Why"When the audience sees themselves in the story, they share it." This one decision is what the 14.4% engagement rate traces back to.
03 · The run

Built for organic sharing.

  • Flagship film. Produced the Ubuntu 24.04 LTS "Noble Numbat / 20 Years" video, designed for organic sharing - the most-produced video asset of the year.
  • Coordinated publish. Ran a synchronized release across LinkedIn, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and X with platform-specific formats.
  • Community amplification. Amplified UGC from the open source community and Canonical employees - hundreds of community pieces extended reach beyond Canonical's own audience.
The lesson

The highest-performing content is the most resonant, not the most polished.
