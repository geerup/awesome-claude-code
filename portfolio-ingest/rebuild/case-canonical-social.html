Canonical · Social & content Case 01 of 05 2022 - 2025 Built the function end to end - strategy, production, measurement, creator network

A social function built from zero, wired to Salesforce.

Social's real problem at Canonical was never the content. It was that nobody could prove it earned a thing. When I joined in 2022, the publisher of Ubuntu had no structured social function: content went out inconsistent, untracked, and disconnected from pipeline. I built the whole thing and wired it to prove its own commercial worth - social MQLs went from 36 to 785 in a single year. Every number on this page names its analytics export.

01 · The problem

The problem was proof, not posts.

There was no content calendar, no influencer programme, and no process for tying social to MQLs or revenue. Content went out inconsistently and disappeared.

For an enterprise open source company selling to developers and sysadmins, social was assumed to be a brand expense - and no tracking architecture existed to prove or disprove it. Leadership had no way to see whether any of it mattered commercially.

02 · The calls

Three calls built the function, and the first one made the other two provable.

I integrated the function with Salesforce from day one, so every social MQL was attributable and reported to leadership.
RejectedRunning social as an untracked brand cost centre, the status quo that left its value impossible to prove either way.
WhyThis is the decision the rest of the case rests on: without it, the 785 number would not exist as evidence.
I built a 48-creator network on access and relationships, not a rate card, in the hardware, homelab and open source micro-communities where Canonical's developer and sysadmin audience actually lives - ExplainingComputers, Jeff Geerling, Lawrence Systems, Level1Linux and 44 more.
RejectedPaid amplification bought on a rate card.
WhyCanonical's developer and sysadmin audience lives in these micro-communities; the network met them there on relationships rather than a bought placement.
I gave LinkedIn, X, YouTube, Facebook and Instagram each a distinct purpose, format mix and success metric.
RejectedOne cross-posted feed pushed identically to every platform.
03 · The run

A production system, not a posting schedule.

The team that grew around the function ran the day to day; the calls were mine.

  • Content engine. Built a production system generating 4,000+ assets across video, thought leadership, partner activations and live event coverage.
  • Launches. Directed social and video for 20+ global product launches with Dell, Intel, NVIDIA and Framework.
  • Owned audience. Launched a LinkedIn Newsletter that grew organically to 109K subscribers by September 2024.
  • Format bet. Introduced the carousel format mid-2024 (33 new carousels vs 5 previously); it became the top-performing content type.
  • Into the commercial org. Presented the annual strategy to the commercial team at the Prague 2023 sprint; MC'd Ubuntu Summit 2024.
On the record

Honest caveat

LinkedIn engagement grew from 290,445 to 974,046 across 2023 to 2024, and it coincided with X impressions falling from 11.1M to 5.5M over the same window as posting shifted. The platform mix was a deliberate bet on LinkedIn, not a rising tide everywhere.
