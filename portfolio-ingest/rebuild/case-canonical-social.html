Canonical · Social & content Case 01 of 05 2022 - 2025 Built the function end to end - strategy, production, measurement, creator network

A social function built from zero, wired to Salesforce.

When I joined Canonical in 2022, the publisher of Ubuntu had no structured social function: content was inconsistent, untracked, and disconnected from pipeline. I built the whole thing - and made it prove its commercial worth. Social MQLs went from 36 to 785 in a single year. Every number on this page names its analytics export.

01 · The problem

Social was a cost centre no one could measure.

There was no content calendar, no influencer programme, and no process for tying social to MQLs or revenue. Content went out inconsistently and disappeared.

For an enterprise open source company selling to developers and sysadmins, social was assumed to be a brand expense - and there was no tracking architecture that could prove or disprove it. Leadership had no way to see whether any of it mattered commercially.

02 · The calls

Three decisions did the work.

Salesforce integration from day one - every social MQL attributable and reported to leadership.
RejectedRunning social as an untracked brand cost centre - the status quo that left its value unprovable.
WhyThe decision the rest of the case rests on: without it, the 785 number would not exist as evidence.
A 48-creator relationship network in the hardware, homelab and open source micro-communities where the audience actually lives - ExplainingComputers, Jeff Geerling, Lawrence Systems, Level1Linux and 44 more.
RejectedPaid amplification on a rate card - built on access and relationships instead.
WhyDeveloper trust is earned in-community; paid placement reads as noise to this audience.
Platform-specific jobs - LinkedIn, X, YouTube, Facebook and Instagram each with a distinct purpose, format mix and success metric.
RejectedOne cross-posted feed pushed identically to every platform.
03 · The run

A production system, not a posting schedule.

  • Content engine. Built a production system generating 4,000+ assets across video, thought leadership, partner activations and live event coverage.
  • Launches. Directed social and video for 20+ global product launches with Dell, Intel, NVIDIA and Framework.
  • Owned audience. Launched a LinkedIn Newsletter that grew organically to 109K subscribers by September 2024.
  • Format bet. Introduced the carousel format mid-2024 (33 new carousels vs 5 previously); it became the top-performing content type.
  • Into the commercial org. Presented the annual strategy to the commercial team at the Prague 2023 sprint; MC'd Ubuntu Summit 2024.
On the record

Honest caveat

The LinkedIn engagement growth (290,445 to 974,046) coincided with declining X impressions (11.1M in 2023 to 5.5M in 2024) as posting shifted. The platform mix was a deliberate bet on LinkedIn, not a rising tide everywhere.
