01 · The problem

Security awareness as an obligation, not a message.

Cybersecurity Awareness Month is easy to treat as an internal box-tick. The objective here was the opposite: make it a moment that positioned Canonical as a technical authority and a trustable voice - across topics like SysOps, device security, and the EU Cyber Resilience Act.

That meant translating a security product roadmap into narratives a technical audience would actually watch.

02 · The calls

Series, not one-offs.

A 7-part educational video series mapped to the security product roadmap - pitched and planned as a property, not scattered posts.
WhyA series compounds: shared editorial tone, recurring format, cutdowns that feed other campaigns.
Editorial tone tuned to balance clarity with authority - technical enough to be credible, clear enough to be watched.
RejectedMarketing-led promotional framing that a developer audience discounts.
03 · The run

Messaging and production, both led in-house.

  • Scripting. Wrote 7 videos and briefed 3 internal product/security spokespeople.
  • Narrative. Crafted the stories for technical topics - SysOps, device security, the Cyber Resilience Act.
  • Studio. Led planning and coordination for studio-based filming; worked with the media crew on lighting, framing and Ubuntu-styled treatment.
  • Rollout. Built the channel strategy - a YouTube series, LinkedIn reels, and X threads.
  • Reuse. Repurposed episodes as cutdowns across other campaigns (Secure Stack, Ubuntu Pro, Landscape).
